---
hook: 🎭 «Он играет»: сейчас на сцене или вообще музыкант? В английском это два разных времени.
goals: различать «сейчас» и «обычно» · выбирать am / is / are или do / does · не путать What are you doing? и What do you do?
---
::: scene 🎸 Знакомьтесь, Макс
Макс — пилот. Но сегодня выходной, и сейчас он играет на гитаре у костра.
> Friend: What ==does== Max ==do==?
> You: He'==s== a pilot. He ==flies== planes.
> Friend: And what ==is== he ==doing== now?
> You: He'==s playing== the guitar.
:::

## Два настоящих времени
::: compare
# 🎥 Сейчас: am / is / are + -ing
- Max ==is playing== the guitar.
- ==Is== he ==flying== now? — No, he ==isn't==.
- Listen! Somebody ==is singing==.
# 🔁 Обычно: do / does
- Max ==flies== planes.
- ==Does== he ==fly== planes? — Yes, he ==does==.
- I ==sing== in a choir every Friday.
:::

::: timeline
span now-8 now+8 | he's playing (сейчас)
repeat past future | he flies planes (обычно)
:::

## Помощники: не перепутать
:::: row
::: examples 🎥 Сейчас: am / is / are
- ==Are== you work==ing==?
- ==Is== she work==ing==?
- I'==m not== work==ing==. She ==isn't== work==ing==.
:::
::: examples 🔁 Обычно: do / does
- ==Do== you work?
- ==Does== she work?
- I ==don't== work. She ==doesn't== work.
:::
::::

::: trap
✗ ~~Do you watching~~ TV?
✓ Are you watching TV?
✗ ~~Are you like~~ coffee?
✓ Do you like coffee?
✗ Excuse me, ~~are you speaking~~ English? (вообще)
✓ Excuse me, do you speak English?
:::

## Слова-подсказки
::: cards
# 🎥 Сейчас
now · at the moment · today · Look! · Listen!
# 🔁 Обычно
every day · always · usually · often · sometimes · never
:::

::: note 🧠 Глаголы-«состояния»
_want, like, love, know, understand, need, prefer_ почти никогда не ставят в -ing, даже про «сейчас»: _I don't understand_ (а не ~~I'm not understanding~~), _She wants to go home now._
:::

::: quiz
? Look! It ___ (snow).
= 's snowing
? It ___ (snow) a lot here in winter.
= snows
? ___ (you / understand) me?
= Do you understand
? What ___ (your father / do)? — He's a doctor.
= does your father do
:::

::: summary
- **Сейчас** → am / is / are + -ing. **Обычно** → present simple.
- Are you working? / Do you work?
- What are you doing? = что делаешь сейчас. What do you do? = кем работаешь.
- want, know, like, understand — без -ing.
:::
