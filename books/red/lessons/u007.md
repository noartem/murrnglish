> Flatmate: I ==don't smoke==, I ==don't play== loud music, and I ==don't have== a cat.

> Flatmate: She ==doesn't live== here. She ==doesn't like== the city.

## Помощник don't / doesn't
В русском достаточно частицы «не». В английском перед глаголом появляется помощник: **don't** (= do not) или **doesn't** (= does not).

:::: row
::: form
# ➖ Отрицание
I / you / we / they | ==don't== | work
he / she / it | ==doesn't== | work
:::
::: rule 🎒 Куда делась -s?
Буква **-s** «переехала» в помощника: does**n't**. Поэтому сам глагол остаётся **без -s**.

She ==doesn't work==. (а не ~~doesn't works~~)
:::
::::

::: trap
✗ He ~~not like~~ cheese.
✓ He doesn't like cheese.
✗ She ~~doesn't works~~ on Sundays.
✓ She doesn't work on Sundays.
✗ They ~~doesn't~~ know.
✓ They don't know.
:::

::: compare
# ✅ Да
- Anna plays the guitar.
- We watch football.
- He speaks French.
# 🚫 Нет
- Anna ==doesn't play== the guitar.
- We ==don't watch== football.
- He ==doesn't speak== French.
:::

## Нравится или нет
С глаголом _like_ удобно рассказывать о вкусах — своих и чужих.

::: cards
# 😋 like
I ==like== sushi.
# 😐 don't like
Kate ==doesn't like== horror films.
# 😍 love
My kids ==love== the zoo.
:::

## Нечасто и никогда
:::: row
::: examples 🙂 not very often
- I ==don't go== to the theatre very often.
- He ==doesn't cook== very often.
:::
::: note ⛔ never
_never_ — уже отрицание, поэтому _don't_ не нужен: _I never eat meat_, а не ~~I don't never eat~~.
:::
::::

::: quiz
? I ___ (not / know) his phone number.
= don't know
? Sue ___ (not / drink) coffee.
= doesn't drink
? Paul has a car, but he ___ (not / use) it much.
= doesn't use
? My parents ___ (not / speak) English.
= don't speak
:::

::: summary
- Отрицание: **don't / doesn't + глагол**.
- После **doesn't** глагол без -s: she doesn't work.
- not very often = нечасто; never — без don't.
:::
