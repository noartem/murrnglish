::: scene 🎤 Интервью с поваром
> Reporter: ==Do you cook== at home too?
> Chef: Yes, I ==do==. Every Sunday.
> Reporter: ==What does your son like==?
> Chef: Pancakes! He ==doesn't eat== anything else.
> Reporter: ==How often do you go== on holiday?
> Chef: Once a year. Restaurants never sleep!
:::

## Помощник впереди
В вопросе в present simple снова появляется помощник — **do** или **does**. Он встаёт **перед** подлежащим, а глагол остаётся в начальной форме.

::: form
# ❓ do / does → кто → глагол
==Do== | I / you / we / they | work?
==Does== | he / she / it | work?
:::

::: compare
# ✅ Утверждение
- You live near here.
- Lucy plays tennis.
# ❓ Вопрос
- ==Do== you ==live== near here?
- ==Does== Lucy ==play== tennis?
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~You like coffee?~~
✓ Do you like coffee?
✗ Does she ~~plays~~ tennis?
✓ Does she play tennis?
✗ Where ~~your parents live~~?
✓ Where do your parents live?
:::

## С вопросительным словом
::: form
# 🔍 слово → do / does → кто → глагол
==Where== | do | you | live?
==What== | does | this word | mean?
==How often== | do | you | watch TV?
==What time== | does | the film | start?
==What== | do | you | do? (кем работаешь?)
:::

::: note 💼 What do you do?
Этот вопрос — не «что ты делаешь» (это _What are you doing?_), а «чем ты занимаешься, кем работаешь». Ответ: _I'm a nurse. I work in a bank._
:::

## Короткие ответы
:::: row
::: examples ✅
- Do you like jazz? — Yes, I ==do==.
- Does it rain a lot here? — Yes, it ==does==.
:::
::: examples ❌
- Do they speak Russian? — No, they ==don't==.
- Does he drive? — No, he ==doesn't==.
:::
::::

::: quiz
? ___ your sister work in a hospital?
= Does
? Where ___ you usually go on holiday?
= do
? What time ___ the shop close?
= does
? Do you play the piano? — No, I ___.
= don't
:::

::: summary
- Вопрос: **Do / Does + кто + глагол**: Do you live here?
- После **does** глагол без -s: Does she play?
- Со словом: **Where / What / How often + do / does…**
- Ответы: Yes, I do. No, he doesn't.
:::
