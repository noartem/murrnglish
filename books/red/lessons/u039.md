::: scene 🕯️ Пустая комната
> Vera: The room looks strange now.
> Sam: On Friday there **was** a sofa and three pictures. **There were** books on the shelves.
> Vera: What will it be?
> Sam: **There will be** something else. Probably a desk. — **There won't be** a clock; we threw it out.

## there was / there were
Схема из юнита 38, но в прошедшем времени.

::: form
# ⏪ было
**There was** a carpet on the floor. | **There were** three pictures on the wall.
**There was** a small table in the middle. | **There were** some books on the shelves.
# 🔹 вопрос и отрицание
'Was everything OK yesterday?' '**Were** there any problems?'
'I was hungry, but **there wasn't** anything to eat.'
:::
Правило то же: одно → **was**, много или неисчисляемое во множественном числе → **were**. Признак такой же, как у людей: _a sofa, a table, a clock_ — **was**; _pictures, books, flowers_ — **were**.

## there has/have been
Когда важно, что ситуация держится до сих пор: изменения уже произошли и результат на месте.

::: form
# 📌 есть и последствия
'Why are the police outside the bank?' '**There's been** a robbery.'
'Since January?' '**There have been** three accidents.'
'Last week I went back to my birthplace.' '**There have been** a lot of changes.'
:::
Схема та же, что у **have/has**: **There has been** (одно) и **There have been** (много).

## there will be
Будущее время той же схемы.

::: form
# 📅 будет
**There will be** 200 people at the conference.
**There will be** somebody at the station to meet you.
'Will there be music at your party?' 'Yes, there will.'
# ❌ не будет
**There won't be** a concert on Saturday. | I don't think **there will be** any problems.
:::
Отрицание будущего — **there won't be**, а вопрос **Will there be…?** В ответе обязательно **there is**, **there are** или **there will be** — но никогда не ~~they~~.

::: trap
✗ ~~There was nothing in it.~~
✓ **There was** nothing in it. — так верно, но в отрицании часто пишут: 'it was empty. **There was** nothing in it.'
✗ ~~Twenty years ago there was many tourists here.~~
✓ Twenty years ago **there were** many tourists here.
✗ ~~Will there be 200 people at the conference? — Yes, there will.~~
✓ **Will there be** 200 people at the conference? — **Yes, there will be**.
✗ ~~When you arrive tomorrow, there will somebody at the station.~~
✓ … **there will be somebody** at the station.
Ключ к прошедшему — число: _a lot of tourists_ требует **were**, не **was**. А в будущем **there will be** не сокращается до _there'll be_ в утверждении с существительным во множественном числе — пишут полностью.
:::

## Одно время или другое
Форма **there** полностью повторяет форму обычного глагола в том же времени.

::: form
# 🔹 одно
**There is** a cafe. | **There was** a storm. | **There will be** a concert.
**There has been** a robbery. | **There is** 24 hours in a day — точнее: **There are**
# 👥 много
**There are** many people. | **There were** many tourists.
**There have been** three accidents. | **There will be** 200 people.
:::
Запомните как таблицу: **is / was / has been / will be** — для одного; **are / were / have been / will be** — для многих.

::: summary
- **there was / were** — было; **there has/have been** — есть, с последствиями; **there will be** — будет.
- Форма по числу: **is/was/has been** — одно, **are/were/have been** — много.
- Вопрос: **Was there…? / Were there…? / Will there be…?**
- Отрицание: **there wasn't / weren't / won't be**.
:::
