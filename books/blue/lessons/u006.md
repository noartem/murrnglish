::: scene 🔌 The power cut
Last night at 9 p.m. all the lights in Oak Street went out. What was everyone doing?
> Mr Ruiz: I ==was cooking== pasta.
> Emma: The twins ==were doing== their homework.
> Joe: I ==was watching== the final — and I missed the goal!
:::

## In the middle of something
The **past continuous** — **was / were + -ing** — puts you inside an activity at a moment in the past. It had started before that moment and hadn't finished yet.

:::: row
::: timeline
span past-12 past+14 | Joe was watching TV
point past | 9 p.m.
:::
::: form
# ➕ / ➖
I / he / she / it | ==was== / ==wasn't== | work==ing==
we / you / they | ==were== / ==weren't== | work==ing==
# ❓
==was== | I / he / she / it | work==ing==?
==were== | we / you / they | work==ing==?
:::
::::

::: examples
- Back in 2020 I ==was renting== a tiny room in Seoul.
- What ==were you doing== at midnight? — I ==was sleeping==, of course.
- It ==wasn't raining==, so we walked.
:::

## Was doing + did: the interruption
Put the two pasts together and you get a longer background action with a short event happening in the middle of it.

::: rule ⚡
The longer action → **past continuous**. The short event → **past simple**.

I ==was cycling== home when a fox ==ran== across the road.
:::

::: timeline
span past-14 past+16 | I was cycling home
point past | a fox ran out
:::

::: compare Two stories, two meanings
# 🎬 When we arrived, they were having dinner.
- They started before we came.
- We walked in in the middle of the meal.
# 🍽️ When we arrived, they had dinner.
- First we arrived, then they ate.
- One action after the other.
:::

::: cards
# ⏳ while + continuous
My phone rang ==while== I ==was driving==.
# 🔔 when + simple
==When== the bell rang, the students ==were chatting==.
# ➡️ one after another
She ==opened== the letter, ==read== it and ==smiled==.
:::

## Not for states
Like the present continuous, it doesn't go with state verbs such as _want, know, need, like, believe_.

::: trap
✗ I ~~was wanting~~ to be a pilot when I was little.
✓ I wanted to be a pilot when I was little.
✗ We ~~weren't knowing~~ what to do.
✓ We didn't know what to do.
:::

::: quiz
? I ___ (read) when the lights went out.
= was reading
? What ___ (you / do) at this time yesterday?
= were you doing
? She ___ (fall) while she was skiing.
= fell
? ___ (you / go) out last night? — No, I stayed in.
= Did you go
:::

::: summary
- **was / were + -ing** = in progress at a moment in the past.
- Long background → past continuous; short event → past simple.
- _while_ often introduces the longer action.
- State verbs keep the past simple: I wanted, I knew.
:::
