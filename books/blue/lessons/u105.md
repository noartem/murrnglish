## -er or more?

**Short words take -er.** **Long words take more.** There is no in-between and no reason to worry: pick by length.

::: compare
# ➕ one syllable — add -er
old · rich · big · cheap · tall · short · hard · long · fast · warm

# ➕ two syllables or more — add more
expensive · important · peaceful · serious · reliable · boring · interesting
:::

::: form
# a quick look
rich → rich==er==
cheap → cheap==er==
dangerous → more danger==ous==
important → more import==ant==
:::

## The spelling of -er

Three endings need one more letter before the **-er**.

::: form
# ➕ e doubles the consonant
big → big==ger==
hot → h==otter==
thin → th==inner==

# ➕ y becomes i
easy → eas==ier==
happy → happ==ier==
pretty → prett==ier==
:::

## The four that break the rule

::: compare
# ✅ good / bad
- good → ==better==
- bad → ==worse==

# ✅ far
- far → ==further== / ==farther==

# ✅ many
- many → ==more==
:::

**Further** and **farther** are both correct for distance; for progress people usually say _further_: _We must go further._

## than

::: compare
# with than
- cheaper ==than== the shop round the corner
- more interesting ==than== my old job

# without than
- Please speak more quietly.
- Can you move a bit further away?
- She works harder than her brother does.
:::

**than** is left out after a preposition — _cheaper **than that** one_ → _cheaper **that** one_ — and in short answers like _than that_ or _than usual_.

::: trap
✗ The new one is more cheap than the old one.
✓ The new one is ==cheaper== than the old one.
✗ This is more bigger.
✓ This is ==bigger==.
✗ He arrives more late than me.
✓ He arrives ==later== than me.
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? This tea is very weak. I'd like some ___ .
= stronger
? The job was quite hard. It was ___ than I expected.
= more difficult
? That jacket is ___ than this one.
= warmer
? You look ___ . Have you lost weight?
= thinner
? Prices here are ___ than in the capital.
= higher
? I ran ten kilometres. Dan only managed eight, so I ran ___ .
= further
? Please speak ___ ; the baby is asleep.
= quietly
? I thought it would be ___ , but it was easy.
= harder
? We need a ___ room for the whole family.
= bigger
? Health matters ___ money.
= more
:::

::: summary
- Short words take **-er**; long words take **more**.
- Spelling: double the consonant (bigger), change **y** to **i** (easier).
- Irregulars: good → **better**, bad → **worse**, far → **further**, many → **more**.
- **than** compares, but drops after a preposition: _cheaper **than that** one_.
:::
