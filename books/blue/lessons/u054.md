::: scene 💍 Two friends and a decision
Amir and Lena have been talking about it for months. Today they finally said it out loud.
> Lena: Shall we get married one day?
> Amir: ==Yes, let's==. Let's ==arrange to tell my parents== first.
> Lena: You always ==manage to calm== them down.
> Amir: I ==offered to carry== all the boxes last time and you refused.
> Lena: I ==refused to help== with the washing-up, that's all.
> Amir: I ==promised not to say== anything about your brother. I haven't.
> Lena: I ==pretended not to see== him last Christmas.
:::

## Deciding, promising, refusing
A big family of verbs takes **to + verb**. Most of them are about a decision, an offer or a promise — so the action is still ahead of you.

::: words
agree · arrange · decide · fail · forget · hope · learn · manage · offer · plan · promise · refuse · threaten · afford · deserve · tend · try
:::

::: examples
- They ==arranged to meet== at eight.
- She ==agreed to help== him move.
- He ==offered to drive== us home.
- They ==decided to get married== in June.
- We ==managed to catch== the last train.
- He ==failed to finish== the report on time.
- She ==refused to sign== the form.
- I ==promised not to tell== anybody.
- He ==tends to talk== too much.
:::

The negative is **not to**: _They decided **not to have** children for the moment._

::: note ➕
**dare** is the odd one out: **dare to** and **dare** (no *to*) are both correct in the
positive — _Nobody **dared to speak**._ / _Nobody **dared speak**._ But after **daren't** the
*to* disappears: _I **daren't tell** him._ (never _I daren't to tell him_).
:::

## seem, appear, pretend, claim — saying more than you know
These four also take **to + verb**, and they let you pass on an impression rather than a fact.

::: form
# 🎭 four ways of hedging
seem | _It **seems to rain**._ = it looks like it will
appear | _The car **appears to have broken down**._ = I can see that it has
pretend | _She **pretended to be asleep**._ = she acted as if she were
claim | _They **claim to have solved** it._ = they say so, unproven
:::

Each of them can reach further back, with **have done**, or further into a moment, with **be doing**:

::: compare
# ⏮️ perfect: before now
- It ==seems to have stopped== raining.
- She ==claimed not to have seen== me.
# 🎞️ continuous: at that moment
- He ==pretended to be reading== a newspaper.
- The house ==appears to be empty==.
:::

## what / how / where / whether + to
You can put a question word in front of **to + verb** after verbs of asking and deciding — **ask, wonder, remember, forget, learn, understand** — and that gives you the small, tidy way to ask about an action.

::: rule 🧩 the frame
question word + **to** + verb

_I don't know **what to do**._
_We agreed **where to meet**._

_the question word_ · _the action that isn't settled yet_
:::

::: examples
- I don't know ==whether to apply== or not.
- Could you tell me ==how to use== this machine?
- Ask Ravi. He'll advise you ==where to park==.
- We haven't decided ==when to leave==.
- She explained ==how to start== the engine.
:::

::: note 🗣️
**show / tell / ask / advise / teach + person + what/how/where to do** is the same frame with a
person dropped in the middle: _Could you **show me how to start** this thing?_ / _Ask Jo — she'll
tell you **what to pack**._
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~We decided to going abroad.~~
✓ We decided ==to go== abroad.
✗ ~~She refused to telling him.~~
✓ She refused ==to tell== him.
✗ ~~I promise to not tell anyone.~~
✓ I promise ==not to tell== anyone.
✗ ~~He pretended not seeing me.~~
✓ He pretended ==not to see== me.
✗ ~~I don't know what I should do with it.~~
✓ I don't know ==what to do== with it.
These verbs take **to**, not **-ing** — the -ing family is [[u53|unit 53]].
:::

::: cards
# 🎬 to + verb
She agreed ==to help== him.
# 🙈 not + to + verb
They agreed ==not to argue==.
# 🎭 seem + to + verb
It ==seems to have cooled down==.
# 🧭 what + to + verb
I don't know ==what to cook==.
:::

::: quiz
? They arranged ___ at eight o'clock.
= to meet
? She refused ___ his name.
= to give / to tell
? He denied ___ the window.
= breaking
? I can't decide whether ___ or not.
= to apply
? The wall seems ___ by someone.
= to have been painted
? She pretended ___ asleep.
= to be
? Could you tell me ___ the machine?
= how to start
? Nobody dared ___ anything.
= to say / say
:::

::: summary
- Decision and promise verbs take **to do**: agree, arrange, decide, offer, promise, refuse, manage, afford, fail, plan, tend — negative **not to do**.
- **dare to do** or **dare do**; after **daren't**, drop the *to*.
- **seem, appear, pretend, claim + to do**, with **have done** or **be doing**.
- After **ask, wonder, remember, learn**: a question word plus **to do**.
- These verbs take **to**, not **-ing**.
:::
