::: scene 🎂 Two phone calls
**Morning.** Hana tells her dad her plan.
> Hana: I=='m going to bake== a cake for Mum's birthday.
**Evening.** Her dad calls again.
> Dad: I forgot to buy candles!
> Hana: No problem, ==I'll get== some on my way home.
:::

## Deciding: now or earlier?
Both talk about future actions. The difference is the moment of the decision.

::: compare
# ⚡ will — decided now
- "We're out of milk." "Oh, ==I'll buy== some."
- "This box is heavy." "==I'll help== you."
- the decision happens as you speak
# 📌 going to — decided before
- "We're out of milk." "I know. ==I'm going to buy== some later."
- I=='m going to paint== my room blue this weekend.
- you already had the plan
:::

::: timeline
point 20 | morning: decides on a cake → going to
point now | evening: decides on candles → will
arrow now future | Mum's birthday
:::

::: trap
✗ Why do you need the ladder? — ~~I'll clean~~ the windows.
✓ I'm going to clean the windows.
You already decided before the question, so it's **going to**.
:::

## Predicting: what you see, or what you think
Both can predict too.

:::: row
::: rule 👀 going to — the signs are here
Look at those waves. Someone=='s going to fall== in.

She's pale and swaying. She=='s going to faint==.
:::
::: rule 💭 will — your opinion
Don't worry, the waves ==won't reach== us.

I think she=='ll feel== better after some water.
:::
::::

::: cards
# 🧊 a crack in the ice
Get off! The ice ==is going to break==!
# 🎟️ a good film
You=='ll enjoy== it — trust me.
# 🌳 a new tree
It=='s going to be== taller than the house one day.
# 👴 an old friend
Ivan probably ==won't remember== me.
:::

::: note 💬
Often both are fine: _I think the match ==will be== exciting_ or _==is going to be== exciting_. When something is obviously coming from the present situation, **going to** is the safer choice.
:::

::: quiz
? "I need a pen." "Here, ___ (I / lend) you mine."
= I'll lend
? I've decided. ___ (I / study) medicine.
= I'm going to study
? Look at the queue! We ___ (wait) for hours.
= 're going to wait / are going to wait
? "What would you like?" "___ (I / have) the soup."
= I'll have
:::

::: summary
- **I'll** = a decision made now; **I'm going to** = decided earlier.
- **going to** predicts from what you can see now.
- **will** predicts from what you think or believe.
:::
