::: scene 🏚️ The house that has to be sold
Two people talk about the sale, and about everything else.
> Rune: We can't hold up the sale any longer; the buyers are losing interest.
> Ada: Then let's look up the prices of similar houses tonight.
> Rune: I can't stand this street. The noise is unbearable.
> Ada: You'll have to put up with it for the winter at least.
> Rune: Don't bring up the roof again. And if you invent an excuse, I will mix you up.
:::

## Talking about things

::: compare
# 🔖 bring up — introduce a topic
- That subject is closed. Please don't ==bring it up== again.
# 🗣️ come up — be raised in conversation
- Several interesting points ==came up== in our discussion.
# 💡 come up with — produce an idea
- Sarah is creative; she is always ==coming up with new ideas==.
# 🎭 make up — invent it
- He made the whole story ==up==. None of it is true.
:::

==== **Come up** appears on its own; an idea needs **come up with**: _the topic **came up**_, _she **came up with** an idea._

## Mood and weather

::: compare
# 🙂 cheer up
- You look so sad. ==Cheer up==!
- We took him out for a meal to ==cheer him up==.
# ☀️ clear up — the weather improves
- It poured until noon, and then it ==cleared up==.
# 💰 save up for
- Dan is ==saving up for== a bicycle.
:::

## Destruction

::: compare
# 💥 blow up — explode, or be destroyed deliberately
- The engine caught fire and ==blew up==.
- The bridge was ==blown up== by the engineers last winter.
# ✂️ tear up — into pieces
- I didn't read the letter; I ==tore it up== and threw it away.
# 👊 beat up — hit badly
- He was attacked and ==beaten up==. He spent a night in hospital.
:::

## Repairs, fastenings and dictionaries

::: compare
# 🔧 do up — fasten, or repair and improve
- Do ==your coat up== before you go out.
- The kitchen looks better now that it has been ==done up==.
# 📖 look up — find the meaning
- If you don't know the word, ==look it up== in a dictionary.
# 🚪 break up / split up — separate
- I was surprised that Dan and Ruth have ==split up==.
:::

## Tolerating, delaying and confusing

::: compare
# 😐 put up with — tolerate
- We live on a busy road and have to ==put up with== the traffic noise.
# ⏳ hold up — delay
- Walk on ahead; I don't want to ==hold you up==.
# 🔀 mix up — take one for the other
- The brothers look alike; people often ==mix them up==.
- or ==get them mixed up==.
:::

::: trap
✗ It was raining at eight, but it ==cleared in== later.
✓ It was raining at eight, but it ==cleared up== later.
✗ The bridge was ==torn up== by the engineers.
✓ The bridge was ==blown up== by the engineers.
✗ We had to ==put with up== the noise.
✓ We had to ==put up with== the noise.
✗ Don't ==hold up me==; I'll walk to the station.
✓ Don't ==hold me up==; I'll walk to the station.
:::

::: summary
- In conversation: **come up** (raised), **come up with** (produce), **bring up** (introduce), **make up** (invent).
- **cheer up**, **clear up** (weather), **save up for**, **blow up**, **tear up**, **beat up**.
- **do up** = fasten or repair; **look up** = check the meaning; **put up with** = tolerate.
- **hold up** = delay, **break up / split up** = separate, **mix up** = confuse.
