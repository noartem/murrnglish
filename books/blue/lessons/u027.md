hook: 🤔 We could go… it could rain… you could have told me! — could for ideas and possibilities.
goals: make suggestions with could · say something is possible now or later · say what was possible but didn't happen with could have done · say something is impossible with couldn't (have)
---
::: scene 🏕️ A rainy Saturday
Ruth and Jonah planned a picnic, but it's pouring.
> Ruth: We ==could go== to the museum instead.
> Jonah: Or we ==could== just stay in and bake.
> Ruth: You know, we ==could have checked== the forecast yesterday.
> Jonah: True. Still, it ==could be== worse — at least we're dry!
:::

## Could for ideas
Use **could** to suggest something. It is softer than _let's_ or _should_.

::: cards
# 🍝 dinner
What shall we cook? — We ==could make== pasta.
# 🎁 a present
I don't know what to get Sam. — You ==could give== him a plant.
# 🧳 the weekend
We ==could take== the train to the coast.
:::

::: compare
# ✓ can — real ability, general truths
- I ==can== play the guitar.
- Winters here ==can== be very cold.
# ✓ could — ideas, maybe, imagined
- We ==could== play something together.
- It ==could== snow tonight.
- I'm so happy I ==could== dance!
:::

## Could for maybe, now or later
**could** also says something is **possible**: it might happen, or might be true.

:::: row
::: rule 🌦️ could = it's possible
The parcel ==could arrive== today.

Don't throw that away — it ==could be== useful.
:::
::: note 🎭
It can be unreal too: _This bed is so comfy I ==could stay== in it till Monday._ = I feel like that, though I won't.
:::
::::

## Could have done: possible, but didn't happen
**could have + past participle** looks back: something was possible in the past, but it didn't happen.

::: timeline
point 30 | a chance: take the bus
point 55 | you walked in the rain
point now | You could have taken the bus!
:::

::: examples
- Why did you wait outside? You ==could have rung== the bell.
- I ==could have gone== to Oxford, but I chose Leeds.
- The fall wasn't bad. He ==could have broken== his leg. // luckily he didn't
- We don't know where Kim is. She ==could have gone== home. // maybe she did
:::

## Couldn't, couldn't have: impossible
**couldn't** = it's impossible (now or in an imagined situation). **couldn't have done** = it was impossible in the past.

::: compare
# 🚫 couldn't do
- I ==couldn't live== without music.
- We ==couldn't afford== a house there.
# 🚫 couldn't have done
- I ==couldn't have finished== without you.
- The day ==couldn't have been== better. (= it was perfect)
:::

::: trap
✗ It was a perfect trip. It ~~couldn't be~~ better.
✓ It couldn't have been better.
Looking back at the past needs **have + past participle**.
:::

::: quiz
? "Where shall we meet?" "We ___ meet at the café."
= could
? Why did you buy a new one? You ___ (borrow) mine.
= could have borrowed
? The team was amazing. They ___ (not / play) better.
= couldn't have played
? I ___ (not / work) in an office. I'd go crazy.
= couldn't work
:::

::: summary
- **could** for suggestions: _We could go out._
- **could** = possible now or later: _It could rain._
- **could have done** = possible in the past, but it didn't happen.
- **couldn't (have)** = impossible: _I couldn't have done it without you._
:::
