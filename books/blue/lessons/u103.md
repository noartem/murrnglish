::: scene 🚗 The car park
A family tries, one Saturday morning, to get a large van into a small space.
> Marek: The space isn't wide enough for us.
> Ines: It's much too big; we'll never manage in that one.
> Marek: We were lucky — there was just enough room by the wall.
:::

## enough

**enough** goes **after** the adjective, the adverb or the noun — never before it.

:::: row
::: compare
# ✅ enough after
- The room is big ==enough== for six people.
- He runs fast ==enough== to catch the train.
- We had money ==enough== for a taxi home.

# ❌ before it
- ✗ _a enough big room_
- ✗ _enough fast_ to catch the train
:::

::: note 📌
On its own, **enough** means _sufficient_: _Will you come?_ — _I think that will be enough._

:::
::::

## too

**too** also follows the adjective or adverb, and it means *more than is wanted or possible*.

::: compare
# 🟢 too + adjective
- It is ==too cold== to sit outside.
- I am ==too busy== to talk now.
- The wall is ==too high== to climb.

# 🔢 too + noun
- There's ==too much noise== in here.
- I've had ==too many late nights== this week.
:::

## enough and too with to

After **enough** the action is possible; after **too** it is not. That is the whole difference.

::: form
# ✅ enough → can
He isn't ==tall enough== **to** reach the shelf.
There's ==enough room== **for** five of us.

# ✅ too → cannot
The piano is ==too heavy== **to** move.
I'm ==too shy== **to** speak to her.
:::

::: trap
✗ He isn't enough tall to reach the shelf.
✓ He isn't ==tall enough== to reach the shelf.
✗ It's enough warm to sit outside.
✓ It's ==warm enough== to sit outside.
✗ He doesn't have too much patience for small talk.
✓ He doesn't have ==much patience== for small talk.
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? The room isn't ___ for all of us.
= big enough
? It's ___ late to call her now.
= too
? He isn't ___ old to drive.
= old enough
? We had ___ time to finish the report.
= enough
? This box is ___ big for the car.
= too
? She speaks English ___ to work in Malta.
= well enough
? There were ___ people waiting outside.
= too many
? The kitchen wasn't ___ clean for the inspection.
= clean enough
? I was ___ tired to keep walking.
= too
:::

::: summary
- **enough** goes after the adjective, adverb or noun; **too** likewise.
- **enough** + to = something is possible: _old enough to drive_.
- **too** + to = something is impossible: _too tired to walk_.
- **too much / too many** for a quantity; **too** means more than you want.
:::
