::: scene 📻 A voice on an old recording
Tom is going through a box of tapes with his sister Bea. One is labelled "school, 1998".
> Bea: Do you remember waiting for the bus that morning?
> Tom: I remember ==waiting== — but I don't remember ==being== late.
> Bea: You were. You regret ==missing== the swimming lesson.
> Tom: I do. I regret ==taking== that job.
> Bea: And then you went on ==working== there for nine years.
> Tom: You kept ==asking== me to leave.
> Bea: I did. You stopped ==answering==.
:::

## Look at the moment
Some verbs take **-ing** or **to**, and which one you choose tells the listener **when** the action sits.

::: form
# ⏱️ -ing looks back · to looks forward
remember **-ing** | the thing happened before now
remember **to do** | you had a duty before now
forget **-ing** | the thing happened; it's gone from your mind
forget **to do** | you never did it at all
regret **-ing** | you did it, and now you wish you hadn't
regret **to do** | you didn't do it, and now you wish you had
:::

::: cards
# 💭 remember
"Her name was…?" — I don't remember ==meeting== her.
"You have to post it." — I remember ==to post== it.
# 😔 regret
"That trip was a mistake." — I regret ==taking== that trip.
"I never spoke to him." — I regret ==not speaking== to him.
# 🙈 forget
"I bumped into Ravi." — I've forgotten ==bumping== into him.
"Sorry — the milk!" — I forgot ==to buy== milk.
:::

## stop, keep, go on
These three are about what somebody did **again and again**, or **stopped** doing, and they keep the **-ing**.

::: compare
# 🔁 again and again, or never quite stopping
- Why do you keep ==asking== me questions?
- He went on ==working== until midnight.
- They stopped ==arguing== at last.
# ⏸️ the action ends, or pauses
- Please stop ==interrupting== me.
- It stopped ==raining== at last.
- We stopped ==to wait== for the lights.
:::

**Keep** can also take **to**, with a slightly different shade:

::: note 🔄
_He **kept forgetting** his passport_ = again and again, every time.
_He **kept to** the left side of the road_ = made a habit of it, deliberately.
Same **-ing** shape; a different meaning, which is why both exist.
:::

## A useful default
When a verb appears in both this unit and [[u53|the -ing unit]], the **-ing** reading is nearly always the one about something that already happened. Ask yourself: **before or after this moment?**

::: examples
- I stopped ==smoking== in March. (the habit ended)
- I stopped ==to smoke== in March. (one single act)
- She went on ==teaching== there for a decade. (the whole stretch)
- She went on ==to teach== there after a break. (back to it)
- They denied ==breaking== in. (what happened)
- They denied ==to break== in. (declined to do it)
:::

## Remember, regret, forget in practice
::: examples
- I don't remember ==hearing== the phone last night.
- Remember ==to lock== the door behind you.
- I clearly remember ==leaving== it by the window.
- I regret ==choosing== the cheap hotel.
- I now regret ==giving up== my studio.
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~I don't remember to meet her.~~
✓ I don't remember ==meeting== her.
✗ ~~I regret to take that job.~~
✓ I regret ==taking== that job.
✗ ~~I forgot to buy milk~~ → correct! Here it means you never bought it.
✗ ~~Please stop to interrupt me.~~
✓ Please stop ==interrupting== me.
✗ ~~I remember to lose my keys.~~
✓ I remember ==losing== my keys.
-ing for something that **happened**; to for something you **were supposed to do** or
**decided to do**.
:::

::: note 🔁
The easiest way to see it: **-ing looks back at the action**, while **to points forward from
the moment you are talking about**. _I remember **locking** it_ (I did lock it, I can picture
it) versus _I remember **to lock** it every night_ (that was my habit).
:::

::: summary
- **remember / forget / regret + -ing** = what **happened**; **+ to do** = what you were
  **supposed to do** or **chose to do**.
- **keep, stop, go on + -ing** = something repeated, or something that ended.
- **stop to do** = pause one thing to do another.
- Ask: is the action **before** this moment? Then usually **-ing**.
:::
