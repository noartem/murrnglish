## the for a kind

**the** before the name of a **type**, an **animal** or an **instrument** — never one particular animal or object.

::: compare
# 🐾 a kind, in general
- ==The giraffe== is taller than any other animal.
- ==The bicycle== is a cheap way to travel.
- When was ==the camera== invented?
- Can you play ==the guitar==?
# 🎯 one real object
- We saw ==a giraffe== at the zoo.
- There's ==a piano== in the corner.
- My camera is over there.
:::

::: trap
✗ Buying ==a piano== is easy. Playing ==a piano== isn't.
✓ Buying ==a piano== is easy. Playing ==the piano== isn't.
✗ We saw ==the giraffe== eating leaves in the zoo.
✓ We saw ==a giraffe== eating leaves in the zoo.
:::

## the + adjective = a group of people

::: form
# 👥 the + adjective, always plural
the old · the rich · the poor · the sick · the young

the elderly · the homeless · the injured · the unemployed

# 🙋 for one person
a rich man · a homeless person · an injured woman
:::

## Nationalities

A handful of nationality words take **the** and behave as plurals — for instance the French, or the Spanish. For one person say _a French woman_ — never _a French_ on its own.

Others work both ways: _the Chinese_ for everybody, _a Chinese_ for one person. Swiss and Portuguese behave the same way.

The rest simply add **-s**: an Italian becomes Italians, a Mexican becomes Mexicans, a Thai becomes Thais. No **the** there.

::: examples
- ==The French== are famous for their food.
- I met ==a French woman== on the train.
- ==The Chinese== are very hard-working.
- She married ==an Italian== last spring.
- Tourists from ==Japan== visited us in May.
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? ___ is the tallest animal in the world.
= The giraffe
? We saw ___ in the zoo: it was eating leaves.
= a giraffe
? Jessica plays ___ in an orchestra.
= the violin
? People should do more to help ___.
= the homeless
? I met ___ from Berlin yesterday.
= a German
:::

::: summary
- **the** for a type or kind: _the giraffe, the bicycle, the piano_.
- But a real single object takes **a**: _a piano, a giraffe_.
- **the + adjective** is a plural group: _the old, the rich, the homeless_.
- **the French, the Chinese, the Swiss** are plural; for one person say _a French woman_.
- Other nationalities pluralise with **-s**: _an Italian → Italians_.
:::
