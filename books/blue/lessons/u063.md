::: scene 🚶 A queue outside a stadium, in the rain
Rob and Prit have been standing outside for an hour. The match has been cancelled.
> Rob: ==There's no point in standing== here. It's soaked through.
> Prit: ==It's no good apologising to== the steward. He won't change anything.
> Rob: And arguing ==isn't worth it==. They won't reopen the gates.
> Prit: We ==wasted two hours getting== tickets for this.
> Rob: ==It's worth coming back== next week, though. I hear it's a good match.
> Prit: ==It was worth paying== extra for the seats, at least.
:::

## Worth it, or not worth it
**worth** and **no point** are the two verdicts you need most. Both are followed by **-ing**, and
both are about whether an action is worth doing.

::: rule 🧩 the three shapes
**worth + -ing** | it has enough value to be worth doing
**not worth + -ing** | the effort isn't worth it
**no point in + -ing** | there is no reason to do it at all

_the action_ · _the verdict_
:::

::: examples
- It was ==worth climbing== to the top for the view.
- I decided it ==wasn't worth applying== for the job.
- ==There's no point in getting== angry about it.
- It ==isn't worth waiting== for the 8.15 — take the earlier one.
- ==There's no point in hurrying== when you have plenty of time.
- We stayed calm; ==there was no point in panicking==.
:::

::: note 🤝
**Worth** also takes a noun or a **to + verb**: _The flat was worth the money._ /
_It isn't worth **going** out in this weather._

**No point in** is nearly always followed by **-ing**: _There's no point **in waiting**._
:::

## no use, no good
Two more verdicts, same shape. **no use** and **no good** say an action will achieve nothing.

::: form
# 🚫 three ways of saying "don't bother"
There's **no point in** -ing | no reason to do it
It's **no use** -ing | the action achieves nothing
It's **no good** -ing | the action achieves nothing
:::

::: examples
- ==It's no good apologising== to me. Say sorry properly.
- ==It's no use standing== here talking. We have to do something.
- ==It's no good trying to escape==. You won't get out.
- ==There's no point in looking== for him. He'll never come.
:::

All three accept **-ing** and nothing else — no **to**, no bare verb.

## Time, effort and difficulty
Three more nouns put the action after them, and each takes **-ing**.

::: form
# ⏱️ time and effort
spend time -ing | how long you do it
waste time -ing | how long you lose it
have trouble -ing | it is difficult
have difficulty -ing | it is difficult
have a problem -ing | something goes wrong
:::

::: examples
- ==How long do you spend travelling== across the city each day?
- ==How much time do you spend going== to and from work?
- ==I waste too much time watching== television.
- ==She had no trouble finding== a job.
- ==We had no difficulty understanding== one another.
- ==I have a problem remembering== people's names.
- Karen is leaving tomorrow, so she's busy ==getting her things ready==.
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~There's no point to wait.~~
✓ There's no point ==in waiting==.
✗ ~~It's no use to apologise.~~
✓ It's no use ==apologising==.
✗ ~~I waste time to watch television.~~
✓ I waste time ==watching== television.
✗ ~~It's worth to apply for it.~~
✓ It's ==worth applying== for it.
✗ ~~There's no point of waiting.~~
✓ There's no point **in** waiting.
**worth**, **no use**, **no good** and **no point in** all want **-ing** — never **to** and never
a bare verb. Only **no point** needs the extra **in**.
:::

::: cards
# 💸 worth
_The climb was worth it._
# 🙄 not worth
_It isn't worth rewriting the whole thing._
# 🧍 no point in
_There's no point in ringing twice._
# 🚫 no use / no good
_It's no good apologising to me._
# ⏱️ spending time
_I spent the morning tidying._
:::

::: note 🥾 go + activity
A small family of verbs takes an **-ing** object of its own: **go swimming, go shopping, go
skiing, go sailing, go riding**. They keep the same shape in every tense — _We **went
swimming**_ / _She **goes riding** every weekend_ — and the activity is part of the verb.
:::

::: quiz
? Why work if you don't need money? There's no point in ___ .
= working
? Don't try to study when you're tired. There's no point in ___ .
= trying to study / studying
? It's no good ___ to escape. You'll never get out.
= trying
? How long do you spend travelling to work each day?
= going / travelling
? It was a beautiful view. It was worth ___ to the top.
= going / climbing
? Lucy found a job easily. She had no trouble ___ one.
= finding / getting
? I'm learning the guitar. I spend a lot of time ___ .
= practising
? I decided it wasn't worth ___ for that post.
= applying
:::

::: summary
- **worth / not worth + -ing**; **there's no point in + -ing**.
- **it's no use -ing**, **it's no good -ing** — all take **-ing**, never **to**.
- **spend time, waste time, have trouble, have difficulty, have a problem + -ing**.
- **no point** needs the extra **in**; the others don't.
- **go swimming / shopping / skiing / sailing / riding** are fixed pairs.
:::
