## up and down: the same verb, two directions

::: compare
# ⬆️ up
- I ==put a picture up== on the wall.
- A key lay on the pavement; Ines ==picked it up==.
- Alan ==stood up== and walked out.
- Can you ==turn it up== a bit? I can't hear it.
# ⬇️ down
- I didn't like it, so I ==took it down==.
- Bea stopped writing and ==put her pen down==.
- I ==bent down== to tie my shoelace.
- The extractor fan is too loud; ==turn it down==.
:::

==== Note the pairs: **put up / take down**, **pick up / put down**, **stand up / sit down**.

## knock down, burn down

**Down** with these verbs means *destroyed or destroyed by force*.

::: examples
- Two warehouses were ==knocked down== for a car park.
- A cyclist was ==knocked down by a van== outside the school.
- They put the fire out before the house ==burnt down==.
- Why has the council ==cut down== the trees on the high street?
:::

## down = getting less

::: compare
# 🐢 slower
- You're driving too fast. ==Slow down==.
# 😌 calmer
- ==Calm down==! Nobody is listening anyway.
# ☕ smaller amounts
- I'm trying to ==cut down on coffee==.
:::

## A handful of down verbs

::: compare
# 🔧 break down — stop working
- The car ==broke down== on the motorway.
- Their marriage ==broke down== after a few months.
# 🏪 close down — stop trading
- There used to be a shop there; it ==closed down== years ago.
# 💔 let somebody down — disappoint
- You can rely on Suki. She has never once ==let me down==.
# 🙅 turn down — refuse
- I was ==turned down== for every job I applied for.
- She was offered the job but ==turned it down==.
# ✍️ write down — note it on paper
- I can't remember the address; I ==wrote it down== somewhere.
:::

==== **Turn down** a request is another meaning: _She turned down the invitation._

::: trap
✗ Please ==turn up down== the volume; I can't hear you.
✓ Please ==turn the volume down==; I can't hear you.
✗ Put the picture ==on== the wall and take it ==off==.
✓ Put the picture ==up== on the wall and take it ==down==.
✗ He was ==knocked down of== a bus.
✓ He was ==knocked down by== a bus.
✗ The house ==burnt away== last night.
✓ The house ==burnt down== last night.
✗ The shop ==broke down== when it stopped trading.
✓ The shop ==closed down== when it stopped trading.
✗ The engine ==turned down== as it climbed the hill.
✓ The engine ==slowed down== as it climbed the hill.
:::

::: summary
- **up** and **down** are opposites with the same verb: _put up / take down, pick up / put down._
- **down** also means *destroyed*: _knock down, burn down, cut down._ And *less*: _slow down, calm down._
- **break down** = stop working; **close down** = stop trading; **let down** = disappoint; **turn down** = refuse.
:::
