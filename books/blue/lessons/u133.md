## about

**about** marks the subject matter: what the talk or the action is *about*.

::: form
# 🗨️ about — the subject
talk about money
read about farming
know about her job
a discussion about safety
do something about the noise
:::

::: trap
✗ We discussed ==about== what we should do.
✓ We discussed ==what we should do==. ==(discuss takes no preposition)==
✗ She knows ==about== the new plan.
✓ She knows ==about== the new plan. ==(this one is right)==
✗ Did something ==to== the noise.
✓ Do something ==about== the noise.
:::

==== **Know about** is fine; **discuss about** is not. The difference is the verb, not the topic.

## for

**for** says what you are waiting, searching, asking or applying *for*.

::: form
# 🎯 for — what you want
ask somebody for help
apply for a job
wait for the rain
search a room for keys
leave home for work
:::

::: trap
✗ I asked ==to== somebody the way here.
✓ I stopped a passer-by and asked her ==for directions==.
✗ She left home ==to work== at seven.
✓ She left home ==for work== at seven.
✗ Wait ==to== me; I'll be there soon.
✓ Wait ==for== me; I'll be there soon.
✗ Apply ==to== the job.
✓ Apply ==for== the job. ==(apply to a company, for a post)==
:::

## care, take care, look after

Three verbs, and they are easy to muddle.

::: compare
# 💛 take care of — be responsible, keep safe
- Leave it to me; I can take care of ==the arrangements==.
# 🧓 care for — look after a person
- Nobody is free to care for ==him== at the weekend.
# 🤷 care about — think it matters
- He's selfish. He doesn't care about ==other people==.
:::

And **care for** also means *like* in a negative: _I don't care for hot weather._

**care what / where / how** takes no **about** at all: _I don't care what you do._

## look for and look after

::: compare
# 🔍 look for — search for
- My keys have gone missing; would you help me look for ==them==? I'll do the same for you.
- Kate is looking for ==a new job==.
# 🧸 look after — take care of
- Nobody is free to look after ==him== at the weekend.
- You may borrow the book, but please look after ==it== — the shop has no more.
:::

==== Looking for your keys and finding them is looking **for** them; keeping your sister's plants alive is looking **after** them.

::: summary
- **about** = the subject: _talk about, a discussion about, do something about it._
- **for** = what you want or wait for: _ask for, apply for, wait for, search for, leave for._
- **take care of** = be responsible for it; **care for** = look after a person; **care about** = think it matters.
- **look for** = search for; **look after** = keep in good condition.
:::
