::: scene 🛋️ A new flat
Ada has moved in. Her brother is helping her unpack.
> Brother: There's ==a rug== in the corner and ==a mirror== above the fireplace.
> Ada: What a room! ==The rug== is lovely, but I can't get ==the mirror== down.
> Brother: Do you like ==the view== from that window?
> Ada: I do. And is there ==a bakery== nearby, or shall we walk to the supermarket?
:::

## Say it once with a, then again with the

**a / an** the first time. **the** for that same thing later.

::: compare
# 🆕 first mention
- We hired ==a van== and ==a driver== for the move.
- She bought ==a bicycle== last spring.
- I dropped ==a cup== on the table.
# 🔁 mentioned again
- ==The van== was too small, so ==the driver== went home.
- ==The bicycle== is much easier to ride now.
- ==The cup== didn't break. It only left a mark.
:::

## Any one, or the one we know

**a/an** points at one of many. **the** picks out the one both speakers have in mind.

::: form
# 📦 one of many
Could you recommend ==a good restaurant==?
She works in ==a small office== near the river.
My uncle drives ==a van== for a living.
# 🎯 the one we know
We ate at ==the restaurant== she had chosen.
She works in ==the small office== on the corner.
Let's use ==the van== — it's already loaded.
:::

In a room, name the fittings with **the**: _the cooker, the window, the wardrobe._
For public places, everyone says **the**: _the station, the airport, the chemist._

## How often

A frequency takes no article: _once a week_, _twice a month_, _three times a day_, _£2 a kilo._

::: examples
- The bus comes ==every ten minutes==.
- She swims ==twice a week==.
- Those apples are ==£3 a kilo==.
- He drinks ==four coffees a day==. (rather a lot)
:::

::: quiz

? How far is it from here to ___ airport?
= the
? I have a headache. I think I need ___ aspirin.
= some
? Do you know ___ way to the station, or shall we ask somebody?
= the
? We visited a castle and ___ museum.
= a
:::

::: summary
- **a / an** the first time; **the** for that same thing afterwards.
- **a/an** = one of many; **the** = the particular one you both know.
- Fittings and public places take **the**: _the window, the airport, the chemist_.
- Frequency takes no article: _twice a week, £3 a kilo_.
:::
