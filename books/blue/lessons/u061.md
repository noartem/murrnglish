::: scene 🚶 The first winter in a new city
Yusuf moved to Gdansk in March. It is now December. He is on the phone to his sister.
> Yusuf: It's freezing and I still can't find the bus stop.
> Sister: You ==used to== live five minutes from a station.
> Yusuf: ==I used to== cycle everywhere. Now I walk.
> Sister: You'll ==get used to== it. The snow is normal here.
> Yusuf: I ==got used to== the cold in about a month.
> Sister: ==I'm used to== it. I've had thirty winters.
:::

## used to — a habit that has stopped
This **used to** describes something you really did, regularly, and **no longer do**. It is followed by the **past** of the verb — never **-ing**, and never **to**.

::: form
# ⏸️ finished habit
used to + **past simple**

_I used to **live** here._ (not any more)
_There used to **be** a bakery on the corner._
_I used to **go** swimming three times a week._

_in the past_ · _repeatedly_
:::

::: examples
- Daniel ==used to drink== a lot of coffee.
- We ==used to have== a big garden.
- There ==used to be== a school here.
- She ==used to smoke==, but she stopped in 2019.
:::

## be / get used to — being accustomed
This **used to** has nothing to do with the past. It means **accustomed to**, and what follows is a
**noun** or **-ing**.

::: rule 🧩 the two halves
**be used to** + noun / -ing | the state — I ==am used to it now==
**get used to** + noun / -ing | the process — I ==am getting used to it==
get **used to** (past) | I ==got used to== it within a month.

_the habit_ · _the adjustment_
:::

::: examples
- I'm ==used to working== nights now.
- I can't go on. I'm ==not used to walking== so far.
- Next week Ana starts a new post. She'll have to ==get used to working== with a new team.
- She ==got used to== living alone after a month.
- He's ==used to having== a long drive every morning.
:::

**Get used to** is what happens; **be used to** is where you have arrived. _He **got used to**
the noise._ → _Now he **is used to** it._

::: note 🪞
The same sentence can carry both, and the difference is the whole point:

_He **is used to** walking ten kilometres_ (this is normal for him now).
_He **used to** walk ten kilometres_ (he did that before, and doesn't now).
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~I am used to walk here.~~
✓ I am used to ==walking== here.
✗ ~~I used to living in Leeds.~~
✓ I used to ==live== in Leeds.
✗ ~~I didn't use to smoke.~~
✓ I ==didn't use to smoke==. / I used ==not to smoke==.
After **be / get used to**, the next word is a **noun** or **-ing**. The **used to** for a past
habit takes a **plain past verb** and never **-ing**.
:::

::: cards
# ⏸️ a habit that stopped
_I used to **smoke**._ (I don't any more)
# 🧱 the state of being accustomed
_I am used to **getting up early**._ (and I do)
# 🌱 the process of adjusting
_I am **getting used to** it._ (I'm on the way there)
:::

## Getting there takes time
**Get used to** almost always sits in a bigger story: something new, a period of difficulty, and
then normality.

::: timeline
span past-20 now | something new
point past-10 | it was strange at first
point past | she had to get used to it
point now | it's no problem at all
:::

::: examples
- Kate sold the big house. It took her months to ==get used to living== in so little space.
- Anna never got used to the weather. She can't ==get used to== it.
- Lee's salary fell. He had to ==get used to having== a lot less money.
- The children soon ==got used to== their new teacher.
:::

::: note 🪞
Note the two ways of saying no: _I **can't get used to** it_ (I never will) is different from
_I'm **not used to** it_ (I haven't yet). The first is a verdict, the second a snapshot.
:::

::: quiz
? I don't feel well. I stayed up until 3 a.m. I'm not ___ to bed so late.
= used
? My feet hurt. I'm not ___ so far.
= used to walking
? From Monday I'm starting somewhere new. I'll have to get ___ with a different team.
= used to working / used to being
? There ___ to be a bakery here.
= used
? When we were kids, we used to ___ to the lake every August.
= go
? She moved to a small flat. She had to ___ living in it.
= get used to
? I'm in charge here. I'm not used to ___ what to do.
= being
? Lisa had to get used to ___ on the left.
= driving
:::

::: summary
- **used to + past simple** = a habit that has stopped: _I used to live here._
- **be used to + noun / -ing** = being accustomed: _I'm used to working nights._
- **get used to + noun / -ing** = the process of adjusting: _I got used to it._
- After **be / get used to** comes a **noun** or **-ing**, never a bare verb.
- **I didn't use to…** or **I used not to…**.
:::
