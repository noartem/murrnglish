::: scene 🛎️ The doorbell
Kemal and Rosa are cooking when the doorbell rings.
> Rosa: My hands are covered in flour!
> Kemal: Don't worry, ==I'll get== it.
> Rosa: Thanks. Oh — ==shall we== invite whoever it is to dinner?
> Kemal: Ha! ==I'll ask==.

## I'll: decided right now
Say **I'll** when you make up your mind at the moment of speaking. It is a new decision, not a plan.

:::: row
::: rule ⚡ I'll = my decision, now
It's dark in here. ==I'll turn on== the light.

The soup's cold? ==I'll heat== it up.
:::
::: timeline
point now | decide and say it
arrow now 70 | do it
:::
::::

::: examples
- Fish or chicken? Hmm… ==I'll have== the fish.
- ==I think I'll== stay in tonight. I'm worn out.
- ==I don't think I'll== come to the gym today.
- I'm late, so I ==won't== stop for coffee. // won't = will not
:::

::: trap
✗ The phone's ringing. ~~I answer~~ it.
✓ The phone's ringing. I'll answer it.
A decision made now needs **will**, not the present simple.
:::

## Offers, promises and refusals
::: cards
# 🙋 offering
That box looks heavy. ==I'll carry== it for you.
# 👍 agreeing
"Can you water my plants?" "Sure, ==I'll do== it on Monday."
# 🤞 promising
==I'll pay== you back tomorrow. ==I won't forget==.
# 🙅 refusing
My cat ==won't eat== this new food. The door ==won't open==.
:::

::: note 🙏
**Will you…?** asks someone to do something: _==Will you== pass me the salt?_
:::

## Not for what's already arranged
If the decision was made earlier, use the present continuous or _going to_ instead.

::: compare
# ✓ decided earlier
- I=='m playing== football on Sunday.
- We=='re having== a party on the 9th.
# ✓ deciding now
- OK, ==I'll come== with you.
- Good idea, ==I'll bring== some snacks.
:::

## Shall I…? Shall we…?
Use **shall I / shall we** to offer, to suggest, or to ask what to do.

::: examples
- ==Shall I== close the window? // do you want me to?
- ==Shall we== go for a walk? // a suggestion
- The shop's closed. What ==shall we== do now? // asking for ideas
- Where ==shall I== put these flowers?
:::

::: quiz
? "Would you like cake or ice cream?" "___ ice cream, please."
= I'll have
? I can't come on Saturday. ___ (I / visit) my aunt.
= I'm visiting
? You and a friend can't decide where to eat. You ask: "Where ___?"
= shall we eat / shall we go
? I've asked him three times, but he ___ (not / help).
= won't help
:::

::: summary
- **I'll** = a decision made now; also offers, agreeing, promises.
- **won't** can mean _refuse_: _The car won't start._
- Already arranged → _I'm doing_, not _I'll do_.
- **Shall I / shall we…?** to offer, suggest or ask for ideas.
:::
