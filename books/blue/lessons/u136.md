## Verbs that take in

::: form
# 🧠 in — thinking, feeling, success
believe in ghosts
succeed in finding a way
concentrate on → no: concentrate in your studies
specialise in sports
insist on → no
invest in something
:::

Watch which of them also takes **on**: **insist on**, **concentrate on**, **rely on**.

## Verbs that take into

**into** names a surface you end up against, by accident or on purpose.

::: examples
- A thief is somebody who gets ==into a house== at night.
- The car stopped and I drove ==into the barrier==.
- Ben collided ==with another player==.
- The teacher split the class ==into four groups==.
- Translate the text ==from one language into another==.
:::

==== **Collide with** and **crashed into** both appear; the difference is whether something is struck.

## Verbs that take with

**with** names the thing you fill, equip or burden yourself with.

::: examples
- Steve gave me a bucket and told me to ==fill it with water==.
- I filled the tank ==with the wrong fuel==.
- They equipped the room ==with a new screen==.
- The road is lined ==with trees==.
:::

## Verbs that take to or on

::: compare
# 🎯 to — a purpose
- I hope you ==succeed in== — no: ==succeed to== never.
- provide somebody ==with something== → provide something ==to somebody==
- refer ==to a person== → mention something ==on the radio==
# 🧱 on — a surface, a difficulty
- The children did it ==on their own==.
- She spent a fortune ==on shoes==.
- It's just a film ==on television==.
:::

## Where there is no preposition at all

::: trap
✗ I don't believe ==in== wasting money.
✓ I don't believe ==wasting it==. ==(believe in = have faith in)==
✗ He succeeded ==to== finding another job.
✓ He succeeded ==in finding== another job.
✗ She apologised from the delay.
✓ She apologised ==for the delay==.
✗ I prefer this job ==to== my last one, so it's not a matter of luck.
✓ I prefer this job ==to== my last one. ==(right)==
✗ She concentrated on her exams.
✗ The road is filled of snow.
✓ The road is covered ==with== snow.
✗ She broke in my car and stole my bag.
✓ She broke ==into my car== and stole my bag.
:::

==== **Believe in** = have faith in. To think something is a waste of money takes nothing: _I don't believe wasting it is sensible._

::: summary
- **in**: believe in, succeed in, specialise in. **into**: break into, crash into, split into.
- **with**: fill with, equip with, crowded with. **collide with** a person, **crash into** a thing.
- **on**: insist on, concentrate on, depend on, spend money on.
- Some need nothing at all: _I don't believe wasting it is sensible._
:::
