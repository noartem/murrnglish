## so and such

**so** goes in front of an **adjective or an adverb**. **such** goes in front of a **noun**.

::: compare
# 🟢 so + adjective / adverb
- Everything is ==so expensive== these days.
- She sings ==so beautifully== that we all stop.
- I haven't been ==so tired==.

# 🔵 such + noun
- He always looks good. He wears ==such nice clothes==.
- We had ==such bad luck== that everything went wrong.
- What a garden! These are ==such lovely flowers==.
:::

::: note ➕
With a **singular countable** noun, **such** needs **a**: _such a nice garden_, _such a shame_, _such a long way_. With a plural or an uncountable noun it does not: _such nice clothes_, _such bad luck_.

:::

## The order of the words

The two patterns swap places round the adjective, and that is the whole test.

::: form
# so
so + adjective + a/an + noun
It was ==so bad a storm== that the coast was closed.

# such
such + a/an + adjective + noun
It was ==such a bad storm== that the coast was closed.
:::

Short questions and exclamations follow the same order: _Why are you **in such a hurry**?_ · _You're **driving so slowly**!_

::: trap
✗ It was so a long journey that we stopped twice.
✓ It was ==such a long journey== that we stopped twice.
✗ I had no idea the station was so a long walk from here.
✓ I had no idea the station was ==such a long walk== from here.
✗ He was such terrible weather for the match.
✓ The weather was ==so terrible== for the match.
:::

## so much, so far, such a shame

Some pairs are fixed and must be learnt whole.

::: words
so much · so many · so far · so little · so few
such a shame · such a pity · such a long time · such a good idea
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? It was ___ a relief to sit down.
= such
? The queue was ___ long that we gave up.
= so
? What ___ weather!
= such
? She sings ___ well that they ask her to come back.
= so
? He wears ___ shirts to work.
= such
? Why does it always take you ___ time?
= such
? We had ___ bad luck that nothing worked.
= such
? I've never seen ___ people at one concert.
= so
? It was ___ pity you missed the show.
= such
:::

::: summary
- **so** + adjective or adverb: _so expensive_, _so beautifully_.
- **such** + noun; with a singular countable noun, **such a**: _such a shock_.
- The word order flips: _so bad a storm_ / _such a bad storm_.
- Learn the fixed pairs whole: so much, so far, such a shame.
:::
