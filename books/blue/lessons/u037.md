It's busy at Café Lumo. Jin is at the counter.
> Jin: ==Could I have== a hot chocolate, please?
> Barista: Sure. ==Would you like== cream on top?
> Jin: Yes, please. Oh — ==do you mind if I== charge my phone here?
> Barista: Go ahead. ==Could you== just keep it away from the sink?

## Asking someone to do something
Start with **Can you…?** or **Could you…?** _Could_ is a bit more polite.

:::: row
::: examples
- ==Can you== hold this for a second?
- ==Could you== close the door, please?
- ==Do you think you could== lend me your notes?
- ==Would you mind== repeating that? // mind + -ing
:::
::: note 🚫
Don't ask with _Do you…_: not _~~Do you pass me the salt?~~_ Say ==Can / Could you pass== me the salt?
:::
::::

## Asking for things
Use **Can I have…?** / **Could I have…?** / **Can I get…?** In shops and cafés, **I'd like…** is common too.

::: cards
# 🧾 at the restaurant
==Could we have== the bill, please?
# 🍰 at the bakery
==I'd like== two of those buns, please.
# 💧 a favour
==Can I have== a glass of water?
:::

## Asking for permission
::: compare
# 🙋 Can / Could / May I…?
- ==Can I== sit here?
- ==Could I== use your charger?
- ==May I== ask a question (formal)?
# 🙏 Is it OK / Do you mind if…?
- ==Is it OK if I== open the window?
- ==Do you mind if I== sit here?
- Answer: _No, not at all._ = it's fine
:::

::: trap
✗ Do you mind if I sit here? — ~~Yes, sure!~~
✓ No, not at all. Go ahead.
_Do you mind…?_ = _Is it a problem?_ So "no" means "it's fine".
:::

## Offering and inviting
::: cards
# 🍪 offering things
==Would you like== a biscuit? (not _Do you like…?_)
# 🙌 offering help
==Can I== carry that for you? ==Shall I== get the door?
# 🎟️ inviting
==Would you like to come== to the park with us?
:::

::: words
Would you like…? · Would you like to…? · Can I…? · Shall I…? · I'd like… · I'd like to…
:::

::: quiz
? You want your friend to help you move a sofa. You say: "___ help me with this sofa?"
= Can you / Could you
? You want to use a friend's laptop. You ask: "___ use your laptop?"
= Can I / Could I / Do you mind if I
? You offer a guest some tea: "___ some tea?"
= Would you like
? At a ticket office: "___ two tickets for the 8 o'clock show, please."
= I'd like
:::

::: summary
- Asking people to do things: **Can / Could you…? Do you think you could…?**
- Asking for things: **Can / Could I have…? I'd like…**
- Permission: **Can / Could / May I…? Is it OK if…? Do you mind if…?**
- Offers and invitations: **Would you like (to)…? Can I…? Shall I…?**
:::
