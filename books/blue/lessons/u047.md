::: scene ☎️ After the phone call
Ines has just talked to her friend Bruno. Her flatmate wants to know what he said.
> Bruno (earlier): "I'm moving to Berlin. I've found a job there. I'll call you next week."
> Flatmate: So, what did he say?
> Ines: He ==said he was moving== to Berlin. He ==said he'd found== a job there, and that he ==would call== me next week.
:::

## Direct and reported speech
**Direct speech** gives the speaker's exact words. **Reported speech** tells someone else what was said, usually with **said (that)** or **told me (that)**. _that_ can be left out.

::: compare
# 💬 direct speech
- Ana: "I'm tired."
- Leo: "I don't like jazz."
# 🔁 reported speech
- Ana ==said (that)== she ==was== tired.
- Leo ==told me== he ==didn't like== jazz.
:::

## The tenses move back
When the reporting verb is past (_said_), the verb in the reported part usually moves one step into the past.

::: form
# 🔙 what was said → how you report it
am / is / are | ==was / were==
want / like / know | ==wanted / liked / knew==
am doing | ==was doing==
have done / did | ==had done== (or _did_)
will / can | ==would / could==
am going to | ==was going to==
:::

::: cards
# 😴 Nora
"I'm going to bed." → She said she ==was going== to bed.
# 🎧 Sam
"You can have my headphones." → He said I ==could have== his.
# 🚲 Omar
"I lost my bike." → He told me he=='d lost / lost== his bike.
:::

::: trap
✗ She ~~said me~~ that she was busy.
✓ She told me that she was busy. / She said that she was busy.
**tell** + a person; **say** without one (more on this in [[u48]]).
:::

## When you don't have to change
If you report something that is **still true** now, you can keep the original tense. Moving back is always possible.

::: examples
- "My sister lives in Rome." → She said her sister ==lives / lived== in Rome. // still true
- "I'm going to Lima in May." (it's April) → He said he=='s going / was going== in May.
- "I can't swim." → Tom said he ==can't / couldn't== swim.
:::

::: note 📌
Words like _I, my, here, today_ often change too: "I'll see you **here tomorrow**." → She said she'd see me **there the next day**.
:::

::: quiz
? "I don't want to come." → Max said he ___ to come.
= didn't want
? "I'll help you." → She said she ___ me.
= would help / 'd help
? "We've finished." → They told me they ___.
= had finished / 'd finished
? "I'm watching a film." → He said he ___ a film.
= was watching
:::

::: summary
- **said (that)…** / **told me (that)…** to report speech.
- After a past reporting verb, tenses usually move back: _is → was, will → would, can → could, have done → had done_.
- If it's still true, you can keep the present.
:::
