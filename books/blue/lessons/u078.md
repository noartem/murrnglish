## Streets and transport

Street names, squares and parks take **no the**: _Abbey Road, Fifth Avenue, Hyde Park, Broadway, Times Square._

Airports, stations and universities keep it too, and their names are usually two words: _Manchester Airport_, _Victoria Station_, _Harvard University_, _Yale University Press._

::: trap
✗ We took a train from ==the Manchester Airport==.
✓ We took a train from ==Manchester Airport==.
✗ I live on ==the Abbey Road==.
✓ I live on ==Abbey Road==.
:::

## Hotels, museums, companies

Most other buildings **do** take **the**, and you can leave out the type word: _the Sheraton (Hotel)_, _the Odeon (cinema)_, _the Guggenheim (Museum)._

Some are only **the + noun**: _the Acropolis_, _the Kremlin_, _the Pentagon_, _the White House_, _the Eiffel Tower_, _the Empire State._

::: note 🏰
_Buckingham Palace_ has no the, but _the Royal Palace_ does — _Royal_ is an adjective, not a name.
:::

## The rest of the alphabet

Shops and restaurants named after people end in **-’s** and take no the: _McDonald’s, Joe’s Diner, Macy’s, Barclays_.
Churches: _St John’s Church, St Patrick’s Cathedral_.
Newspapers and organisations take **the**: _the Washington Post, the BBC, the Red Cross, the European Union_.
Companies often don't: _Fiat, Sony, Kodak, IBM, Singapore Airlines_.

And names with **of** take **the**: _the Bank of England, the Tower of London_ — but _the University of Cambridge_ becomes _Cambridge University_.
::: examples
- We visited ==the Bank of England== and then had lunch nearby.
- She works at ==the Museum of Modern Art==.
- ==The Tower of London== is open to the public.
- He graduated from Cambridge University.
:::

::: summary
- Streets and transport take **no the**: _Abbey Road, Manchester Airport, Cambridge University_.
- Most other buildings take **the**: _the Odeon, the Guggenheim, the White House_.
- Shops named after people take no the: _McDonald’s, Barclays_.
- Newspapers and organisations take **the**: _the BBC, the Washington Post_.
- Names with **of** take **the**: _the Bank of England, the Tower of London_.
