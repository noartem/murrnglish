::: scene 🧭 Sunday morning
Nadia and Leo are in the flat, then out on the street.
> Leo: Your keys are **on** the table, next to the fruit.
> Nadia: There is milk **in** the fridge and bread **on** the counter.
> Leo: I'll meet you **at** the bus stop **in** ten minutes.
> Nadia: Careful — there's a spider **on** the door.
> Leo: Relax, I'm not going **in** there.
:::

## in — inside a space

**in** says a thing is *within* an enclosed space or an area, with no attention to its surface.

::: form
# 📦 in + a space
in a room
in a garden
in a pool
in a box
in a town
in the city centre
in the river
:::

- There is nobody **in** the building.
- What do you have **in** your hand?
- A friend of mine grew up **in** a village **in** the mountains.

## at — a point, a place to go

**at** marks a single point. On a route it tells you where to turn; on a map, where a place stands.

::: form
# 📍 at + a point
at the bus stop
at the door
at the roundabout
at her desk
at the traffic lights
:::

- Turn left **at** the church.
- Leave your key **at** reception.

## in or at?

::: compare
# 🏠 in — inside the building
- The shop was packed **in** the afternoon.
- Wait for me **in** the hotel lobby.
# 🚪 at — the point itself
- Walk down the road and turn left **at** the chemist.
- Wait for me **at** the hotel entrance. ==(outside)==
:::

The same doorway gives you either reading: **at the door** is the doorway as a point, **in the room** is inside the room.

## on — touching a surface

**on** says a thing rests on a surface: a top, a side, a face.

::: words
on the wall · on the door
on the table · on the floor
on her nose · on a page
on an island · on the beach
:::

- I sat **on** the grass.
- There's a dirty mark **on** your shirt.
- You will find the details **on** page seven.

## in, on or at — the same object

::: compare
# 🥛 in — the stuff inside
- There is a little water **in** the bottle.
# 🏷️ on — the surface
- The label is printed **on** the bottle.
# 🚪 at — the point
- There is somebody **at** the door, and nobody will open it.
:::

::: summary
- **in** = inside a space or an area: _in the fridge, in the river, in a small village._
- **at** = a single point, and on a route it tells you where to turn: _at the bus stop, at the roundabout._
- **on** = touching a surface: _on the table, on her nose, on page seven._
- The same object takes different ones: water **in** the bottle, a label **on** it.
:::
