## the -est or the most

Exactly the same rule as the comparative, with **the** in front.

:::: row
::: compare
# ➕ the + -est
the tallest · the cheapest · the shortest · the oldest
the happiest · the easiest

# ➕ the + most + …
the most expensive · the most important · the most interesting
:::

::: note 🅰️
With **most** you must say **the most** and never leave out the **the**: _the most expensive hotel in town_.

:::
::::

## Spelling and the irregulars

::: form
# spelling
big → the bigg==est==
easy → the eas==iest==
happy → the happ==iest==
thin → the thin==nest==

# irregular
good → the ==best==
bad → the ==worst==
far → the ==furthest== / ==farthest==
many → the ==most==
:::

## in or of

::: form
# 📍 in — for a place or a group
the best room ==in== the hotel
the busiest time ==of== — see the next box

# 📦 of — for something you belong to
the happiest day ==of== my life
the most valuable painting ==in== the museum
:::

The rule: **in** for a container or a group of people (_in the class_, _in the world_), **of** for the thing that belongs to someone (_the best day of my life_, _the worst moment of the trip_).

## one of the …

To say somebody is among the best rather than the best, use **one of the** + superlative + a plural noun.

::: compare
# 🏆 the winner
She is ==the fastest== runner.

# 🥈 one of many
She is ==one of the fastest== runners.
:::

## Two things, or ever

For only **two** things, keep the comparative: _Which is quicker, the bus or the train?_ And **ever** turns a superlative into a life-time claim: _the best film I've ever seen._

::: trap
✗ She is the most tallest girl in her class.
✓ She is ==the tallest== girl in her class.
✗ Everest is the highest than any other mountain.
✓ Everest is the highest mountain in the world; it is ==higher== than any other.
✗ This is the better restaurant in town.
✓ This is the ==best== restaurant in town.
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? It was the ___ day of the year.
= hottest
? This building is 250 metres high, but it isn't ___ in the city.
= the tallest
? What is ___ sport in your country?
= the most popular
? The mountain we climbed last week is the ___ of the three.
= highest
? Everest is ___ mountain on earth.
= the highest
? She is ___ of the best players here.
= one
? Which is ___ , the bus or the train?
= quicker
? She is one of the best ___ in the club.
= players
? That's the ___ museum I've ever visited.
= best
:::

::: summary
- **the + -est** for short words, **the + most** for long ones.
- Spelling follows the comparative: biggest, easiest; irregulars: best, worst, furthest.
- **in** for a place or a group; **of** for what belongs to someone.
- **one of the best + plural noun** for "among the best"; keep the comparative for two things.
:::
