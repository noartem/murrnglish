## Two kinds of noun

Some nouns **count**. You can say one, two, three of them, and they have a plural.

Some nouns **don't count**. They have one form only, and you never put a number in front of them.

:::: row
::: form
# 🔢 countable
one banana · two bananas · three bananas

a banana · the banana · my bananas

You can count these.
:::
::: form
# 🥣 uncountable
rice · bread · milk · music · money

No _two rices_. No _a rice_.
:::
::::

::: note 🍚
An **event** noun counts even when the thing itself doesn't: _a race_, _a class_, _a journey_, _a meal_.
:::

## a and an

**a / an** goes in front of a **singular countable** noun: one single thing you could point at.

:::: row
::: cards
# 🌂 an umbrella
I need ==an== umbrella. The shop sells ==umbrellas==.
# ⌚ a watch
She bought ==a== watch and three ==watches==.
# 📕 a book
This is ==a== good book. I've read six ==books== this year.
:::
::: trap
✗ There has been ==a accident== on the road.
✓ There has been ==an accident== on the road.
✗ I need ==a information== about the flights.
✓ I need ==information== about the flights. (no _a_ at all)
:::
::::

A plural countable noun can stand **alone**: _Apples are healthy._

## The words that go with each kind

::: form
# 📦 quantifiers
countable plural | some bananas · many pictures · a few jokes · how many languages
uncountable | some rice · much work · a little patience · how much time
both | some · any · no · a lot of · plenty of
:::

Measure an uncountable noun with **a … of**: _a bag of rice_, _a glass of water_, _a piece of advice_.

## Quiz

::: quiz
? I'd like ___ glass of water, please. → I'd like
= a
? There are ___ people waiting outside.
= many
? We don't have ___ bread left.
= any
? She ate ___ rice and an apple.
= – (no article with an uncountable noun)
? How ___ languages do you speak?
= many
? There was ___ traffic this morning.
= a lot of
:::

::: summary
- Countable nouns have a singular and a plural: _banana / bananas_.
- Uncountable nouns have one form only: _rice, bread, money, music_.
- **a / an** only before a singular countable noun.
- **many / few / a few / how many** with plurals; **much / little / how much** with uncountable nouns.
- Measure with **a … of**: _a bag of rice_, _a piece of advice_.
:::
