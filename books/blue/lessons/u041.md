::: scene 🏠 The flatmates
Rosa and Tim share a flat. It's Saturday morning.
> Rosa: ==I wish== you ==wouldn't leave== your socks on the sofa.
> Tim: ==I wish== you ==would== stop nagging! Anyway, ==I hope== it's sunny today — I'm going cycling.
> Rosa: Well, ==I wish== you a nice ride. Take your socks!
:::

## Wish or hope?
::: compare
# 🎁 wish somebody something
- ==I wish== you a happy birthday.
- They ==wished== us good luck.
- set phrases with a noun
# 🤞 hope + a possible future
- ==I hope== you have a great time.
- ==We hope== the rain stops.
- it can really happen
# 🌠 wish + past = unreal
- ==I wish== I ==was== on holiday.
- ==I wish== it ==wasn't== raining.
- it's not true now
:::

::: trap
✗ I ~~wish~~ you get the job!
✓ I hope you get the job!
For something that may well happen, use **hope**.
:::

## I wish … would
**I wish somebody / something would…** = I'd like it to happen or change; often I'm annoyed or impatient.

:::: row
::: rule 😤 wish + would
==I wish== the kettle ==would== hurry up.

==I wish== you ==would== listen to me!

==I wish== someone ==would== fix that light.
:::
::: rule 🙄 wish + wouldn't
For habits that annoy you:

==I wish== you ==wouldn't== chew so loudly.

==I wish== people ==wouldn't== park here.
:::
::::

::: note ⚠️
Not with your own actions or with states: not _I wish I would…_ Say _I wish I ==had== a car_ (not _would have_), _I wish I ==knew==_ (not _would know_).
:::

## Which form after wish?
::: form
# 🗺️ a map of wishes
now, a state | wish + **past** | I wish I ==was== taller.
now, ability | wish + **could** | I wish I ==could== sing.
the past | wish + **had done** | I wish I=='d gone== to bed earlier.
past ability | wish + **could have done** | I wish I ==could have come==.
a change / an annoying habit | wish + **would** | I wish it ==would== stop snowing.
:::

::: cards
# 😴 tired
I wish I ==wasn't== so tired.
# 🎸 the concert
I wish I ==could have gone== — I was ill.
# 🚪 the door
I wish you ==would== close the door behind you!
:::

::: quiz
? Good luck tomorrow. I ___ you pass. (wish / hope)
= hope
? My neighbour plays drums at night. I wish he ___ (stop).
= would stop / 'd stop
? I didn't go to the party. I wish I ___ (go).
= had gone / 'd gone
? I can't come with you. I wish I ___ (can) come.
= could
:::

::: summary
- **wish somebody luck / a happy…**; **hope** for real possibilities.
- **I wish … would** = I want a change, often impatient or annoyed.
- **I wish … wouldn't** = an annoying habit.
- wish + past (now), + could, + had done (past), + could have done.
:::
