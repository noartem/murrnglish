::: scene 🎬 Ten minutes to showtime
Hugo and Ama are still at home. The cinema is across town.
> Ama: We=='d better== leave now, or we'll miss the start.
> Hugo: Wait — we=='d better not== forget the tickets.
> Ama: Hugo, ==it's time we went==! You've been looking for your shoes for ages.
:::

## Had better: do it, or there'll be trouble
**had better (do)** = it's advisable in this situation; if you don't, something bad could happen. It's for one particular situation, now.

:::: row
::: form
# ➕
I'd / you'd / we'd… | ==better== | go
# ➖
I'd / you'd / we'd… | ==better not== | go
# ❓ (rare)
==hadn't== we ==better== go?
:::
::: rule 🚨 now, in this situation
Your hands are frozen. You=='d better== put gloves on.

The baby's asleep. We=='d better not== make a noise.
:::
::::

::: trap
✗ We'd better ~~to~~ go.
✓ We'd better go.
✗ You'd ~~not better~~ be late.
✓ You'd better not be late.
**had** is past in form, but the meaning is now or the future. No _to_ after _better_.
:::

## Had better or should?
::: compare
# 🚨 had better — one situation, a risk
- It's going to rain. We=='d better== take umbrellas.
- I=='d better== charge my phone before we go.
# 🧭 should — in general, an opinion
- You ==should== always carry an umbrella in London.
- People ==should== spend less time on their phones.
:::

::: note 📌
**should** also works in particular situations: _It's late. We ==should== go._ But _had better_ doesn't work for general advice: not _Everybody had better exercise._
:::

## It's time…
::: cards
# ⏰ It's time to…
==It's time to== go to bed.

==It's time for== lunch.
# 👉 It's time somebody did…
==It's time== the dog ==went== for a walk.

==It's time== I ==bought== a new laptop.
:::

::: rule 🕰️ It's time + subject + past
After **It's time** + a subject, use the **past** form, but the meaning is present: it should happen now, or it should have happened already.

==It's time== they ==were== here. They're late.

==It's high time== something ==was done== about the traffic.
:::

::: quiz
? The shop closes in five minutes. We ___ hurry.
= 'd better / had better
? You ___ (not / tell) Mum about the broken vase.
= 'd better not tell
? I think everybody ___ learn first aid. (should / had better)
= should
? It's 11 p.m. It's time you ___ (go) to bed.
= went
:::

::: summary
- **had better (not) + verb** = urgent advice for a particular situation.
- No _to_: _You'd better go._
- **should** is for general advice too; _had better_ isn't.
- **It's time to do** / **It's time somebody did** (past form, present meaning).
:::
