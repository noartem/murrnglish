::: scene 🍣 Dinner plans
Last week Kofi told Lena, "I don't eat raw fish." Tonight, he has an idea.
> Kofi: Let's go for sushi!
> Lena: Sushi? ==But you said== you ==didn't eat== raw fish!
> Kofi: I've changed. Anyway, you ==told me== to try new things.
> Lena: I ==asked== you ==to== try new vegetables, not raw fish!
:::

## But you said…
When what someone says now doesn't match what they said before, use **But you said…** with the past tense: you're reporting their earlier words.

::: cards
# 🎹 the piano
"I'm buying a piano." — ==But you said== you ==couldn't== play!
# 🏖️ the beach
"Let's go to the beach." — ==But you said== you ==hated== sand!
# 🏠 the cousin
"My cousin lives here." — ==But you said== you ==didn't have== any cousins!
:::

## Say or tell?
::: compare
# 👤 tell + a person
- ==Tell== me about your trip.
- She ==told== us she was leaving.
- He ==told== Jo the truth.
- tell a story / a joke / a lie / the time
# 🗨️ say — no person (or say to)
- ==Say== something!
- She ==said== she was leaving.
- He ==said== goodbye ==to== me.
- say hello / sorry / yes / no
:::

::: trap
✗ He ~~said me~~ to wait.
✓ He told me to wait.
✗ What did she ~~tell~~?
✓ What did she say?
:::

## Told / asked somebody to do
Report orders, advice and requests with **tell / ask + person + to + verb**. For a negative, **not to**.

:::: row
::: form
# 🔁 reporting
"Sit down, please." | She ==asked== me ==to== sit down.
"Don't touch it!" | He ==told== us ==not to== touch it.
"Could you help me?" | Ali ==asked== me ==to== help him.
:::
::: rule 🧩 the pattern
**told / asked** + **person** + **(not) to** + verb

_said_ works without the person: _He ==said to== wait here._ (but not _said me_)
:::
::::

::: examples
- "Please call me back." → Maya ==asked== me ==to== call her back.
- "Don't be late, kids." → Dad ==told== the kids ==not to be== late.
- "Can you lend me some money?" → My brother ==asked== me ==to lend== him some money.
:::

::: quiz
? You said you were vegetarian. Now you're eating a steak! → But you said you ___ vegetarian!
= were
? Can you ___ me the way to the museum? (say / tell)
= tell
? She ___ goodbye and left. (said / told)
= said
? "Don't open the box." → He told me ___ the box.
= not to open
:::

::: summary
- **But you said…** + past, for a contradiction.
- **tell + person**; **say** with no person (or _say to someone_).
- **told / asked + person + (not) to + verb** for orders and requests.
:::
