> Friend: Did you guess?

## I was given…: two objects
Some verbs have two objects: _give, offer, send, show, tell, ask, pay, teach_. Either object can be the subject of the passive. It's more usual to begin with the **person**.

::: compare
# 🙋 active
- They gave **Ali** a medal.
- Someone showed **us** the way.
- They paid **me** €50.
# 📦 passive — the person first
- **Ali** ==was given== a medal.
- **We** ==were shown== the way.
- **I** ==was paid== €50.
:::

::: note 🔄
The thing can come first too: _A medal ==was given== to Ali._ That's possible but less common.
:::

## Being done
After verbs like **like, hate, avoid, enjoy, remember**, and after **prepositions** (_of, without, about_), the passive is **being + past participle**.

::: cards
# 😤 hate
Kids hate ==being told== what to do.
# 🎉 without
We went in without ==being seen==.
# 😨 afraid of
He's afraid of ==being left== alone.
# 🦟 avoid
Wear long sleeves to avoid ==being bitten==.
:::

::: examples
- I don't mind ==being asked== for help.
- She remembers ==being taken== to the zoo as a child.
- I didn't expect ==to be invited==. // after expect: to be done
:::

## Born
We say **I was born**, never _I am born_ or _I born_.

::: trap
✗ Where ~~are~~ you born?
✓ Where were you born?
✗ I ~~born~~ in 2005.
✓ I was born in 2005.
:::

## Get + past participle
**get** can replace _be_ in the passive, mostly in everyday speech, for things that **happen** (often by chance or something bad).

:::: row
::: words
get hurt · get stolen · get broken · get paid · get invited · get stung · get lost · get stopped
:::
::: examples
- Be careful — you'll ==get hurt==.
- My phone ==got stolen== on holiday.
- How often do you ==get paid==?
- ==Did== the window ==get broken== in the storm?
:::
::::

::: note 📌
Not with states: _The house ==is owned== by my aunt_ (not _gets owned_). And _get_ makes questions with _do / did_: _How ==did== it ==get== damaged?_
:::

::: quiz
? They told me the news. → I ___ the news.
= was told
? I'm tired of ___ (ask) the same question.
= being asked
? Which hospital ___ you born in?
= were
? We ___ (get / lose) on the way and arrived late.
= got lost
:::

::: summary
- Two-object verbs: _I was given…, We were told…_
- **being done** after _like, hate, avoid…_ and prepositions.
- **was / were born** — always passive.
- **get + past participle** = passive in everyday speech.
:::
