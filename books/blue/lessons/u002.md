::: scene 🐝 Meet Hana
Hana ==keeps== bees on the roof of her building in Lisbon.
> Hana: My bees ==fly== up to three kilometres for flowers. In a good summer they ==make== about twenty kilos of honey.
> Neighbour: And ==do== they ever sting you?
> Hana: Not often. They ==don't like== bad weather, so I never ==open== the hives when it rains.
:::

## Things that are true in general
Nothing in the scene is happening at this moment — Hana might be asleep as she says it. The **present simple** describes how things are: habits, routines, facts about the world, things that happen again and again.

::: cards
# 🔁 Habits
I cycle to work, even in winter.
# 🗓️ Routines
The market opens at seven on Saturdays.
# 🌍 Facts
Light travels faster than sound.
# 💼 Jobs and lives
My aunt runs a small hotel by the sea.
:::

::: timeline
repeat past future | it happens again and again
:::

## Forms: -s for he, she, it
:::: row
::: form
# ➕
I / we / you / they | work
he / she / it | work==s==
# ➖
I / we / you / they | ==don't== work
he / she / it | ==doesn't== work
:::
::: form
# ❓
==do== | I / we / you / they | work?
==does== | he / she / it | work?
:::
::::

After _does_ and _doesn't_ the verb has no -s: the -s has moved to the helper. ==Does== she ==work==? She ==doesn't work== on Fridays.

::: words ✍️ -es and -ies
watch → watches · go → goes · fix → fixes · miss → misses · study → studies · carry → carries · have → has
:::

::: trap
✗ She ~~work~~ at the airport.
✓ She works at the airport.
✗ ~~Does she works~~ at the airport?
✓ Does she work at the airport?
✗ He ~~don't~~ eat fish.
✓ He doesn't eat fish.
:::

::: examples ❓ Questions people ask
- ==Where do== you ==live==?
- ==What does== your sister ==do==? — She's a vet.
- ==How long does== it ==take== to get to the station?
- ==Do== you ==know== what this word means?
:::

## How often?
When you say how often something happens, the present simple is the natural choice.

::: compare
# 🔝 Adverbs before the verb
- I ==always== have tea in the morning.
- We ==usually== eat at eight.
- She ==never== drinks coffee.
# 🔚 Phrases at the end
- I go swimming ==twice a week==.
- They visit us ==every summer==.
- How often do you see him? — ==Once a month==.
:::

::: words 📊 From always to never
always · usually · often · sometimes · rarely · hardly ever · never
:::

## Saying it is doing it
Some verbs do the action just by being said. _I promise_ is a promise, _I apologise_ is an apology. These go in the present simple, not the continuous.

::: examples 🤝
- ==I promise== I'll call you tonight.
- ==I apologise== for being so late.
- You must let me pay. ==I insist==.
- Try the fish soup — ==I recommend== it.
- ==I suggest== we leave early and avoid the traffic.
- You're right. ==I agree== with you.
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~I'm promising~~ I won't tell anyone.
✓ I promise I won't tell anyone.
:::

::: quiz
? Water ___ (boil) at 100 degrees.
= boils
? ___ (you / often / go) to concerts?
= Do you often go
? My brother ___ (not / eat) meat.
= doesn't eat
? What time ___ (the shop / close)?
= does the shop close
:::

::: summary
- Present simple = habits, routines, facts, things that happen again and again.
- he / she / it + **-s**: she works, it goes, he studies.
- Questions and negatives use **do / does**: Do you…? She doesn't…
- Say how often with always, usually, never, twice a week…
- I promise, I apologise, I agree — the words do the action.
:::
