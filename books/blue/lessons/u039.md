## If I knew…: the opposite of the facts
Use **if + past** for a present situation that **isn't true**. The past form doesn't mean past time here.

::: compare
# 📰 the real situation
- I don't know his address.
- We live in a tiny flat.
- It's raining.
# 💭 the imagined one
- ==If== I ==knew== his address, I=='d send== him a card.
- ==If== we ==didn't live== in a tiny flat, we=='d get== a dog.
- ==If== it ==wasn't raining==, we ==could play== outside.
:::

::: trap
✗ If I ~~would know~~ the answer, I'd tell you.
✓ If I knew the answer, I'd tell you.
:::

## If I were…
After **if**, you can say **were** instead of _was_ (I / he / she / it). It's common in careful speech and writing. **If I were you** is the usual way to give advice.

:::: row
::: examples
- ==If I were== taller, I'd play basketball.
- ==If== Sam ==were== here, he'd know what to do.
- ==If I were you==, I'd call her. // my advice
:::
::: note 🪄
_If I ==could==…_ = if I were able to: _If I ==could== fly, I'd go to work by air._
:::
::::

## I wish I knew…
**I wish + past** = I'm sorry things are this way; I'd like them to be different **now**.

:::: row
::: rule 🌠 wish + past = now
I ==wish== I ==had== a bigger room (but I don't).

I ==wish== you ==lived== nearer (but you don't).

I ==wish== it ==wasn't== so hot.
:::
::: form
# 😕 the fact → the wish
I can't swim. | I wish I ==could== swim.
I have to work. | I wish I ==didn't have to== work.
I'm not at home. | I wish I ==was / were== at home.
I don't know Spanish. | I wish I ==knew== Spanish.
:::
::::

::: trap
✗ I wish I ~~would have~~ a car.
✓ I wish I had a car.
✗ I wish I ~~can~~ dance.
✓ I wish I could dance.
:::

::: cards
# 🌦️ the weather
I ==wish== it ==was== sunny.
# 📚 school
I ==wish== I ==didn't have== a test tomorrow.
# 🐶 a pet
I ==wish== we ==had== a dog.
:::

::: quiz
? I'd go out tonight if I ___ (not / have) so much homework.
= didn't have
? If I ___ (be) you, I wouldn't buy that phone.
= were / was
? I can't drive. → I wish I ___ drive.
= could
? It's so noisy here. → I wish it ___ (be) quieter.
= was / were
:::

::: summary
- **if + past** = an unreal present: _If I knew…, I'd…_
- **if I were** (also _was_); **If I were you…** for advice.
- **I wish + past** = I'd like the present to be different.
- **could** after _if_ and _wish_: _I wish I could…_
:::
