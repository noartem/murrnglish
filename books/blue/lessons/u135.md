::: scene 🏥 The ward round
A doctor talks to a patient and a visitor.
> Doctor: You've been **suffering from** a chest infection for a week.
> Nia: Most of the time it protects me **from** catching anything else.
> Doctor: Then I'd say the clinic **depends on** patients turning up.
> Nia: Nobody **blames** me for catching it, I hope.
> Doctor: Not at all. And I must say, you **cope with** it remarkably well.
:::

## of

::: form
# ⚖️ of — accusation, approval, cause, contents
accuse somebody of lying
suspect somebody of cheating
approve of what he does
die of a heart attack
consist of seven courses
:::

==== **Accuse somebody of** it is, while **blame somebody for** it is your fault — the same mistake, a different word.

## for

**for** marks what somebody did, or what something cost.

::: compare
# 💸 pay for — a cost or a thing
- We hadn't brought enough cash to ==pay for dinner==.
# 🧾 pay — a bill, rent, a fee, tax
- The landlord wants three months in advance; we must ==pay the rent== first.
# 🙏 thank / forgive / apologise for
- I'll never ==forgive them for the trick they played==.
- She apologised ==for my mistake==. ==(and to them)==
# 👉 blame for / on
- Everyone ==blamed me for the accident==.
- They said I ==was to blame for the accident==.
- Don't ==blame it on me==.
:::

The **for** and the **on** can swap in the last pair: _blame the accident **on** the driver_ / _blame the driver **for** the accident._

## from

**from** marks what somebody has or suffers.

::: form
# 🛡️ from — illness, protection
suffer from heart disease
recover from the flu
protect the skin from the sun
protect somebody from harm
shield somebody from the weather
:::

==== **Suffer from** an illness, but **die of** or **die from** it — the verb decides.

## on

**on** marks a support, a cost of living, or a congratulation.

::: form
# 🧱 on — dependence and congratulation
depend on the traffic
rely on your friend
live on a small salary
congratulate somebody on passing
compliment somebody on their cooking
:::

And a small extra: **depend** can take **how / whether / when** with or without **on** — _It depends **how much** it is_ or _It depends **on how much** it is._

## Rewriting with a different verb

::: compare
# 💔 the plain meaning
- ==I apologised because the mistake was mine==.
- The plain verb becomes: ==I apologised **for** the mistake==.
# 🎉 good news
- ==I congratulated her because she had won==.
- ==I congratulated her **on** winning==.
:::

::: summary
- **of**: accuse, suspect, approve, die, consist.
- **for**: pay (a cost), thank, forgive, apologise, blame a person.
- **from**: suffer, recover, protect. **die of** or **die from**, never die from a person.
- **on**: depend, rely, live (on money), congratulate, compliment.
:::
