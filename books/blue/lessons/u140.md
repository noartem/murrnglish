---
hook: 🔌 Put the kettle on, take your coat off, see her off. Four phrasal verbs, and three of them are about leaving.
goals: use on and off with lights, machines and clothes · delay or cancel an event with put off and call off · say off with journeys and goodbyes
---
::: scene ✈️ The early flight
A family gets ready and leaves.
> Mum: Leave the hall light on until we're in the car.
> Dad: I'd rather switch it off. We always forget.
> Son: Can I put my trainers on? Mine are soaked.
> Mum: Take your cardigan off, then. It's boiling in here.
> Dad: We'd better set off now, or we'll miss the train.
> Mum: And don't put off packing again.
:::

## on and off for lights and machines

::: compare
# 💡 on = working, running
- The light ==is on==.
- Let me ==put the kettle on==. ==(we need boiling water)==
- Let's ==put some music on==.
# 🔘 off = not working
- 'Is the heating on?' 'No, I ==switched it off==.'
- Want me to ==leave the lights on==, or ==switch them off==?
:::

## on and off for events

::: compare
# 📰 go on — happen
- What's all that noise? What's ==going on==?
# ❌ call off — cancel
- The concert ==was called off== because of the weather.
# ⏳ put off — delay
- The election ==has been put off== until the spring.
- We can't ==put off making a decision== any longer.
:::

==== A noun takes **call off**; an action takes **put off**: _call off the match_, _put off the meeting_, _put off leaving._

## on and off for clothes

::: compare
# 👕 put on — get dressed in
- My fingers were numb, so I ==put my gloves on==.
- Put your ==seat belt on== before you move.
- I've ==put on two kilos== in a month. ==(weight, not clothes)==
# 🪞 try on — test the fit
- I ==tried a coat on== in the shop; the sleeves were too long.
# 🧥 take off — remove
- It was boiling in there, so I ==took off my coat==.
:::

## off = away, leaving

::: compare
# 🧳 be off to — on the way to
- Tomorrow I'm ==off to Berlin==. ==I'm off on holiday next week==.
# 🚴 walk off, run off, ride off
- Bea got on her bike and ==rode off== without a word.
# 🚗 set off — start the journey
- We ==set off early== to beat the traffic.
# ✈️ take off — a plane leaves the ground
- After a delay the plane finally ==took off==.
# 👋 see somebody off — say goodbye at the airport or station
- We went to the station with Noor to ==see her off==.
:::

::: trap
✗ She put her gloves ==off== before she went out.
✓ She put her gloves ==on== before she went out.
✗ I want to ==put off two kilos== before June.
✓ I have ==put on two kilos== since January.
✗ The meeting was ==called off== until January.
✓ The meeting was ==put off== until January.
✗ The plane ==took on== from the runway at last.
✓ The plane ==took off== from the runway at last.
✗ We went to the terminal to ==see off her==.
✓ We went to the terminal to ==see her off==.
✗ She put her hat ==on== and left. — both positions work for a noun
✗ Let me ==put on the oven==; I'm going to bake.
✗ The concert was ==put off== by the rain.
✓ The concert was ==called off== by the rain.
:::
