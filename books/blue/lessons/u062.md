::: scene 🕵️ A car outside a warehouse
A car has been sitting outside a warehouse since dawn. The police have stopped it.
> Officer: Show me your licence. We stopped the car because we ==suspect you of taking== things from here.
> Driver: ==I insist on speaking== to my solicitor.
> Officer: Fine. But we may ==accuse you of breaking in== if you don't.
> Driver: Nobody can ==accuse me of stealing== anything. I was ==looking for== a garage.
> Officer: Then you'll ==apologise to me for wasting== our time.
> Driver: I'll ==apologise for stopping== you. I'll ==thank you for not== holding me.
:::

## Some verbs bring their own preposition
These verbs are never followed directly by an **-ing** phrase. A **preposition** always comes
first, and it belongs to the verb — you cannot swap it for another one.

::: form
# 🔗 verb + preposition + -ing
insist **on** | she insisted on leaving
apologise **to somebody for** | he apologised to Jo for being rude
accuse somebody **of** | they accused him of lying
suspect somebody **of** | the police suspected the driver of stealing
congratulate somebody **on** | she congratulated him on passing
thank somebody **for** | I thanked her for coming
prevent somebody **from** | the fog prevented them from leaving
:::

::: examples
- She ==insisted on paying== for the meal.
- Dan ==insisted on telling== me anyway.
- We ==apologised to Sarah for being== so rude.
- Jen ==thanked Sue for coming== to see her.
- He ==congratulated me on getting== the job.
- Bad weather ==prevented the plane from taking off==.
:::

::: note 🚫
Say _accused him **of** lying_, never _accused him lying_. The preposition is part of the verb,
as fixed as its ending.
:::

## A person in the middle
Half of these also take **a person** between the verb and the preposition. The pattern is regular
once you see it in two columns.

::: form
# 👤 no person in the middle
apologise **for** doing | I apologised for knocking.
thank **for** doing | Thanks for helping.
congratulate **on** doing | I congratulated them on winning.
# 👥 a person goes in
apologise **to** somebody **for** doing | I apologised to Ana for knocking.
thank somebody **for** doing | I thanked Ana for helping.
congratulate somebody **on** doing | I congratulated them on winning.
:::

::: cards
# 🙏 apologise
_She apologised **to me for** being late._
# 📣 accuse
_They accused **him of** cheating._
# 🔍 suspect
_The police suspected **the driver of** stealing it._
# 🎉 congratulate
_He congratulated **her on** the news._
# 🛑 prevent
_The police prevented **him from** entering._
:::

## What you feel, plan and avoid
A third family works the same way, and the preposition is just as fixed.

::: words
feel like · think of · dream of · look forward to · object to · be used to · decide against · be fed up with · succeed in
:::

::: examples
- I feel lazy. I don't feel like ==doing== any work.
- I've ==dreamed of having== a small house near the sea.
- I'm ==looking forward to seeing== them again.
- She ==objected to being== interrupted.
- We can't afford a car, so we've ==decided against buying== one.
- It took us months, but we ==succeeded in solving== it.
- Nobody can ==accuse me of not trying==.
:::

::: note 🔍
**insist on / object to / look forward to / be used to / succeed in** all look like phrasal
verbs with the *-ing* attached, and they behave like them: the **-ing** is compulsory, and the
preposition never takes a different form.
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~They accused him from lying.~~
✓ They accused him ==of lying==.
✗ ~~She insisted on tell me.~~
✓ She insisted on ==telling== me.
✗ ~~I apologised for late.~~
✓ I apologised ==to Ana for being== late.
✗ ~~The rain prevented to play.~~
✓ The rain prevented us ==from playing==.
✗ ~~He accused of stealing it.~~
✓ He accused ==the driver of stealing== it.
✗ ~~I'm looking forward to see you.~~
✓ I'm looking forward to ==seeing== you.
The preposition comes first, **-ing** comes second, and the person (if the verb wants one)
stands in the middle.
:::

::: cards
# 🔒 insist on
_He insisted **on opening** the window._
# 🙋 apologise to … for
_She apologised **to me for** forgetting._
# 🛑 prevent … from
_They prevented him **from leaving**._
# 🤝 succeed in
_We succeeded **in fixing** it ourselves._
:::

::: quiz

:::

::: summary
- Fixed pairs: insist **on**, accuse somebody **of**, suspect somebody **of**, congratulate
  somebody **on**, thank somebody **for**, apologise **to** somebody **for**, prevent somebody
  **from**.
- Some take a **person** in the middle: accuse **him of**, thank **her for**.
- **feel like, think of, dream of, look forward to, object to, succeed in, decide against** take
  **-ing**.
- The preposition is compulsory and never changes.
:::
