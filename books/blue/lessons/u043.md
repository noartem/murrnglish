::: scene 🚲 The bike shop
Arun brings his bike in for repair.
> Arun: Can it ==be fixed== by Friday?
> Mechanic: It ==should be done== by Thursday. Come back then.
**Thursday.**
> Arun: Is it ready?
> Mechanic: Not yet — it=='s being painted== now. The brakes ==have been replaced==, though.
:::

## Be done after can, must, will, to…
After **will, can, must, should, might, may, have to, going to, want to…**, the passive is **be + past participle**.

:::: row
::: compare
# 🙋 active
- We must fix the roof.
- Someone will meet you.
- They're going to close the pool.
# 📦 passive
- The roof ==must be fixed==.
- You=='ll be met==.
- The pool ==is going to be closed==.
:::
::: note 🎟️
Also after _would like to, need to, want to_: _I'd like ==to be invited==. Nobody wants ==to be laughed== at._
:::
::::

## Have been done after modals
For the past, use **modal + have been + past participle**.

::: cards
# 📩 might have been
The parcel ==might have been left== with a neighbour.
# 🧹 should have been
This floor ==should have been cleaned== yesterday.
# 🏆 would have been
If you'd entered, you ==would have been chosen==.
:::

## Present perfect and past perfect passive
::: form
# ✅ has / have been + done
The tickets ==have been sold==.
My phone ==has been found==!
# ⏪ had been + done
When we arrived, the food ==had been eaten==.
The room ==had been tidied==.
:::

::: trap
✗ My bag ~~has stolen~~!
✓ My bag has been stolen!
Perfect passive needs both **been** and the past participle.
:::

## Being done: in progress
Continuous passive: **is / are / was / were being + past participle**.

:::: row
::: timeline
span 30 75 | the bridge is being repaired
point now | now
:::
::: examples
- The bridge ==is being repaired==. You can't cross.
- I think we=='re being watched==.
- When I called, the patient ==was being examined==.
- Our street ==was being dug up== all summer.
:::
::::

::: compare
# 🔨 being done — still in progress
- My car ==is being serviced==. I'll get it at five.
# ✅ been done — finished
- My car ==has been serviced==. It's ready.
:::

::: quiz
? The results ___ (will / announce) tomorrow.
= will be announced
? I can't find my keys. They ___ (might / take) by mistake.
= might have been taken
? Sorry about the noise. The house next door ___ (build) at the moment.
= is being built
? Somebody has eaten my sandwich. → My sandwich ___.
= has been eaten
:::

::: summary
- Modal / _to_ + **be done**: _must be done, to be invited_.
- Modal + **have been done** for the past.
- **has / have / had been done** = perfect passive.
- **is / was being done** = continuous passive.
:::
