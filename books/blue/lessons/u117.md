::: scene 🎭 Two jobs
One person describes a colleague — and gets the two words muddled.
Nadia doesn't cook. She's hopeless in the kitchen.
Nadia works ==as a nurse==. She's always on time.
Like her mother, she has a very good voice.
:::

## like = similar to

**like** compares two things by what they look or feel like. Nothing is being said about a job or a role.

::: compare
# 🟰 like = similar
- That house is lovely; it is ==like a palace==.
- My feet are freezing; they're ==like blocks of ice==.
- She swims ==like a fish==.

# 🧰 as = the job or the role
- Marion had a part-time job ==as a tour guide==.
- Hannah has been working ==as a waitress== for two months.
- She came ==as my representative==.
:::

::: note 🎭
The quickest test: can you put **the same as** after it? _like a palace_ → _a house **the same as** a palace_. _as a tour guide_ → no.

:::

## as = the same as, such as, as usual

A third family of **as** has nothing to do with likeness.

::: compare
# 🟰 the same as
- David is ==the same age as== James.
- I got there ==at the same moment as== you.

# 🧺 such as = for example
- In parts of Asia, ==such as== Japan and Indonesia, traffic drives on the left.

# 📅 as usual / as before
- I'll phone tomorrow ==as usual==.
- Everything is the same ==as before==.
:::

## The fixed phrases

Some **as** phrases cannot be changed, only memorised.

::: words
as usual · as before · as always · as ever · as well as · as soon as · as long as · as far as I know
:::

## Two easy mistakes

:::: row
::: trap
✗ As her aunt, Bridget has a lovely singing voice.
✓ ==Like== her aunt, Bridget has a lovely singing voice.
✗ I can't stand people as him.
✓ I can't stand people ==like him==.
✗ She works as a teacher as her sister.
✓ She works ==like a teacher==; her sister works ==as a one==.
:::

::: note ✅
**as** works where **like** would not: _You can do as you like_, _as far as I know_, _the same as_.

:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? This tea is awful. It tastes ___ water.
= like
? He is 22, but sometimes he behaves ___ a child.
= like
? She has worked ___ a nurse since 2019.
= as
? My brother gave me this watch ___ a birthday present.
= as
? In some countries, ___ Thailand, people eat with chopsticks.
= such as
? This bag is heavy ___ a suitcase full of books.
= like
? He's noisy, ___ always.
= as
? We did the same thing ___ you suggested.
= as
? He looks ___ his father.
= like
:::

::: summary
- **like** = similar to: it is like a palace, it tastes like water.
- **as** = the job or role: she works as a nurse.
- **as** also = the same as, such as, as usual — fixed phrases, learn them whole.
- When in doubt, try **the same as**: it only fits **like**.
:::
