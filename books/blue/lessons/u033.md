::: scene 🎻 Before the concert
Felipe has a violin solo tonight. He's backstage with his teacher, Ms Okafor.
> Felipe: My hands are cold. What ==should== I do?
> Ms Okafor: You ==should== hold a cup of tea. And you ==shouldn't== look at the audience.
> Felipe: I ==should have practised== more last week.
> Ms Okafor: Relax. It ==should== go fine — you know it by heart.
:::

## Should: it's a good idea
**should / shouldn't + verb** = it's the right thing, a good idea; it's advice or an opinion.

:::: row
::: rule 👍 advice
You look pale. You ==should== see a doctor.

He ==shouldn't== drink so much coffee.

What ==should== I wear to the interview?
:::
::: words
I think … should · I don't think … should · Do you think … should? · should probably
:::
::::

::: cards
# 📱 too much screen
You ==shouldn't== play games until 2 a.m.
# 💸 a low salary
You ==should== ask for a pay rise.
# 🌄 a great view
We ==should== come back here at sunset.
:::

::: trap
✗ I think you ~~shouldn't~~ to go.
✓ I don't think you should go.
No **to** after _should_. And for a negative opinion, put _not_ in _I don't think_.
:::

## Should: what you expect
**should** also says that something is likely or expected, or that it's not as it ought to be.

::: examples
- Oscar left an hour ago. He ==should be== here soon. // I expect so
- I've charged it all night, so the battery ==should last== all day.
- The road is new. The trip ==shouldn't take== long.
- It's March. It ==should be== warmer than this! // it's not right
:::

## Should have done: right, but it didn't happen
**should have + past participle** = it was the right thing, but it didn't happen. **shouldn't have done** = it happened, but it was wrong.

::: timeline
point 25 | Felipe didn't practise
point now | now he's nervous
:::

::: compare
# 😔 should have done
- I ==should have set== an alarm. I overslept.
- You ==should have told== me. I'd have helped.
# 🙈 shouldn't have done
- I ==shouldn't have eaten== that third burger.
- We ==shouldn't have left== so late.
:::

::: note ⏰
For things that ought to be happening **now**: _The shop opens at nine. It's half past. It ==should be== open by now._ For an action in progress: _You ==shouldn't be driving== so fast._
:::

::: quiz
? You've got a temperature. You ___ go to school today.
= shouldn't
? I posted it yesterday, so it ___ arrive tomorrow.
= should
? We missed the train. We ___ (leave) earlier.
= should have left
? I feel sick. I ___ (not / eat) all those sweets.
= shouldn't have eaten
:::

::: summary
- **should / shouldn't + verb** = advice, what's right.
- **should** = what we expect: _He should be here soon._
- **should have done** = right but didn't happen; **shouldn't have done** = happened but wrong.
- No _to_ after _should_.
:::
