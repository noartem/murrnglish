## A finished time
The **past simple** is for things that happened and are over, at a time that's also over. Often the time is named — _yesterday_, _last week_, _in 2019_, _two days ago_ — or clear from the story.

::: timeline
point past | Priya drove up
point past+10 | climbed to the lake
point past+20 | Sam jumped in
:::

## Regular and irregular
:::: row
::: rule ✅ Regular verbs
Add **-ed**: work → work==ed==, play → play==ed==.

After e, just **-d**: live → liv==ed==. Consonant + y → **-ied**: study → stud==ied==. Short vowel + one consonant → double it: stop → sto==pp==ed.
:::
::: words 🔀 Irregular: learn them by heart
buy → bought · catch → caught · teach → taught · sell → sold · fall → fell · throw → threw · spend → spent · cost → cost · hurt → hurt · write → wrote
:::
::::

::: note 🃏
There are around a hundred common irregular verbs. The _irregular verbs_ deck in Cards is made for exactly this: a few minutes a day and they stick.
:::

## Questions and negatives: did
One helper for every person: **did**. After it, the verb goes back to its base form.

::: form
# ➕
I / you / he / she / we / they | ==went==
# ➖
I / you / he / she / we / they | ==didn't== go
# ❓
==did== | I / you / he / she / we / they | go?
:::

::: examples
- ==Did== you ==sleep== well? — Yes, I ==did==.
- Where ==did== they ==stay==?
- We ==didn't have== time to call you.
- How much ==did== it ==cost==? — It ==didn't cost== much.
:::

::: trap
✗ Did you ~~went~~ out last night?
✓ Did you go out last night?
✗ She ~~didn't told~~ me.
✓ She didn't tell me.
:::

## Was and were
_Be_ has its own past, and it doesn't use _did_.

:::: row
::: form
# ➕ / ➖
I / he / she / it | ==was== / ==wasn't==
we / you / they | ==were== / ==weren't==
:::
::: examples
- The hotel ==wasn't== very clean.
- ==Were== you tired after the trip?
- The bags ==were== really heavy.
:::
::::

::: quiz
? I ___ (lose) my phone yesterday.
= lost
? ___ (you / see) the match last night?
= Did you see
? We ___ (not / enjoy) the film. It was too long.
= didn't enjoy
? The weather ___ (be) terrible, so we stayed in.
= was
:::

::: summary
- Past simple = finished action, finished time.
- Regular **-ed**; irregular forms are learnt one by one.
- **did / didn't** + base verb for every person.
- **was / were** for the past of _be_ — no _did_.
:::
