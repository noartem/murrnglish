::: scene 🏡 House rules
Priya's new landlord, Mr Varga, shows her round.
> Mr Varga: I ==insist that== tenants ==keep== the hallway clear.
> Priya: Of course. What do you ==suggest I do== with my bike?
> Mr Varga: I ==suggest you leave== it in the yard. And ==should== you ==have== any problems, call me.

## Suggest, insist, demand… that somebody (should) do
After verbs like these, you can use **should + verb**, or the **plain base verb** (the same for every person).

::: words
suggest · propose · recommend · insist · demand · ask · advise
:::

:::: row
::: form
# 🧩 that + subject + (should) + verb
The coach suggested | that we ==(should) train== twice a week.
They insisted | that she ==(should) stay== for dinner.
The doctor recommended | that he ==(should) rest==.
:::
::: note ✍️
With the plain verb there's no _-s_ and no past: _She insisted that he ==stay==_ (not _stays / stayed_). In British English _stays / stayed_ is also heard.
:::
::::

::: compare
# 📌 the same after nouns and adjectives
- It's ==important that== everyone ==(should) vote==.
- It's ==essential that== the bridge ==(should) be repaired==.
# ✨ even for the past
- I proposed that we ==(should) meet== on Friday.
- They demanded that the rent ==(should) be paid== at once.
:::

## Suggest: the right pattern
**suggest** never takes _somebody to do_.

::: trap
✗ What do you suggest ~~me to wear~~?
✓ What do you suggest I wear? / What do you suggest I should wear?
✗ Leo suggested ~~me to call~~ the bank.
✓ Leo suggested that I (should) call the bank. / Leo suggested calling the bank.
:::

## It's strange that… should
After words like **strange, funny, typical, natural, surprised**, **should** can stress your reaction to something.

::: cards
# 😮 surprised
I was amazed that she ==should ask== me for help.
# 😄 funny
It's funny that you ==should mention== Rome. I'm going next week!
# 🙄 typical
It's typical of Sid that he ==should arrive== an hour late.
:::

## If … should / Should …
**If … should** makes a possibility sound less likely, more "just in case". You can also start with **Should** and drop _if_.

::: examples
- ==If== anyone ==should call==, tell them I'm out.
- ==Should== anyone ==call==, tell them I'm out. // the same, more formal
- ==If== there ==should be== a delay, we'll text you.
- ==Should== you ==need== help, just ask.
:::

## I should… = my advice
**I should do** can mean _if I were you, I would do_: it's advice.

::: quiz
? "Shall I take a coat?" "Yes, I ___ (take) one. It's cold."
= should take
? The manager insisted that we ___ (finish) by five.
= (should) finish
? It's strange that he ___ (say) that — he never talks about money.
= should say
? ___ the train ___ (be) late, take a taxi.
= Should … be
:::

::: summary
- **suggest / insist / demand… that + (should) + verb**; plain verb, no _-s_.
- Not _suggest somebody to do_.
- **It's strange / natural / important that … should …**
- **If … should …** or **Should …,** for less likely events.
- **I should…** = my advice to you.
:::
