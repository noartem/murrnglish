Grandpa Leo shows his granddaughter Ivy an album.
> Leo: That's me! I ==used to have== long hair and a guitar.
> Ivy: Did you ==use to== play in a band?
> Leo: Every weekend. And there ==used to be== a dance hall on this street.
> Ivy: Now it's a car park.

## Used to: then, not now
**used to + verb** is for things that happened regularly, or were true, in the past and have **stopped**.

:::: row
::: timeline
repeat 10 60 | Leo played in a band
point now | not any more
:::
::: rule 📼
**used to** + base verb, the same for every person.

I / she / they ==used to== play.

The habit or situation is over now.
:::
::::

::: cards
# 🔁 habits
I ==used to== cycle to school every day.
# 🏠 states
We ==used to== live above a bakery.
# 🏛️ places
There ==used to be== a cinema here.
# 💪 abilities
I ==used to be able== to do the splits!
:::

::: note 📌
_Used to_ only talks about the past. For the present, use the present simple: _I usually walk to work_, not _I use to walk_.
:::

## Did you use to…? I didn't use to…
In questions and negatives, **did** carries the past, so the verb loses its **-d**: **use to**.

::: form
# ➕
I / he / we… | ==used to== | swim
# ➖
I / he / we… | ==didn't use to== | swim
# ❓
==did== | you / she… | ==use to== swim?
:::

::: trap
✗ Did you ~~used to~~ have a pet?
✓ Did you use to have a pet?
✗ I ~~didn't used to~~ like olives.
✓ I didn't use to like olives.
:::

::: examples
- What music ==did you use to== listen to as a teenager?
- Mo ==never used to== drink coffee, but now he has three cups a day.
- I don't go to concerts any more, but I ==used to==. // the verb can be left out
:::

## Then and now
Put the old habit next to the new one with **but**.

::: compare
# 📼 then
- She ==used to be== shy,
- I ==didn't use to== cook,
- They ==used to have== a cat,
# 📱 now
- but now she's a TV presenter.
- but now I make bread every week.
- but it ran away last year.
:::

::: quiz
? Pablo ___ (live) in Madrid, but he moved to Lima.
= used to live
? ___ you ___ (play) any sports at school?
= Did … use to play
? I ___ (not / like) spicy food, but I love it now.
= didn't use to like
? There ___ (be) a lake here before they built the houses.
= used to be
:::

::: summary
- **used to + verb** = a past habit or state that has stopped.
- Questions and negatives: **did … use to**, **didn't use to** (no -d).
- Also **never used to**, **used to be able to**.
- No _used to_ for the present: say _I usually…_
:::
