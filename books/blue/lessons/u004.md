::: scene 🍰 At the bakery counter
> Customer: I ==want== a slice of that lemon cake, please. It ==looks== amazing.
> Baker: Good choice. Do you ==know== what's in it?
> Customer: No, but I ==don't need== to know. I ==believe== you.
Nobody is _doing_ wanting, knowing or believing. These verbs describe a state of mind, not an activity, so they stay in the present simple even about right now.
:::

## Verbs that stay simple
::: rule
With these verbs, use the present simple even when you mean _now_.

I ==need== a break. (not ~~I'm needing~~)
:::

::: cards
# 💭 Mind
know · believe · understand · remember · forget · realise · mean · suppose
# ❤️ Feelings
like · love · hate · want · need · prefer
# 📦 Having and being
belong to · own · contain · consist of · depend on · seem
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~I'm not understanding~~ this question.
✓ I don't understand this question.
✗ Who ~~is this bag belonging~~ to?
✓ Who does this bag belong to?
:::

## Two meanings, two forms
A few verbs have one meaning that is a state and another that is an action. The state takes the simple form; the action can be continuous.

::: compare
# 💭 State → simple
- I ==think== you're right. (= my opinion)
- This soup ==tastes== of garlic.
- I ==see== what you mean. (= understand)
- They ==have== a flat by the river. (= own)
# 🎬 Action → continuous possible
- I =='m thinking== about changing jobs. (= considering)
- The chef ==is tasting== the soup.
- I =='m seeing== the dentist tomorrow. (= meeting)
- We =='re having== lunch. (= eating)
:::

:::: row
::: note 👀 See, hear, smell
We usually add _can_: ==I can hear== music next door. Can you smell something burning?
:::
::: note 🤒 Feel
With _feel_ both forms are fine: How ==do you feel== today? / How ==are you feeling== today?
:::
::::

## He's nice, or he's being nice?
_Am/is/are being_ means **behaving** a certain way right now — often not how the person usually is.

::: examples
- Jake ==is== very kind. (that's his character)
- Jake ==is being== very kind today. (he's acting kindly — and it might not last)
- Why ==are you being== so quiet? Is something wrong?
- The kids ==are being== silly because they're tired.
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~Are you being~~ hungry?
✓ Are you hungry?
Not every adjective fits: _being_ works for behaviour (rude, careful, patient, silly), not for states like hungry, tired or ill.
:::

::: quiz
? Sorry, I ___ (not / remember) your name.
= don't remember
? What ___ (you / think) of my new haircut?
= do you think
? I ___ (think) of selling my bike. Do you want it?
= 'm thinking
? Why ___ (you / be) so rude to him today?
= are you being
:::

::: summary
- State verbs (know, want, like, need, belong…) stay in the **present simple**.
- think, have, see, taste: state = simple, action = continuous.
- feel works both ways.
- **is being** + adjective = behaving like that right now.
:::
