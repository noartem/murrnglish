## Same subject, same object

When the one acting is also the one affected, use a **reflexive pronoun**.

::: form
# 🙋 singular -self
myself · yourself · himself · herself · itself

# 👥 plural -selves
ourselves · yourselves · themselves
:::

::: trap
✗ Please don't pay for me. I'll pay for ~~me~~.
✓ Please don't pay for me. I'll pay for ==myself==.
✗ Rosa had a wonderful week. She really enjoyed ~~her~~.
✓ Rosa had a wonderful week. She really enjoyed ==herself==.
:::

## Where to leave it out

After **feel, relax, concentrate, meet** — and usually after **wash, shave, dress**:

::: compare
# ❌ no reflexive
- I'm finding it hard to relax.
- Please concentrate now.
- What time shall we meet outside?
- He got up, washed, shaved and dressed.
# ✅ reflexive
- She struggled to express ==herself== clearly.
- You have to put ==yourself== in my position.
- They enjoyed ==themselves== enormously.
:::

## themselves or each other?

::: compare
# 🪞 themselves — each does it to themselves
Two dancers faced the mirrors and checked ==themselves==.

# 🔄 each other — they do it to one another
One looked at the other, and the other looked back. They watched ==each other==.
:::

**one another** is the long form of **each other**: _Do the flats adjoin each other? / adjoin one another?_

## Reflexive for emphasis

Use it to underline that **you** did it, not somebody else.

::: examples
- 'Who mended your bike?' '==I mended it myself==.'
- Let's repaint the flat ==ourselves== — it'll cost nothing.
- The bread itself was fine; it was the butter I disliked.
- Hanne doesn't think so ==herself==.
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? The band had a great evening. They really enjoyed ___.
= themselves
? We couldn't get in: we'd locked ___ out.
= ourselves
? Beatriz and I don't meet ___ very often now.
= each other
? 'Who fixed the car for you?' 'Nobody. I fixed it ___.'
= myself
? May I take another biscuit? 'Of course. Help ___!'
= yourself
:::

::: summary
- Reflexive pronouns when subject and object are one person: _She enjoyed herself_.
- None after feel, relax, concentrate, meet, wash, shave, dress.
- **themselves** = each does it to themselves; **each other** = they do it to each other.
- Reflexive for emphasis: _I did it myself_, _Let's do it ourselves_.
:::
