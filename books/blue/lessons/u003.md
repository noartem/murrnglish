::: scene 🎻 Two photos of Marco
> Marco: I ==play== the violin in an orchestra. That's my job.
> Marco: But right now I =='m not playing== anything — I =='m eating== an ice cream on the beach.
The first sentence is about Marco's life. The second is about this afternoon.
:::

## Now, or in general?
:::: row
::: rule ⏳ Present continuous
Something in progress **now** or **around now**. It has started and it will stop.

The kettle ==is boiling==. Can you turn it off?
:::
::: rule ♾️ Present simple
Something true **in general**, or something that happens **again and again**.

Water ==boils== at 100 degrees.
:::
::: timeline
span now-8 now+8 | I'm reading (now)
repeat past future | I read every evening
:::

::: compare
# ⏳ I am doing
- ==Are you listening== to me? You look far away.
- Look! The river ==is flowing== really fast today.
- Somebody ==is knocking== at the door.
# ♾️ I do
- ==Do you listen== to podcasts on your way to work?
- The river ==flows== into the lake.
- He ==knocks== twice before he comes in.
:::
::::

## For now, or for good?
The same pair splits another way: a situation that's only **temporary** against one that is **permanent**.

::: cards
# 🏨 For now
Tomás ==is staying== with his cousin until he finds a flat.
# 🏠 For good
Tomás ==lives== in Valencia. He was born there.
# 🧑‍🍳 For now
I =='m working== in a café this summer.
# 💼 For good
I ==work== for a bank.
:::

::: note
A question about someone's job asks about their life, so it's present simple: ==What do you do?== — I'm a dentist. But ==What are you doing?== asks what's going on right now.
:::

## Always + -ing: "too often!"
_Always_ usually goes with the present simple — _He always starts on time_. But _always_ with the continuous means **too often**, and it tends to sound annoyed or surprised.

::: scene 🙄
> Anna: You =='re always losing== your keys!
> Ben: And you =='re always reminding== me about it.
:::

::: compare
# 😊 Normal habit
- She always leaves the office at six.
# 😤 Happens too much
- She's always leaving her umbrella on the bus.
:::

## Words that make it now
A few everyday phrases only make sense as things happening right now, so they need the continuous.

::: trap
✗ Wait for me, ~~I come~~!
✓ Wait for me, I'm coming!
✗ Let's go home, ~~it gets~~ late.
✓ Let's go home, it's getting late.
✗ How often ~~are you going~~ to the gym?
✓ How often do you go to the gym?
:::

::: quiz
? Look, it ___ (snow)!
= 's snowing
? It ___ (not / snow) much in this part of the country.
= doesn't snow
? My sister ___ (stay) with us this week.
= is staying
? He ___ (always / interrupt) me — it drives me mad!
= 's always interrupting
:::

::: summary
- Now / around now → **am/is/are + -ing**. In general / repeatedly → **present simple**.
- Temporary: I'm living with friends. Permanent: I live in Rome.
- **always + -ing** = too often, often with a complaint.
- "I'm coming", "It's getting late" — happening now, so continuous.
:::
