hook: 👋 She waved to him and he waved back. Away takes you out of the picture; back puts you where you started.
goals: use away and back as opposite directions · say get away, get away with, keep away and put away · use wave back, call back, get back to, look back and pay back

## away and back

**Away** moves from a person or place; **back** returns to it.

::: compare
# ➡️ away — off
- We're going ==away on holiday== today.
- She started the engine and ==drove away==.
- The bird flew ==away== before I got the camera out.
- My cap came off and ==blew away== before I could grab it.
- They searched the flat and ==took away a laptop==.
# ⬅️ back — to the start
- We'll be ==back in three weeks==.
- 'When will you be ==back==?' 'About eleven.'
- After the restaurant we ==walked back== to the hotel.
- I still have her keys; I forgot to ==give them back==.
- Put the book ==back on the shelf== when you've finished.
:::

==== With the same verbs: **throw away** into the bin, **throw back** to somebody; **give away** to somebody else, **give back** to the owner.

## Five verbs + away

::: compare
# 🏃 get away — escape
- We tried to catch the thief, but he ==got away==.
# 🤫 get away with — do it without being caught
- I parked in a no-parking zone and ==got away with it==.
# ⚠️ keep away from — do not go near
- ==Keep away from== the cliff edge; the wind is strong.
# 🎁 give away — give it to somebody else
- 'Did you sell the bicycle?' 'No, I ==gave it away to a neighbour==.'
# 📦 put away — put it where it belongs
- When the children finished, they ==put the toys away==.
# 🗑️ throw away — put it in the bin
- I kept the note itself, but the wrapper went in the bin.
:::

## Five verbs + back

::: compare
# 👋 wave back — return the greeting
- I ==waved to her== and she ==waved back==.
# 📞 call back — return a phone call
- 'I can't talk now.' 'I'll ==call you back== in ten minutes.'
# ↩️ get back to — reply by message
- I emailed him, but he never ==got back to me==.
# 🔙 look back on — look at the past
- ==Looking back on it==, that job taught me a lot.
# 💷 pay back — return money
- Thanks for the loan; I'll ==pay you back== next week.
# ⚽ hit back — return a blow
- He hit me and I ==hit him back==.
:::

==== **Throw back** means return it: _throw the ball back to me._ **Throw away** means the bin: _throw the wrapper away._

::: trap
✗ I still have her keys; I forgot to ==give them away==.
✓ I still have her keys; I forgot to ==give them back==.
✗ I filed the note, but returned the envelope.
✓ I filed the note, but binned the envelope.
✗ I waved to her and she ==waved away==.
✓ I waved to her and she ==waved back==.
✗ Please ==give back it== to its owner.
✓ Please ==give it back== to its owner.
✗ I couldn't hear you; say it ==again back==.
✓ I couldn't hear you; say it ==again==.
:::

::: summary
- **away** = off from a person or place; **back** = to where you started: _drove away / walked back._
- With **away**: _get away, get away with, keep away from, give away, put away, throw away._
- With **back**: _wave back, call back, get back to, look back on, pay back, throw back, hit back._
- The pair that catches people: **give away** to somebody else, **give back** to the owner.
