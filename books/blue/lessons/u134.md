## hear: about, of, from

Three different questions, three prepositions.

::: compare
# 📰 hear about — you have been told
- Did you hear ==about the crash== on the ring road?
# 👤 hear of — the thing exists
- I've never heard ==of that singer==. ==(not heard from him)==
# ✉️ hear from — you are in contact
- Have you heard ==from Jane== recently?
:::

==== **Hear of** is for names and things. **Hear from** is for people writing or speaking.

## think about and think of

**think about** = consider, turn over in your mind. **think of** = produce an idea.

::: compare
# 🤔 think about — consider
- I've thought about ==what you said==, and I'll take your advice.
- I'll ==think about it== and let you know. ==(not think of it)==
# 💡 think of — an idea arrives
- The name completely escapes me: I can't ==think of it==.
- That line was mine; I ==thought of it== on the bus.
:::

==== The two often swap for a future action: _I'm **thinking of** going to Canada_ = _**thinking about** going._

When you ask for an opinion, use **think of**: _What did you **think of** the film?_ ==I didn't **rate it** much.==

## dream about and dream of

::: compare
# 😴 dream about — asleep
- I dreamed about you last night.
# 🌟 dream of — imagine
- Do you dream ==of being== famous?
- or ==dream about being== famous.
# 🙅 dream of doing — would never do it
- 'Don't tell anybody what I said.' 'No, ==I wouldn't dream of it==.'
:::

## complain about and complain of

**complain about** = say you are not satisfied. **complain of** = report a pain or an illness.

::: compare
# 😤 complain about — dissatisfaction
- We complained ==to the manager about the food==.
# 🤕 complain of — a symptom
- George was complaining ==of a headache== all evening.
:::

## remind someone of and about

**remind somebody about** = tell them not to forget. **remind somebody of** = make them remember something past.

::: compare
# 📌 remind about — don't forget
- Thanks for reminding me ==about the meeting==. I'd forgotten.
# 🕰️ remind of — it brings back a memory
- That bakery reminds me ==of the one on my street==.
- Who does he remind you ==of==?
:::

## Trap

::: trap
✗ I've never heard ==from== that writer.
✓ I've never heard ==of== that writer.
✗ I'll think about it and let you know. — this is right
✗ I couldn't think about anything to say.
✓ I couldn't think ==of anything== to say.
✗ George was complaining about a headache.
✓ George was complaining ==of a headache==.
✗ She complained to us about the noise. — this is right
✗ This song reminds me about my school.
✓ This song reminds me ==of my school==.
✗ Thanks for reminding me of the dentist.
✓ Thanks for reminding me ==about the dentist==.
✗ He never heard of that band.
✓ He never heard ==about that band==.
✗ What did you think about the new film?
✓ What did you think ==of== the new film?
:::

::: summary
- **hear about** = you were told; **hear of** = the thing exists; **hear from** = you are in contact.
- **think about** = consider it; **think of** = produce an idea, or give an opinion.
- **complain about** = dissatisfaction; **complain of** = a pain or an illness.
- **remind somebody about** = don't forget; **remind somebody of** = it brings back a memory.
:::
