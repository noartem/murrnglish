> Receptionist: Yes. Anything else?

## One word, two jobs

Some nouns count in one meaning and don't count in another. The meaning decides.

::: compare
# 🔢 countable — one particular thing
- I heard ==a noise== from the kitchen.
- There's ==a hair== in my soup!
- We had ==a great time==.
- Could I have ==a light==, please? (one lamp)
# 🌫️ uncountable — the stuff or the idea in general
- There's too much ==noise== in here.
- Your ==hair== is very long. (all of it)
- Have ==a good time==! (the general wish)
- ==Light== comes from the sun.
:::

## The ones that never take a plural

These have no plural at all — not with **-s**, not with **of**.

::: form
# 📋 always uncountable
advice · bread · furniture · luggage · progress
behaviour · damage · information · news · permission
chaos · luck · scenery · traffic · weather
:::

::: trap
✗ I've had a lot of ==experiences== in that job.
✓ I've had ==a lot of experience== in that job.
✗ Could you send me more ==informations== about the trip?
✓ Could you send me more ==information== about the trip?
✗ We had a lot of ==baggages==.
✓ We had a lot of ==luggage==.
:::

## Drinks are a small exception

With **coffee, tea, juice, beer** you may count the cups: _two coffees_, please.

But the drink itself stays uncountable: _I don't like coffee._

::: cards
# ☕ two coffees
We’ll have ==two coffees==, please. (two cups)
# 🥛 the drink itself
I don’t drink much ==coffee==. (coffee in general)
:::

## Asking for what you want

::: examples
- Excuse me, can I ask you for ==some advice==?
- I'd like ==an hour of your time==, if that's possible.
- Hello, Paula. I've just had good news — I got the job!
- What ==awful weather==!
- Could I have ==another coffee==, please?
- Sorry to trouble you. Do you have ==any change==?
:::

**some** to ask politely or to offer; **any** when you really don't know.

## Quiz

::: quiz
? Could I have ___ milk in my tea, please?
= some
? Do you have ___ sugar at home?
= any
? There's a lot of ___ outside. I can barely hear you.
= noise
? I've worked here for years, so I have plenty of ___.
= experience
? We need to buy ___ chairs for the new office.
= some
? She poured herself a ___ coffee and sat down by the window.
= a
:::

::: summary
- A noun can count for **one thing** and not count for **the stuff or the general idea**: _a noise_, but _a lot of noise_.
- The usual uncountable nouns have **no plural**: _advice, information, luggage, news, weather_.
- With drinks, count the **cups** (_two coffees_) but not the drink itself.
- **some** to ask or offer politely, **any** when you are unsure.
:::
