::: scene 🔧 The shed
Two people try to make sense of a mess.
> Nils: The bulb has gone again.
> Rae: I'll get a torch. Try not to ==turn the lights out== before I find it.
> Nils: No chance. They ==went out== when the fuse blew, and my battery ==ran out== ten minutes later.
> Rae: Then we can't ==work out== where the break is.
> Nils: Give me five minutes; I'll ==sort it out==.
:::

## out = no longer burning or shining

::: compare
# 💡 lights
- Suddenly all the lights ==went out==.
- I ==turned the lights out== before leaving.
# 🔥 fire
- It took the crew two hours to ==put the fire out==.
# 🕯️ a candle
- We don't need it. You can ==blow it out==.
:::

## work out, in three lives

::: compare
# 🏋️ physical exercise
- Ida ==works out== at the pool on Monday evenings.
# 📈 things going well
- I hope everything ==works out== for you.
- Things didn't ==work out==. ==(they didn't go well)==
# ➗ calculate
- 1,480 ÷ 7? That's too hard without a machine; I can't ==work it out== mentally.
# 🔎 understand a problem
- The engineers are still trying to ==work out what caused the collapse==.
- or ==figure out what caused it==.
:::

==== **Work out** with a calculation, or with a problem to understand. **Figure out** does the second job only.

## Carrying out and finding out

::: compare
# 🔨 carry out — do a task that was arranged
- Soldiers are expected to ==carry out orders==.
- An investigation ==will be carried out==.
# 🔍 find out — get information
- The police never ==found out who did it==.
- I read two reviews to ==find out about the hotels== there.
:::

**Find out** takes a clause (**who, what, whether**) or **about**.

## Handing out and pointing out

::: compare
# 🤲 give / hand out — to each person
- At the end the speaker ==gave out information sheets==.
# 👉 point out — draw attention to
- The guide ==pointed out all the sights==.
- I didn't realise my mistake until someone ==pointed it out to me==.
:::

==== **Point out** is not the same as pointing at something in the room. It means *mention, draw attention to*.

## Running out and sorting out

::: compare
# ⛽ run out of — use up completely
- Our tank ran dry: we ==ran out of petrol== before the next town.
# 🧰 sort out — solve or arrange
- Several details still need sorting: someone must ==sort them out==.
- These papers are mixed up; I'll ==sort them out==.
# 🧪 try out — test something new
- The company is ==trying out new software== at the moment.
:::

## turn out

**Turn out** often carries a surprise: what people believed first turns out to be different.

::: compare
# 💡 the final reality
- Nobody believed him, but he ==turned out to be right==.
- I assumed they were friends; it ==turned out that they had only just met==.
# ☀️ a result
- The morning was awful, but it ==turned out nice later==.
:::

::: trap
✗ I need a torch; the lights have ==gone in==.
✓ the lights have ==gone out==.
✗ We need to ==find out what happened==.
✓ We need to ==find out what happened==.
✗ She can't ==work out her rent== in her head.
✓ She can't ==work out her rent==. ==(calculate it)==
✗ They have ==ran out of bread==.
✓ They have ==run out of bread==.
✗ The investigation ==will carry out== next week.
✓ The investigation ==will be carried out== next week.
✗ I just found out about ==it's her birthday==.
✓ I just found out that ==it's her birthday==.
✗ We ran out of the milk. — no
✓ We ran out ==of the milk==.
✗ She was ==taken in to== by the advert.
✓ She was ==taken in by== the advert.
:::

::: summary
- **out** with light and fire: _the lights went out, put the fire out, blow the candle out._
- **work out** = exercise, go well, calculate, or solve a problem; **figure out** = solve a problem only.
- **carry out** a task, **find out** information, **give out** to each person, **point out** to draw attention to.
- **run out of**, **sort out**, **try out**, **turn out** — the last often brings a surprise.
:::
