## in — conditions, languages, states

**in** covers a surprising amount: the weather, a language, a mood, a feeling, an opinion.

::: form
# 🌧️ in — weather and conditions
in the rain
in the sun
in the shade
in the dark
in bad weather
:::

::: form
# 🌍 in — other fixed uses
in Russian
in dollars
in love (with somebody)
in a bad mood
in my opinion
in kilometres
:::

- How do you say _cheers_ **in** your language?
- They are **in love**.
- You seem **in** a mood this morning.
- Fifty miles? How much is that **in** kilometres?

## on — media, purpose, activity

**on** marks the thing something happens *through* or the state it is in.

::: form
# 📺 on — the medium
on TV
on the radio
on the phone
on a website
on the screen
# 🎯 on — the purpose or state
on purpose
on sale
on fire
on the whole
# 🧳 on — an activity you are away for
on holiday
on business
on a trip
on a tour
on a cruise
on a diet
on strike
:::

- Nobody did it **on purpose**.
- **On the whole**, the job suits me.
- The drivers are **on strike**; there are no trains.
- Emma's away **on business** this week.

You can also say _go somewhere **for** a holiday_: _She has flown to Crete **for a holiday**._

## at — rates, ages, speeds

**at** introduces a number you measure something by.

::: form
# 📏 at + a number
at the age of sixteen
at ninety miles an hour
at one hundred degrees
at a speed of 800 kph
at an altitude of nine thousand metres
:::

- Tracy left school **at 16**. (also: **at the age of 16**)
- Water boils **at** 100 degrees.

## The same sentence, three prepositions

::: compare
# 🌍 in — conditions and surroundings
- We sat ==in the shade== all afternoon.
- How much is it ==in euros==?
# 📺 on — medium, purpose, state
- I heard the forecast ==on the radio==.
- The engine was running ==on== all morning.
# 📏 at — a measurement, a rate
- The plane was flying ==at 900 kilometres an hour==.
:::

## Trap

::: trap
✗ She is ==in love with== him. — wrong only if you leave **with** out after **love**.
✓ They fell ==in love==. ==(no with)==
✗ He works ==in home==.
✓ He works ==at home==.
✗ She said it ==on purpose== to hurt you.
✗ She was driving ==in 120 kilometres an hour==.
✓ She was driving ==at 120 kilometres an hour==.
✗ The shop is ==on sale== this week.
✗ We are going ==in holiday== on Friday.
✓ We are going ==on holiday== on Friday.
✗ He left school ==in the age of 16==.
✓ He left school ==at the age of 16==.
:::

::: summary
- **in** for weather and conditions, languages and currencies, moods, love and opinions.
- **on** for the medium, for purpose and state, and for trips and activities.
- **at** for rates, ages, speeds and degrees: _at the age of sixteen, at ninety miles an hour._
- _go somewhere **for** a holiday_ and _stay **at** home_ are the two that break the pattern.
:::
