::: scene 🔎 The empty fridge
Ella comes home and opens the fridge. The cheesecake is gone; there are crumbs on the sofa.
> Ella: Somebody ==must have eaten== it.
> Flatmate: It ==can't have been== me — I was at work all day.
> Ella: Then it ==must be== your brother. He ==must be== still here!
:::

## Must and can't: sure about now
These are not about rules here. They show what you **conclude** from the facts.

::: compare
# ✓ must — I'm sure it's true
- He's been running for an hour. He ==must be== exhausted.
- Every light is on. They ==must be== at home.
- You ==must be== Lily's sister — you look just like her.
# ✗ can't — I'm sure it's not true
- He's only just started. He ==can't be== tired yet.
- The car isn't here. They ==can't be== at home.
- She ==can't be== his mother — she's too young.
:::

::: rule 🧩 structure
**must / can't + base verb**: _must know, can't be_

**must / can't + be + -ing** for something happening now: _She ==must be working== — her office light is on._
:::

::: trap
✗ That bill ~~mustn't~~ be right — it's huge!
✓ That bill can't be right.
For "I'm sure it's not true", use **can't**, not _mustn't_.
:::

## Must have done, can't have done: sure about the past
For a conclusion about the past, add **have + past participle**.

:::: row
::: form
# ✓ sure it happened
I / you / they… | ==must have== | left / been / seen
# ✗ sure it didn't
I / you / they… | ==can't have== / ==couldn't have== | left / been / seen
:::
::: timeline
point 30 | something happened?
point now | I see the clues
:::
::::

::: cards
# 🔑 no keys
I can't find my keys. I ==must have left== them at work.
# 🌊 wet streets
It ==must have rained== in the night.
# 📵 no reply
She ==can't have seen== my message, or she'd have answered.
# 🎉 the noise
The neighbours ==must have been having== a party.
:::

::: note 🗝️
**couldn't have** works like _can't have_: _He ==couldn't have== heard us — we were whispering._
:::

::: quiz
? Their house is right next to the airport. It ___ be very noisy.
= must
? You've just eaten a huge meal. You ___ be hungry already!
= can't
? Lena's wearing her running clothes and she's red in the face. She ___ (run).
= must have been running / must have run
? Ivan failed the test. He ___ (study) very hard.
= can't have studied / couldn't have studied
:::

::: summary
- **must** = I'm sure it's true; **can't** = I'm sure it's not true.
- Present: **must / can't + verb** or **+ be -ing**.
- Past: **must have / can't have (couldn't have) + past participle**.
- Not _mustn't_ for "it's impossible".
:::
