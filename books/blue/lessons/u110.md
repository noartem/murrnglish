::: scene ⏰ Habits
A colleague asks about routines. Every answer has the adverb in the wrong place.
She drinks always tea before work.
I take usually a shower before work.
Steve gets hardly ever angry.
:::

## One position, three shapes

Where a frequency adverb sits depends on the verb in front of it.

::: compare
# 🟢 a plain verb — adverb BEFORE it
- Helen ==always drives== to work.
- I ==usually take== a shower before work.
- We ==often walk== after dinner.

# 🟢 the verb be — adverb AFTER it
- I'm ==always hungry== when I get home.
- He was ==never late== for class.

# 🟢 an auxiliary — adverb AFTER it
- I've ==never worked== in a factory.
- She ==has probably forgotten==.
:::

::: note 🔑
Say the auxiliary first, then the adverb, then the main verb: _I **have** **never** **worked** here._ Put the adverb in front and it is wrong.

:::

## Long adverbs

Adverbs that are longer — *quickly, suddenly, probably, unnecessarily* — usually come **after** the verb or after the object.

:::: row
::: compare
# 🔹 short, before the verb
- She ==never complains==.

# 🔸 long, after it
- She complained ==very quietly==.
- He arrived ==two hours late==.
- The shop was ==probably closed==.
:::

::: trap
✗ Helen drives always to work.
✓ Helen ==always drives== to work.
✗ I take usually a shower before work.
✓ I ==usually have== a shower.
✗ Steve gets hardly ever angry.
✓ Steve ==hardly ever gets== angry.
✗ I never have worked in a factory.
✓ I ==have never worked== in a factory.
:::
::::

## Two adverbs, or a softener

With **also** and **still**, the order follows the length: short before long.

::: examples
- I called him and I ==also sent== an email.
- We ==haven't moved==; we are ==still living== in the same flat.
- Martin is learning Spanish, and he is ==also learning== Japanese.
- It is ==probably== the ==cheapest== ticket.
:::

**all**, **both**, **probably** and **always** sit after the verb when the subject comes first, and right in front of it when the subject comes first: ==we all== had a great time / ==all of us== had a great time.

## Quiz

::: quiz
? She ___ to the office on Fridays.
= always goes
? He ___ angry when people shout.
= is never
? I ___ spoken to him.
= have never
? Our cat ___ sleeps under the bed.
= usually
? The film ___ started twenty minutes late.
= actually
? We ___ at the same hotel.
= were all
? Tanya ___ that she'll phone, but she ___ .
= always says / never does
? If we had taken different trains, we ___ .
= would never have met
? ___ I see her, I think about the old days.
= Whenever
:::

::: summary
- Plain verb → adverb **before** it: _always drives_.
- Verb **be** → adverb **after** it: _is always hungry_.
- Auxiliary → adverb **after** it, before the main verb: _have never worked_.
- Longer adverbs tend to come after the verb or object.
:::
