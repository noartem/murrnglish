## up to, catch up, keep up

**Up** here is about distance and speed.

::: compare
# 🚶 walk / come / go up to — approach
- A cyclist ==came up to me== beside the canal and asked for directions.
- She ==walked up to the door== and rang the bell.
# 🏃 catch up with — reach somebody ahead
- You go on; I'll ==catch up with you==.
- or I'll ==catch you up== before you reach the gate.
# 🚶 keep up with — stay at the same pace
- You're striding ahead. I can't ==keep up with you== on these paths.
- You're doing well. ==Keep it up==!
:::

==== **Catch up** = overtake somebody ahead. **Keep up** = stay level with them.

## Starting something

::: compare
# 🏢 set up — start an organisation or system
- The council has ==set up a committee== to look into the flooding.
# 🎾 take up — start a hobby or activity
- Yara ==took up photography== when she was nineteen.
# 📅 fix up — arrange
- We've ==fixed up a meeting== for Monday.
# 👧 grow up — become an adult
- She was born in Seoul but ==grew up in Australia==.
# 🧺 bring up — raise a child
- Her parents died young; she was ==brought up by her aunt==.
:::

## Cleaning

::: compare
# 🧹 tidy up / clean up / clear up
- Look at this mess! Who is going to ==tidy up==?
- or ==tidy it up==.
# 🍽️ wash up — the dishes
- I hate ==washing up== after a big dinner.
:::

## Seven verbs worth knowing by heart

::: compare
# 📍 end up — and that is where you finished
- A fight broke out; three men ==ended up in hospital==.
- We missed the last ferry and ==ended up sleeping in the car park==.
# 🙌 give up — stop trying, or stop doing something
- Don't ==give up==. Keep trying!
- Sue got bored and decided to ==give it up==.
# 🧩 make up / be made up of
- Pupils under 16 ==make up== half the roll at that school.
- Air is ==made up of== nitrogen and oxygen.
# ⏳ take up — use space or time
- The table ==took up== most of the room.
# ⏰ turn up / show up — arrive
- We arranged to meet David, but he didn't ==turn up==.
# 🧈 use up — use all of it
- There's a glut of courgettes, and I want to ==use them up== before they wilt.
:::

::: trap
✗ She's walking too fast and I can't ==keep up==.
✓ She's walking too fast and I can't ==keep up with her==.
✗ A man ==walked up of me== and asked the way.
✓ A man ==walked up to me== and asked the way.
✗ I couldn't find a hotel, so I ==ended up sleep== on a bench.
✓ I couldn't find a hotel, so I ==ended up sleeping== on a bench.
✗ Her parents died and she was ==brought up of== her aunt.
✓ Her parents died and she was ==brought up by== her aunt.
✗ She got bored and decided to ==give up it==.
✓ She got bored and decided to ==give it up==.
✗ There's a glut of courgettes and I want to ==use up them==.
✓ There's a glut of courgettes and I want to ==use them up==.
✗ The exam ==took up== three hours of my life. — no
✗ She said something she ==took up on== the radio.
✓ She said something she ==picked up on== the radio.
✗ He ==showed up his ticket== at the door.
✓ He ==showed his ticket== at the door.
:::

::: summary
- **up to** = approach, **catch up with** = reach somebody ahead, **keep up with** = stay at the same pace.
- **set up** an organisation, **take up** a hobby, **fix up** a meeting, **grow up**, **brought up**.
- **end up** = and that is where you finished; **turn up** = arrive; **use up** = use all of it.
- **give up** = stop trying, **make up** = form a whole, **take up** time or space.
