::: scene 🧾 The shortlist
A manager has 40 applications and a very small amount of time.
Ten people applied for the job, most of whom had no relevant experience.
We were sent a lot of paperwork, which was useless.
My car broke down, of which I had no warning.
:::

## A preposition can come first

Extra clauses are formal, and a formal clause likes to open with its preposition: **in which**, **to whom**, **of which**, **for which**.

::: form
# 🏠 a preposition + whom / which
The wedding, ==to which== only family were invited, went on all night.
I share an office with my boss, ==with whom== I get on very well.

# 🔚 the everyday way
The wedding ==that only family were invited to== went on all night.
:::

For a **person**, use **whom**: the son ==to whom== the money went. For a **thing**, use **which**.

## all of, most of, none of

When the noun is a group, you can put the part of it **inside** the clause: all of, most of, none of, one of, half of, several of, both of.

::: compare
# 👥 people
- Nora has two sisters, ==both of whom are architects==.
- Ten people applied, ==none of whom was suitable==.
- We had three candidates, ==two of whom spoke French==.

# 📦 things
- We were given a lot of information, ==most of which was useless==.
- James won £100,000, ==half of which he gave away==.
- There were many people at the party, ==only a few of whom I knew==.
:::

::: note 🔗
You can also push the noun out front: the hotel ==the name of which== I cannot remember. It means the same thing and sounds more formal still.
:::

## which for a whole fact

Here **which** does not point at a noun at all. It stands for everything in the clause before it — in writing, _this_ or _that_.

::: compare
# 🔵 which = this / that
- Laura couldn't come, ==which was a shame==. (= that was a shame)
- My flight was delayed, ==which meant two hours of waiting==. (= that meant …)

# 🔁 the word itself
- She apologised for the delay, ==for which she was== forgiven.
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? Our flight was cancelled, ___ we spent the night at the airport.
= which
? Ben showed me his new bike, ___ he is very proud.
= of which
? The three candidates were all interviewed, ___ one got the job.
= of whom
? She showed us a photo of her son, ___ she is very proud.
= of whom
? We bought a sofa, ___ we paid three hundred pounds.
= for which
? Nadia has two brothers, ___ live abroad.
= both of whom
? My laptop stopped working, ___ I lost two days of work.
= which
:::

::: summary
- An extra clause may open with a preposition: **in which**, **to whom**, ==of which==.
- **whom** for people, **which** for things.
- A group goes inside the clause: ==all of whom==, ==most of which==, ==none of whom==.
- **which** can also stand for a whole fact: my flight was delayed, ==which was a pity==.
:::
