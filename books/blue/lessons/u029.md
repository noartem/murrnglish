---
hook: 🤷 Perhaps, maybe, possibly — may and might for things you're not sure about.
goals: say something is possibly true now with may or might · say something possibly happened with may have / might have done · tell might not have from couldn't have
---
::: scene 📱 Where's Dev?
Dev was meant to meet Clara at the café twenty minutes ago.
> Clara: He's not answering. He ==might be driving==.
> Friend: Or he ==may have forgotten==. He does that.
> Clara: He ==might not have seen== my message, either.
> Friend: He ==couldn't have forgotten== — he set the time himself!
:::

## May and might: maybe it's true
**may** or **might + base verb** = it's possible that something is true. They mean the same here.

:::: row
::: rule 🌫️ may / might + verb
That ==might be== the right answer.

The museum ==may be== closed on Mondays.

Ask Ben — he ==might know==.
:::
::: rule ➖ the negative
**may not / might not** = possibly not.

She ==may not be== at home.

They ==might not want== to come.
:::
::::

::: examples
- Don't call now. The baby ==might be sleeping==. // be + -ing: happening now
- Whose coat is this? — It ==may be== Ali's.
- Gil's very quiet. He ==might not be feeling== well.
:::

::: trap
✗ I'm not sure. Maybe ~~he might be~~ ill.
✓ I'm not sure. He might be ill. / Maybe he's ill.
**might** already means _maybe_; you don't need both.
:::

## May have done, might have done: maybe it happened
For something possible in the past, use **may / might + have + past participle**.

::: cards
# 🧾 the bill
I can't find the receipt. I ==might have thrown== it away.
# 🚪 the door
Nobody answered. They ==may have been== asleep.
# 🙈 the surprise
How did Pam find out? — Jo ==may have told== her.
# 🕰️ late
Leo isn't here yet. He ==might not have left== work.
:::

## Might not have or couldn't have?
They look similar but say different things.

::: compare
# 🤔 might not have — maybe it didn't happen
- I'm not sure he saw the sign. He ==might not have seen== it.
- possible, not certain
# 🚫 couldn't have — it's impossible
- He ==couldn't have seen== it. It was covered in snow.
- I'm sure it didn't happen
:::

::: quiz
? I'm not sure where Rita is. She ___ (be) in the garden.
= might be / may be
? "Who left the gate open?" "The postman ___ (leave) it open."
= may have left / might have left
? He's not usually late. He ___ (miss) the bus.
= might have missed / may have missed
? The thief ___ (get) in through the window — it's too small.
= couldn't have got
:::

::: summary
- **may / might + verb** = perhaps it's true now.
- **may / might + be -ing** = perhaps it's happening now.
- **may / might + have + past participle** = perhaps it happened.
- **might not have** = maybe not; **couldn't have** = impossible.
:::
