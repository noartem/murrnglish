## Would: imagining
**would ('d) + verb** = we imagine a situation that isn't real, or isn't happening.

:::: row
::: rule 🌈 imagine
It ==would be== lovely to live by a lake.

I=='d love== to speak Japanese.

I ==wouldn't like== to be famous.
:::
::: rule 🔄 looking back
**would have + past participle** = imagine something in the past that didn't happen.

The party was great. You=='d have enjoyed== it.

We took the car — walking ==would have been== faster.
:::
::::

::: compare
# ⏩ will — real future
- I'm going to Spain. It ==will be== fun.
# 💭 would — imagined
- I'd like to go to Spain. It ==would be== fun.
# ⏪ would have — imagined past
- I couldn't go to Spain. It ==would have been== fun.
:::

::: note 🤔
Asking for advice: _What ==would== you ==do== in my place?_
:::

## Would = will in the past
**will** becomes **would** when you report what someone said or thought before.

::: examples
- Ned: "I'll call you." → Ned promised he=='d call== me.
- "It won't rain." → They said it ==wouldn't rain==. It poured.
- I knew you ==would== understand.
:::

## Wouldn't = refused
**somebody wouldn't do something** = they refused to do it.

::: cards
# 🙅 the child
I begged him to eat his peas, but he ==wouldn't==.
# 🚗 the car
The engine ==wouldn't start==. (like _won't_ in [[u21]])
# 🤐 the secret
I asked Rina, but she ==wouldn't tell== me.
:::

## Would = used to: past habits
**would** can describe things that happened **regularly** in the past.

:::: row
::: timeline
repeat 10 50 | every summer at the farm
point now | now
:::
::: examples
- Every summer, Grandpa ==would wake== us at dawn.
- We ==would spend== hours by the river.
- Whenever it snowed, the school ==would close==.
:::
::::

::: trap
✗ We ~~would live~~ in a tiny flat when I was small.
✓ We used to live in a tiny flat when I was small.
For **states** (live, have, be, know), use _used to_. _would_ is only for repeated actions.
:::

::: quiz
? I'd love a dog, but my flat ___ (be) too small.
= would be
? You missed a great match. You ___ (love) it.
= would have loved / 'd have loved
? She promised she ___ (not / be) late, but she was.
= wouldn't be
? When I was little, my mum ___ (sing) to me every night.
= would sing / used to sing
:::

::: summary
- **would + verb** = an imagined situation; **would have done** = an imagined past.
- **would** = _will_ in the past: _He said he'd come._
- **wouldn't** = refused: _She wouldn't listen._
- **would** = repeated past actions (not states).
:::
