::: scene 📱 The lost phone
Dev bursts into the kitchen.
> Dev: Good news — I=='ve found== my phone!
> Mum: Finally. Where ==did== you ==find== it?
> Dev: It ==was== in the washing machine. I ==washed== it with my jeans.
> Mum: And does it work?
> Dev: …It ==has stopped== working.
:::

## Still true now, or not any more?
Use the **present perfect** when the result of an action is still there. When the result has gone, or the situation has changed back, use the **past simple**.

::: compare
# ✅ still true → have done
- I=='ve cut== my hair. (it's short now)
- The lift ==has broken== down. (take the stairs)
- Emma ==has gone== to Rome. (she's there)
# ↩️ not true any more → did
- I ==cut== my hair, but it's long again now.
- The lift ==broke== down, but they fixed it.
- Emma ==went== to Rome, but she's home now.
:::

::: timeline
point past | the lift broke down
span past now | still broken
point now | you take the stairs
:::

::: note 🗞️
The present perfect often brings **news**: _Have you heard? Ravi=='s passed== his driving test!_ Without a result now, it sounds odd.
:::

## The news, then the details
People often open with the present perfect, then switch to the past simple for **when, where, how**. The details belong to a finished moment.

::: examples
- There=='s been== a fire at the station. — Oh no! When ==did== it ==start==?
- I=='ve hurt== my back. — How ==did== you ==do== that? — I ==lifted== a heavy box.
- Jess ==has found== a new flat. She ==signed== the contract on Friday.
:::

::: cards
# 🔔 news
I=='ve won== a prize!
# 🔍 detail
What ==did== you ==win==?
# 📍 detail
Where ==was== the competition?
:::

## History and finished lives
For people who are no longer alive and for past events in history, use the **past simple**. Their story is finished.

:::: row
::: examples
- Marie Curie ==won== two Nobel prizes.
- Who ==built== the first car?
- Van Gogh ==painted== about 900 pictures.
:::
::: rule 👶 born
_Be born_ is always about a past moment:

I ==was born== in Lagos. Where ==were== you ==born==?
:::
::::

::: trap
✗ Who ~~has discovered~~ penicillin?
✓ Who discovered penicillin?
✗ Where ~~have you been born~~?
✓ Where were you born?
:::

::: quiz
? I ___ (lose) my wallet, but I found it later.
= lost
? Look! Someone ___ (paint) the fence. It's bright green now.
= has painted / 's painted
? How many films ___ (Charlie Chaplin / make)?
= did Charlie Chaplin make
? I've bought a bike. — Great! How much ___ (it / cost)?
= did it cost
:::

::: summary
- Result still there → **have done**; result gone → **did**.
- News in the present perfect, details (when, where, how) in the past simple.
- History and people no longer alive → past simple.
- **was / were born**, never _have been born_.
:::
