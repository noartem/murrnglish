## One adjective, two questions
A small family of adjectives asks two different questions, and English answers them with two
different shapes.

::: form
# ❓ to do, or of -ing?
adjective + **to** + verb | a fear/interest **of doing** something about to happen
adjective + **of** + **-ing** | a fear/interest **in what happens** or happened

_I'm afraid **to open** it._ → I'm about to, and I don't want to.
_I'm afraid **of opening** it._ → I'm thinking about it, and it worries me.
:::

::: compare
# 🚶 to + verb — the action itself
- ==Nobody here is afraid to swim== in cold water.
- ==I'm afraid to tell== her what happened.
- ==Don't be afraid to ask== me anything.
- ==We were afraid to cross== the bridge in the dark.
# 🧱 of + -ing — the thing itself
- ==I'm afraid of losing== my job.
- ==We were afraid of falling==.
- ==I was afraid of running== for weeks afterwards.
# 👀 interested
- I'm ==interested in buying== an old cottage.
- ==I'm interested to know== what it costs.
:::

**Interested** is the clearest illustration: **interested in** something you want to *do or have*,
**interested to** something you want to *know*.

::: note 🔎
_I'm **interested in** starting my own business._ — the interest is in the business.
_You'll be **interested to know** that she's getting married._ — the interest is in the news.
The preposition follows the object: a **thing** takes **in**, a **piece of information** takes
**to**.
:::

## sorry — doing it, or hearing about it
**Sorry** works the same way, and the difference is about whose situation it is.

::: form
# 😔 sorry to / sorry for
sorry **to do** | sorry about something in the future or just now
sorry **for doing** | sorry about something you did
:::

::: examples
- ==I'm sorry to bother== you, but I have a question.
- ==I'm sorry to hear== about her accident.
- ==I'll be sorry to see== him go.
- ==I'm sorry for saying== that. I didn't mean it.
- ==I'm sorry for making== such a mess.
:::

::: compare
# 🎯 sorry to + verb
- "==" I'm sorry to disturb==" you.
- ==We were sorry to lose== the final.
# 😬 sorry for + -ing
- ==I'm sorry for taking== your car.
- ==Sorry for worrying== you.
:::

## prevented, but not stopped from
**Prevent** always needs **from**, because it describes somebody stopping an action.

::: rule 🧩 prevent somebody from -ing
**prevent** + **person** + **from** + **-ing**

_They prevented the plane **from taking off**._

_the person_ · _the action that never happened_
:::

::: cards
# 🚫 want / hope / fail + to
_She wanted **to leave**._ · _They hoped **to arrive**._ · _He failed **to come**._
# 🛑 prevent + person + from
_They stopped me **from speaking**._ · _The rain prevented us **from leaving**._
# 🙋 insist on / promise to
_He insisted **on driving**._ · _She promised **to help**._
# 🙅 refuse to / offer to
_I refuse **to sign**._ · _He offered **to wait**._
:::

::: note 🙅
**refuse** takes **to**, never _refuse from_, and **insist on** always needs the **on** — see
[[u62|verb + preposition + -ing]].
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~I'm afraid of go out.~~
✓ I'm afraid **to go** out.
✗ ~~I'm afraid to lose my job.~~
✓ I'm afraid **of losing** my job.
✗ ~~I'm sorry for interrupt.~~
✓ I'm sorry **for interrupting**.
✗ ~~I'm sorry to hear that she died~~ → correct.
✗ ~~They prevented to leave.~~
✓ They prevented him **from leaving**.
✗ ~~He refused from helping.~~
✓ He refused **to help**.
**to do** = the action ahead of you. **of -ing** = the thing itself, happening or done.
:::

::: cards
# 😨 afraid
_I'm afraid **to try**._ / _I'm afraid **of failing**._
# 👀 interested
_I'm interested **in** that._ / _I'm interested **to hear** more._
# 😔 sorry
_I'm sorry **to bother** you._ / _I'm sorry **for interrupting**._
# 🛑 prevented
_They prevented him **from leaving**._
:::

::: summary
- **to do** = the action ahead of you; **of -ing** = the thing itself.
- **afraid to cross** / **afraid of falling**; **interested in buying** / **interested to know**;
  **sorry to bother** / **sorry for interrupting**.
- **prevent** needs **from**: _prevented him **from leaving**._
- **refuse to**, **offer to**, **promise to**, **hope to**, **want to**, **fail to** — all take
  **to**.
:::
