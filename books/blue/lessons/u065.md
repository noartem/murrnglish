The dentist's receptionist has asked everyone the same three questions.
> Receptionist: Was it hard to get here?
> Man: No, it was easy. I parked ==right outside==.
> Receptionist: And the wait?
> Woman: It was ==the longest== I've ever had.
> Receptionist: Sorry about that. Did you mind ==me being== so slow?
> Woman: Not at all. You're the only one ==in town== who does same-day appointments.
> Receptionist: I doubt it. You're the first person to say that. // That's a compliment.
> Man: Really? I thought it was impossible to get seen at all.

## Moving the adjective
"It's difficult to open the window" and "the window is difficult to open" say the same thing. The
second puts the **subject** first and the adjective right after it.

::: form
# 🔄 the same fact, two shapes
It is + adjective + **to** + subject + verb | The subject is + adjective + **to** + verb
It was hard to open the door. | The door was hard to open.
Nobody can translate some words. | Some words can't be translated.

_It is …_ puts the action first. · _The subject is …_ puts the thing first.
:::

::: examples
- It was expensive to service the car. → The car was ==expensive to service==.
- It's simple to reach from here. → My place is ==simple to reach== from here.
- It isn't wise to eat this. → This ==isn't wise to eat==.
- It was impressive to watch. → The performance was ==impressive to watch==.
:::

## Adjectives that take a person
A group of **feelings** works in two directions: **glad to hear**, **relieved to hear** — and
**glad that she phoned**, **kind of you to help**.

::: form
# 🙂 two directions
adjective + **to do** | I was glad ==to hear== it.
adjective + **of somebody** + **to do** | It was kind ==of you to ask==.

_about the action_ · _about the person_
:::

::: words
glad · relieved · pleased · delighted · disappointed · annoyed · amazed · surprised · grateful
kind · nice · silly · stupid · strange · generous · sensible
:::

::: examples
- I'm ==glad to be== back.
- She was ==relieved to hear== that they were safe.
- I'd be ==pleased to help== if you need it.
- It was ==disappointing not to see== her.
- It was ==amazed== how quiet the place was.
- It was ==silly of me to forget== your name.
- It was ==kind of you to wait== so long.
:::

::: note 📌
**Of** introduces the person only with this second group: _silly **of me** to forget_. Without
**of** the sentence becomes personal: _I was **silly** to forget._
:::

## The first, the last, the only
**The** … + **person** + **to do** singles somebody out by what they did — and the verb keeps
its bare form.

::: form
# 🥇 superlatives
the + first / last / only / second + **noun** + **to** + verb

the only student ==to pass== · the first person ==to arrive==

_who_ · _what they did_
:::

::: examples
- She was the ==only one to finish== the marathon.
- He was the ==last to leave== the office.
- She was the ==second person to be offered== the job.
- They were the ==first to complain== about the noise.
- You'll be the ==only one not to enjoy== it.
:::

## Bound, sure, likely
A last group says something is **certain** to happen, and these take **to + verb** as well.

::: form
# 🎯 certainty
certain / sure / bound / likely + **to do**

I'm ==sure to forget== it.
It is ==bound to rain==.
She is ==likely to be late==.

_how sure_ · _what will happen_
:::

::: examples
- You're ==bound to be tired== after such a journey.
- He's ==sure to forget== anything you tell him.
- ==It's likely to rain== later.
- The results are ==certain to come through== on Friday.
:::

::: trap
✗ ~~It was difficult opening the window.~~
✓ It was difficult ==to open== the window.
✗ ~~The window was difficult opening.~~
✓ The window was difficult ==to open==.
✗ ~~It was silly me to forget.~~
✓ It was silly ==of me to forget==.
✗ ~~She was glad to hear that she passed.~~
✓ She was ==glad to hear== she had passed.
✗ ~~She was the only student that pass.~~
✓ She was the only student ==to pass==.
**to** before the verb, every time — including after **of somebody** and after
**the first / last / only**.
:::

::: cards
# 🔄 rewritten
_It was hard to reach_ → The place was hard to reach.
# 🙋 a feeling about an action
_I was delighted to see_ her.
# 🫡 a feeling about a person
_It was thoughtful of her to wait._
# 🥇 singled out
_He was the only one to notice._
:::

::: summary
- **It is + adjective + to do** ↔ **the subject + adjective + to do**.
- Feelings take **to do** (glad to hear) or **of somebody + to do** (kind of you to wait).
- **the first / last / only / second + noun + to do** singles somebody out.
- **certain / sure / bound / likely + to do** says something is certain to happen.
- **to** before the verb in every one of these.
:::
