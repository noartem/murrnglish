::: scene 🗞️ The local news
A reporter stands outside a closed factory.
> Reporter: ==It is believed that== the owner has left the country.
> Reporter: He ==is said to have taken== the company's money with him.
> Reporter: The factory ==was supposed to== reopen this week. Workers ==are expected to== protest tomorrow.
:::

## Two ways to pass on what people say
With verbs like **say, believe, think, expect, report, know, consider, allege**, there are two passive patterns.

:::: row
::: form
# 1️⃣ It is said that…
==It is said that== | Ola | is very rich.
==It is thought that== | the fire | started in the kitchen.
# 2️⃣ He is said to…
Ola | ==is said to be== | very rich.
The fire | ==is thought to have started== | in the kitchen.
:::
::: rule 🗣️ same meaning
_People say that Ola is very rich._

= _It is said that Ola is very rich._

= _Ola is said to be very rich._
:::
::::

::: compare
# ▶️ now → to do / to be / to be doing
- The castle ==is said to be== haunted.
- He ==is believed to be living== in Spain.
- The strike ==is expected to end== soon.
# ⏪ past → to have done / to have been
- The castle ==is said to have been== a prison.
- He ==is believed to have left== in May.
- The thieves ==are thought to have escaped== by boat.
:::

::: trap
✗ The film is said ~~to win~~ three awards last year.
✓ The film is said to have won three awards last year.
For something earlier than now, use **to have + past participle**.
:::

## Supposed to = people say
**be supposed to** often means _it is said to_: what people say, which may or may not be true.

::: cards
# 🍝 the restaurant
That new place ==is supposed to be== excellent.
# 🎬 the film
The ending ==is supposed to be== a big surprise.
# 🏝️ the island
They ==are supposed to have found== gold there once.
:::

## Supposed to = it should happen
**be supposed to do** also means it's planned, expected or arranged — and often it doesn't happen.

::: examples
- You=='re supposed to== wear a helmet here. // that's the rule
- The bus ==was supposed to== come at 6. It came at 6.40.
- I'm ==not supposed to== eat sugar. Doctor's orders.
- What's this? Is it ==supposed to be== a cat? // what it should be
- Hey, you =='re supposed to be== my friend! // what you should be like
:::

::: note 🚫
**You're not supposed to…** = it's not allowed: _You =='re not supposed to== smoke in here._
:::

::: quiz
? It is believed that the painting is fake. → The painting ___ fake.
= is believed to be
? It is said that he spoke ten languages. → He ___ ten languages.
= is said to have spoken
? The match ___ (supposed / start) at 3, but it's been delayed.
= was supposed to start / is supposed to start
? You ___ (not / supposed / use) your phone in the exam.
= 're not supposed to use / aren't supposed to use
:::

::: summary
- **It is said / thought / believed that…** = **He is said to…**
- Present: _to be, to do, to be doing_; past: **to have done / been**.
- **supposed to** = people say: _It's supposed to be good._
- **supposed to** = planned or expected: _I was supposed to call._
:::
