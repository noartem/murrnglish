## Nouns that take for

**for** here means *reason, need, purpose* — the thing that explains or justifies.

::: form
# 🔎 for — reason, need, demand
a reason for the delay
a demand for the product
a need for new housing
an excuse for the behaviour
a case for cheaper fares
:::

==== The wrong one is **reason of**: ~~nobody knew the reason of the delay~~.

## Nouns that take of

**of** follows a noun that already says what it is *about* or what it *shows*.

::: form
# 📎 of — cause, content, advantage
a cause of the fire
pictures of her family
a map of the town
an advantage of cycling
a disadvantage of flats
:::

One pair changes its preposition with the verb in front:

::: compare
# ✔ the noun after of
- ==The advantage of living alone== is freedom.
# ✔ the noun after in or to
- There are many advantages ==in living alone==.
- or many advantages ==to living alone==.
:::

## Nouns that take in

**in** follows a noun for a change in a number or an amount.

::: form
# 📈 in — a change
an increase in accidents
a decrease in sales
a rise in prices
a fall in applications
:::

**to** is the other common set: things *done to* something, or an answer *to* something.

::: form
# 🎯 to — damage, invitations, answers
damage to the car
an invitation to the wedding
a solution to the problem
a key to the door
an answer to my question
a reaction to the news
an attitude to the job ==(or towards the job)==
:::

## with, and between two things

**with** for one person or thing, **between** for two.

::: compare
# 🤝 with — a relationship
- Are you on good terms ==with your neighbours==?
- Anna has little contact ==with her family== these days.
# ↔️ between — two things or people
- There is no connection ==between the two cases==.
- What is the difference ==between your job and mine==?
:::

## Word families worth learning whole

::: cards
# 📋 invitation
an invitation ==to== a party · an answer ==to== an email
# 🗺️ a picture or map
a photo ==of== the harbour · a map ==of== Madrid
# 🔧 damage
damage ==to== the car · pay for the damage ==to== the car
# 🤝 connection
a connection ==with== the police · a connection ==between== two firms
:::

::: summary
- **for** = reason, need, demand: _the reason for the delay, a demand for the product._
- **of** = what it is about or shows: _the cause of the fire, a map of the town._
- **in** = a change in a number; **to** = damage, invitations, answers, reactions.
- **with** = a relationship between people; **between** = two people or things.
:::
