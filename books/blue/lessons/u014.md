## Finished time → past simple
If you say or ask **when** something happened, and that time is over, the **past simple** is the only choice.

::: words ⏹️ Finished times
yesterday · last night · last week · two days ago · in 2015 · on Monday · when I was a child · at 6 o'clock · What time…? · When…?
:::

::: trap
✗ I ~~have seen~~ that film last Saturday.
✓ I saw that film last Saturday.
✗ When ~~have you arrived~~?
✓ When did you arrive?
✗ When ~~has this castle been built~~?
✓ When was this castle built?
:::

## A period up to now → present perfect
With time words for a period that isn't over yet, use the **present perfect**. The same goes when no time is said at all and you mean "in my life up to now".

::: compare
# ⏯️ still open → have done
- I ==haven't had== coffee ==today==.
- It=='s been== windy ==this week==.
- She=='s worked== hard ==recently==.
- ==Have you ever been== to Japan?
# ⏹️ finished → did
- I ==didn't have== coffee ==yesterday==.
- It ==was== windy ==last week==.
- She ==didn't work== much ==last year==.
- ==Did you go== to Japan ==in May==?
:::

::: note ☀️
**This morning**, **today**, **this year** can go either way. At 11 a.m., _I haven't seen Kim this morning_. At 4 p.m., the morning is over: _I didn't see Kim this morning_.
:::

## All my life, or a life that ended?
Compare how long something has lasted **until now** with a period that **ended** in the past.

:::: row
::: timeline
span 10 now | Nora has lived in Leeds all her life
point now | now
:::
::: timeline
span 10 60 | Grandpa lived in Leeds for 50 years
point 60 | he moved / he died
point now | now
:::
::::

::: cards
# 🏠 still there
How long ==have you lived== here? — Six years.
# 🧳 before that
Where ==did you live== before? — In Oslo.
# ⏳ finished period
How long ==did you live== in Oslo? — Three years.
:::

::: examples
- Ben ==worked== at the post office for 20 years. Then he ==retired==.
- My great-grandmother ==died== before I was born. I ==never met== her.
- I don't know your cousin. I=='ve never met== him.
:::

::: quiz
? I ___ (not / sleep) well last night.
= didn't sleep
? We ___ (not / have) any rain this month.
= haven't had
? Where ___ (you / be) on Saturday evening?
= were you
? Lisa ___ (play) the violin since she was six.
= has played / 's played / has been playing
:::

::: summary
- Finished time (yesterday, ago, in 2015, when…?) → **past simple**.
- Period up to now (today, this week, recently, all my life) → **present perfect**.
- No time said, life up to now → **present perfect**.
- How long … until now → _have done_; a period that ended → _did_.
:::
