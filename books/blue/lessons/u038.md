::: scene 🎟️ The raffle ticket
Bella has a ticket for the village raffle. First prize: a bike. She chats with her grandfather.
> Grandpa: What would you do ==if== you ==won==?
> Bella: I=='d ride== it to school every day!
> Grandpa: And ==if== you ==don't== win?
> Bella: Then I=='ll== keep walking, like always.
:::

## If I did…, I would do…
For a situation you only **imagine** — unlikely, or not real — use **if + past simple** and **would** in the other part. The meaning is present or future, not past.

:::: row
::: form
# 🧩 the pattern
==If== + past | ==would / 'd== + verb
If I ==had== a boat, | I=='d sail== round the islands.
If she ==moved== to Paris, | she=='d need== to learn French.
If it ==snowed== in July, | everyone ==would be== amazed.
:::
::: rule ↔️ either order
I=='d be== scared ==if== a bear ==walked== in.

==If== a bear ==walked== in, I=='d be== scared.

Use a comma when _if_ comes first.
:::
::::

::: examples
- ==If== I ==found== a lost puppy, I=='d take== it home.
- What ==would== you ==do if== you ==saw== a ghost?
- ==If== we ==didn't have== phones, we ==wouldn't know== where anyone was.
- I ==wouldn't== go there at night ==if== I ==were== you.
:::

::: trap
✗ If I ~~would have~~ more time, I'd read more.
✓ If I had more time, I'd read more.
No _would_ in the **if**-part. _would_ goes only in the other half.
:::

## Real or imagined?
**if I do… I'll do…** = it's a real possibility. **if I did… I'd do…** = you're imagining it; it's less likely or not true.

::: compare
# 🌍 real — it may well happen
- ==If== I ==see== Max, I=='ll give== him the book (I often see him).
- ==If== it ==rains==, we=='ll stay== in.
- ==If== you ==turn== left, you=='ll find== the station.
# 💭 imagined — it probably won't
- ==If== I ==met== the queen, I=='d bow== (not going to happen).
- ==If== it ==rained== gold, we=='d all be== rich.
- ==If== the sun ==disappeared==, what ==would== happen?
:::

::: note 🔀
The same idea can go either way, depending on how you see it: _If I ==get== the job, I'll move to Leeds_ (I've applied, I may get it). _If I ==got== a job abroad, I'd move_ (just a dream for now).
:::

::: cards
# 🗺️ could and might
**would** can become **could** or **might**: _If we had a car, we ==could== drive to the coast. If you asked her, she ==might== help._
# ❓ questions
_What ==would== you ==say if== someone ==asked== you to sing?_
:::

::: quiz
? If I ___ (be) a cat, I'd sleep all day.
= were / was
? I don't think Ivo will call. I'd be surprised if he ___.
= did
? You often see Rosa. "If I ___ (see) her, I'll tell her."
= see
? What ___ (you / do) if your phone stopped working?
= would you do
:::

::: summary
- **If + past, would + verb** = an imagined present or future.
- No _would_ after _if_.
- **if I do… I'll…** = real possibility; **if I did… I'd…** = imagined.
- _would_ can be _could_ or _might_.
:::
