## -’s for people

**-’s** belongs to people and animals.

::: examples
- ==Tom’s== computer isn't working.
- How old are ==Chris’s children==?
- Don't step on ==the cat’s tail==.
- What’s ==your sister’s name==?
:::

You can drop the noun when it is clear: _That coat isn't mine — it's ==my sister's==._

For things and ideas, reach for **of** instead:

::: form
# 👤 -’s
the cat’s tail · my father’s car · Tom’s opinion

# 📦 of …
the temperature of the water · the name of the street
the owner of the shop · the cost of a ticket
the surface of the table · the middle of the road
:::

For organisations and places either tool is fine: the council's ruling or the ruling of the council; the town's river or the river of the town.

## One, or more than one

::: compare
# 1️⃣ one owner → -’s
- ==my sister’s room== (one sister)
- ==Mr Carter’s house==
- ==Jack and Karen’s children== (one couple)
# 👥 several owners → -s’
- ==my sisters’ room== (two or more)
- ==the Carters’ house== (Mr and Mrs Carter)
- ==Ben’s parents’ car== (both parents)
:::

Plurals that don't end in **-s** still take **-’s**: the men's locker room, a children's programme.

## With time

::: examples
- Do you still have ==yesterday’s newspaper==?
- ==Next week’s meeting== has been cancelled.
- ==Tuesday’s lesson== starts at nine.
:::

Periods take **-’s** too, with **-s’** for plurals:

::: form
# ⏱️ time
a week’s holiday | three weeks’ holiday
a ten-minute walk | two hours’ wait
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? How old are ___ children?
= James’s
? The garden of ___ neighbours is tiny.
= our
? I’ve got ___ holiday starting on Monday.
= a week’s
? It’s only a ___ walk from here.
= ten-minute
? What’s ___ of the street called?
= the name
:::

::: summary
- **-’s** for people and animals: _my sister's name, the cat's tail_.
- **of …** for things and ideas: _the name of the book, the cost of a ticket_.
- One owner: **-’s**. Several owners: **-s’** — _my sisters’ room, the Carters’ house_.
- Plurals without -s still take **-’s**: _the men's room_.
- With time: _next week's meeting_, _three weeks' holiday_.
:::
