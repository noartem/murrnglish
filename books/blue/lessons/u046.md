::: scene 🛠️ Moving in
Lars and Mei have just bought an old flat.
> Mei: Are you going to paint the walls yourself?
> Lars: No way. We=='re having== them ==painted== next week.
> Mei: And the kitchen?
> Lars: We=='re getting== a new one ==fitted==. Oh — and I need to ==get== the locks ==changed==.
:::

## Have something done
**have + object + past participle** = you arrange for someone else to do something for you. You don't do it yourself.

:::: row
::: compare
# 🙋 you do it
- Lars ==painted== the walls.
- Mei ==cut== her hair.
# 🧑‍🔧 someone does it for you
- Lars ==had== the walls ==painted==.
- Mei ==had== her hair ==cut==.
:::
::: rule 🧩 the order matters
**have** + **the thing** + **past participle**

I ==had== my bike ==repaired==.

Not: _I had repaired my bike_ — that means _I_ repaired it.
:::
::::

::: form
# ⏱️ any tense
present | We ==have== the windows ==cleaned== every month.
past | She ==had== her car ==serviced== last week.
continuous | I=='m having== my teeth ==checked== tomorrow.
perfect | Have you ==had== your eyes ==tested==?
to-infinitive | It costs a lot ==to have== a suit ==made==.
:::

::: trap
✗ I ~~cut my hair~~ at the hairdresser's yesterday.
✓ I had my hair cut at the hairdresser's yesterday.
✗ Where do you ~~have cut your hair~~?
✓ Where do you have your hair cut?
:::

## Get something done
**get** works the same way, mostly in everyday speech.

::: cards
# 🖼️ the poster
I love this poster. I'm going to ==get== it ==framed==.
# 👂 earrings
Zoe wants to ==get== her ears ==pierced==.
# 🔑 a spare key
I should ==get== a spare key ==made==.
:::

::: note 🗣️
_have_ and _get_ are both fine here: _I need to ==have / get== my phone ==fixed==._
:::

## Something bad happened to you
**have something done** can also describe something unpleasant that happened, usually done by someone else.

::: examples
- Ravi ==had== his phone ==stolen== on the train.
- We ==had== our flight ==cancelled== at the last minute.
- She ==had== her request ==turned down==.
- Did you hear? Our neighbours ==had== their car ==broken into==.
:::

::: quiz
? I didn't fix the tap myself. I had it ___.
= fixed
? My hair's too long. I need to ___ it ___. (get / cut)
= get … cut
? Order: (had / we / built / a garage / last year) → ___
= We had a garage built last year.
? Somebody stole her bag. → She ___ her bag ___.
= had … stolen
:::

::: summary
- **have + thing + past participle** = someone does it for you.
- Word order: _I had my car washed_, not _I had washed my car_.
- **get something done** = the same, more informal.
- Also for bad experiences: _He had his wallet stolen._
:::
