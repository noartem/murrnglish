> Warden: You look exhausted!

## Had been + -ing
The **past perfect continuous** is **had been + -ing**. It describes an activity that went on for some time **before** a moment in the past, and often explains how things were at that moment.

:::: row
::: form
# ➕ / ➖
I / you / she / they… | ==had been== / ==hadn't been== | walk==ing==
# ❓
==had== | I / you / she… | ==been== walk==ing==?
:::
::: timeline
span 15 55 | they'd been walking
point 55 | 8 p.m., at the hut
point now | now
:::
::::

::: cards
# 😓 tired
Kim was exhausted. She=='d been studying== all night.
# 🤧 sore throat
Eli lost his voice. He=='d been shouting== at the match.
# 🧹 dusty
Our hands were black. We=='d been clearing== out the attic.
:::

## How long, up to a past moment
Add **for** or **since**, and you measure the activity: how long it had lasted when something else came along.

::: examples
- We=='d been driving== for an hour when the tyre burst.
- When the shop closed, Mr Ito ==had been running== it for 30 years.
- I=='d been waiting== twenty minutes when I realised I was at the wrong café.
:::

::: note 🧱
State verbs keep the simple form: _I was sad to sell my guitar. I=='d had== it for ages._ (not _had been having_)
:::

## Had been doing, was doing, have been doing
::: compare
# 🎞️ was doing
- in the middle of it at that moment
- When I called, Mia ==was cooking==.
# ⏪ had been doing
- before that moment, maybe just stopped
- When I arrived, Mia ==had been cooking== — the kitchen was a mess.
# ⏩ have been doing
- up to **now**, not a past moment
- Mia ==has been cooking== all morning.
:::

::: trap
✗ We were tired. We ~~were travelling~~ for two days.
✓ We were tired. We'd been travelling for two days.
✗ I'm running a race soon. I ~~had been training~~ every day.
✓ I've been training every day.
:::

::: quiz
? Her eyes were red. She ___ (cry).
= had been crying / 'd been crying
? He ___ (work) there for ten years when the factory closed.
= had been working / 'd been working
? Look at the sky! It ___ (get) darker all afternoon.
= has been getting / 's been getting
? It was noisy next door. They ___ (have) a party.
= were having
:::

::: summary
- **had been + -ing** = an activity before a past moment.
- It explains how things were then: tired, wet, dirty.
- With **for / since**: how long it had gone on.
- _was doing_ = at that moment; _have been doing_ = up to now.
:::
