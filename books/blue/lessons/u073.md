## One of its kind on earth

For things that exist only once on earth, English expects **the**.

::: form
# 🌍 one of a kind
the sun · the moon · the earth · the sea · the sky · the ground

the world · the universe · the equator · the horizon · the North Pole

# 🚫 no article, ever
space · breakfast · lunch · dinner · television · internet
:::

One star among billions is _a star_; **the stars** means all of them.

::: trap
✗ There are millions of stars in ~~the space~~.
✓ There are millions of stars in ==space==.
✗ What did you have for ~~the breakfast~~?
✓ What did you have for ==breakfast==?
✗ Turn off ~~television~~. (meaning the set)
✓ Turn off ==the television==. (the set) / Turn off ==the TV==.
:::

## the with the biggest one

**the** goes with a **superlative**: _the best way_, _the longest river_, _the most expensive hotel in town._

::: examples
- That's ==the cheapest== way to get there.
- She knows ==the most famous== writer in the country.
- We waited for ==the last== bus of the night.
:::

## Numbers in names stay bare

::: form
# 🔢 no article
platform 3 · size 43 · room 25 · page 15 · section B · vitamin C

# ☀️ the
the sun · the moon · the sea · the second floor · the cheapest hotel
:::

::: summary
- **the** for anything unique on the planet: _the sun, the moon, the sea, the sky_.
- No article at all: _space, breakfast, television, the internet_.
- **the** with a superlative: _the cheapest hotel_.
- Numbers in names stay bare: _platform 3, size 43, room 25_.
:::
