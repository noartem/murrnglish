## in — a group, a whole, a piece of space

**in** here does not mean *inside a container*. It means **somewhere within** something bigger.

::: form
# 📊 in + a group or a whole
in a line
in a row
in a queue
in an office
in a department
in a picture
in the world
in the country
:::

- A colleague works **in** the marketing department.
- Who is the woman **in** that photograph?
- There isn't a cloud **in** the sky.
- She lives **in** a town, or somewhere **in** the countryside?

**in the country** means *not in a town*. **In the world** cannot be **at**.

## on — a surface of information, a map, a route

**on** is used where something is printed, drawn, marked — or lies along a line.

::: form
# 🗺️ on + a map, a list, a floor
on the left-hand side
on the second floor
on a map
on the shopping list
on page seven
on our website
:::

- Do you drive **on** the left **in** your country?
- You will find the details **on** our website.
- Vienna stands **on** the river Danube.

And **on the way** marks a place between two others: _We stopped **on the way** to the airport._

## at the top, at the end, at the bottom

**at** + a position word, with **of** the thing you are measuring.

::: form
# 📐 at the + a position
at the top of the page
at the bottom of the hill
at the end of the street
at the beginning of the film
:::

- Please sign your name **at the top** of the form.
- My aunt lives **at the far end** of that road.

## front and back

Here the preposition changes with what you are describing.

::: compare
# 🚗 in — inside a vehicle
- Nobody spoke to me ==in the back of the car==.
# 🏛️ at — a building, a room, a group
- Nobody ever sits ==at the back of the house==.
- We sat ==at the front==, so we saw nothing.
# ✉️ on — a flat surface
- She wrote a note ==on the back of an envelope==.
:::

## in the corner, on the corner

A **room** has an inside corner; a **street** has an outside one, and both prepositions work for a street.

::: cards
# 🛋️ inside a room
The television is ==in the corner of the room==.
# 🛣️ where two streets meet
A bakery stands ==at the corner== where the road bends.
or ==on the corner== (of the street).
:::

::: summary
- **in** for a group, a department, a picture or a whole: _in a row, in the marketing department, in the world._
- **on** for what is printed or mapped, and for a route: _on the list, on page seven, on the way home._
- **at** + top / bottom / end, with **of** what you measure: _at the end of the street._
- A vehicle takes **in** the back; a building takes **at** the back; paper takes **on** the back.
:::
