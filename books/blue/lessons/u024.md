::: scene ✈️ Mia's big day
Tomorrow Mia flies from London to Tokyo. The plane takes off at 10 and lands at 6 the next morning.
> Mia's mum: At noon tomorrow you=='ll be sitting== on a plane.
> Mia: And by this time on Thursday I=='ll have landed== in Tokyo!
:::

## Will be doing: in the middle of it
**will be + -ing** says that an action will be **in progress** at a certain time in the future: it has started but not finished.

:::: row
::: timeline
point now | now
span 40 80 | she'll be flying
point 60 | noon tomorrow
:::
::: form
# ➕ / ➖
I / you / she… | ==will be== / ==won't be== | work==ing==
# ❓
==will== | I / you / she… | ==be== work==ing==?
:::
::::

::: examples
- Don't call at seven — we=='ll be having== dinner.
- This time next week I=='ll be lying== on a beach.
- When you get to the station, Ana=='ll be waiting== for you.
:::

::: compare
# ▶️ will be doing
- At 9 I=='ll be watching== the game (it's already on).
# ⏯️ will do
- At 9 I=='ll watch== the game (it starts at 9).
:::

## Will be doing: things that will happen anyway
**will be -ing** can also describe something that's planned or part of the normal course of events. It sounds natural and polite in questions.

::: cards
# 🛒 anyway
I=='ll be going== to the shop later. Do you need anything?
# 🙋 polite question
==Will you be using== the printer this afternoon?
# 🛬 announcements
We=='ll be landing== in about twenty minutes.
:::

## Will have done: finished by then
**will have + past participle** says an action will be **finished** by a certain moment that is still ahead.

:::: row
::: timeline
span 20 55 | the film
point 55 | it ends
point 75 | 10 p.m.: it'll have finished
:::
::: rule ✅ by then, done
By ten the film ==will have finished==.

By next June, Raj ==will have worked== here for twenty years.

The kids ==won't have eaten== yet when we arrive.
:::
::::

::: trap
✗ By Friday I ~~will finish~~ the book.
✓ By Friday I'll have finished the book.
_by + a time_ points to **will have done**: finished before Friday, not on it.
:::

::: quiz
? From 2 to 4 tomorrow I'm in an exam. At 3, ___ (I / write).
= I'll be writing / I will be writing
? The party starts at 8. If we arrive at 10, most people ___ (already / leave).
= will already have left / 'll already have left
? ___ (you / go) past the post office later? Could you post this?
= Will you be going
? By the end of the year, I ___ (save) €2,000.
= 'll have saved / will have saved
:::

::: summary
- **will be + -ing** = in progress at a future moment.
- It also says something will happen anyway: _Will you be using…?_
- **will have + past participle** = complete before a future moment.
- _by…_ (by then, by Friday) often goes with **will have done**.
:::
