Zoe comes home. Her brother Finn opens the door, covered in blue paint.
> Zoe: What ==have you been doing==?
> Finn: I=='ve been painting== my room. All afternoon!
> Zoe: You've got paint in your hair. And on the cat.

## Have been + -ing
The **present perfect continuous** is **have / has been + -ing**. It's for an activity that went on for a while, up to now or until just now.

:::: row
::: form
# ➕ / ➖
I / you / we / they | ==have been== / ==haven't been== | work==ing==
he / she / it | ==has been== / ==hasn't been== | work==ing==
# ❓
==have== | I / you / we / they | ==been== work==ing==?
==has== | he / she / it | ==been== work==ing==?
:::
::: timeline
span past now | It has been snowing
point now | now
:::
::::

## Just stopped, with signs left
Often the activity has just finished, and you can still see the evidence.

::: cards
# 💦 wet ground
It=='s been raining==. Take your boots.
# 😮‍💨 out of breath
Why are you so red? — I=='ve been cycling==.
# 😢 red eyes
You look upset. ==Have you been crying==?
:::

::: examples
- Your hands are black! ==What have you been doing==? — Fixing my bike.
- Sorry, ==have you been waiting== long? — No, only five minutes.
- I=='ve been thinking== about your offer, and the answer is yes.
:::

## How long? For and since
It also tells you how long something has been going on. It may still be going on now.

::: rule ⏰
**How long have you been + -ing?**

for + a length of time: ==for== two hours, ==for== a week.

since + a starting point: ==since== 9 o'clock, ==since== May.
:::

::: examples
- Jun ==has been learning== the violin ==for== three years.
- We=='ve been queuing== ==since== six this morning!
- How long ==have you been working== at the café? — Since April.
- My grandparents ==have been going== to the same beach every summer for decades.
:::

## Am doing or have been doing?
Compare the present continuous with the present perfect continuous.

::: compare
# 🔵 I'm doing
- Happening around now, no time span.
- Lena ==is studying== in her room.
- Don't text while you're ==driving==.
# 🟣 I've been doing
- A span of time up to now.
- Lena ==has been studying== since lunch.
- He's tired. He=='s been driving== all day.
:::

::: trap
✗ I ~~am working~~ here since 2021.
✓ I've been working here since 2021.
✗ How long ~~are you waiting~~?
✓ How long have you been waiting?
:::

::: quiz
? Your shoes are muddy. ___ (you / walk) in the park?
= Have you been walking
? It ___ (snow) since this morning.
= 's been snowing / has been snowing
? Shh! The baby ___ (sleep). (now)
= is sleeping
? How long ___ (they / play) that game?
= have they been playing
:::

::: summary
- **have / has been + -ing** = an activity over a period up to now.
- Use it to explain signs you see now (wet, tired, dirty).
- **How long…?** with **for** a period or **since** a starting point.
- _I'm doing_ = now; _I've been doing_ = for a time until now.
:::
