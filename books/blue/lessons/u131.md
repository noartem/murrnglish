goals: use of with fear, pride, awareness and certainty · use at, to, in and on with more adjectives · choose the preposition an adjective wants, not the one that sounds right

::: scene 🎾 The club prospectus
Two friends read the small print of the membership form.
> Ada: It says you're not guaranteed a court unless you're a member.
> Rune: I'm only **interested in** playing at weekends, so I won't sign up.
> Ada: Are you sure **about** that? The fees go up in January.
> Rune: I'm not sure **of** the fees. That's why I'm asking.
> Ada: The courts are always **crowded with** beginners in September.
> Rune: Fine. I'm **keen on** football, not on queueing.
:::

## of: the big family

A long list of adjectives takes **of**, and they all mean something like *towards something*.

::: form
# 😨 fear and dislike
afraid of spiders
scared of the dark
jealous of her sister
envious of his success
# 💛 feeling and thinking
proud of my daughter
fond of chocolate
ashamed of what I said
aware of the risks
capable of doing better
full of energy
short of time
typical of him
:::

==== **suspicious of** and **critical of** join them. **certain of** and **sure of** also take **about**: _I'm not sure **about** that._

But watch the flip: **dependent on** somebody, **independent of** anything.

## One adjective, two prepositions, two meanings

::: cards
# 💍 married to somebody
Louise is married ==to an American==.
# 👶 married with children
Louise is married ==with three children==.
:::

That's not an exception to remember so much as two different questions: **to whom**, and **to what state**.

## at, to, in, on, for

::: compare
# 🎯 good at
- She isn't good ==at speaking to strangers==.
# 💍 married / similar to
- Louise is married ==to an American==.
- Your handwriting is similar ==to mine==.
# 🖼️ different from
- His answer was different ==from what I expected==.
# 🎨 interested in
- Are you interested ==in art==?
# 🔥 keen on
- Chris wasn't keen ==on going out==.
:::

**crowded with** for people, and **full of** when you mean the whole place: _The streets were **crowded with** tourists, and the square was **full of** them._

## Trap

::: trap
✗ She is married ==with== an American.
✓ She is married ==to== an American.
✗ I'm good ==in== repairing things.
✓ I'm good ==at== repairing things.
✗ Are you interested ==on== art?
✓ Are you interested ==in== art?
✗ He is independent ==on== anybody.
✓ He is independent ==of== anybody.
✗ I'm a bit short ==in== money.
✓ I'm a bit short ==of== money.
✗ The square was crowded ==of== people.
✓ The square was crowded ==with== people.
✗ She was proud ==for== her son.
✓ She was proud ==of== her son.
✗ I'm not sure ==for== that.
✓ I'm not sure ==of / about== that.
:::

::: summary
- A long family takes **of**: _afraid of, proud of, aware of, capable of, full of, short of, typical of._
- **sure of** and **sure about** both work; **certain of** and **certain about** likewise.
- **good at**, **interested in**, **keen on**, **married to**, **different from**, **dependent on**.
- Two opposites to keep apart: **dependent on** somebody, **independent of** anything.
:::
