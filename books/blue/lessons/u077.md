## Names that stand alone

Most place names — countries, continents, islands, cities, mountains — take **no the**.

::: form
# 📍 no the
continents | Africa · South America · Asia
countries | France · Japan · Texas · Sweden
islands | Sicily · Tasmania
cities | Cairo · Bangkok · Toronto
mountains | Everest · Kilimanjaro · Fuji
:::

Names built on **Republic**, **Kingdom** or **States** usually take it — think of the Czech Republic, the United Kingdom, the United States of America, the Dominican Republic.

## Titles and special words

::: form
# 👤 no the
Doctor Johnson · Professor Brown · President Kennedy · Inspector Roberts
Uncle Robert · Saint Catherine · Queen Catherine
Mount Everest · Mount Etna · Lake Superior · Lake Victoria

# 🔵 with the
the doctor (the one we called) · the president (of the country)

# 👨‍👩‍👧 a family or a couple
the Taylors · the Johnsons
:::

## Water and deserts

::: form
# 🌊 the
the Atlantic · the Red Sea · the Indian Ocean · the Mediterranean
the Amazon · the Nile · the Suez Canal · the Channel

# 🏜️ the
the Sahara · the Gobi Desert
:::

## Compass points

::: compare
# 🧭 with the
- ==the north== of Brazil
- ==the southeast== of Spain
# 🌏 without the, in a region name
- northern Brazil
- southeastern Spain
- North America · South Africa · southeast Asia
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? We drove right across ___ from Lisbon to Istanbul.
= Europe
? ___ is the capital of Australia.
= Canberra
? The Sahara is in the north of ___, isn't it?
= Africa
? She climbed ___ with a team last spring.
= Mount Etna
? Name the river that crosses three capital cities on its way to the Black Sea.
= the Danube
:::

::: summary
- Most place names take **no the**: _France, Sicily, Cairo, Everest_.
- Republic / Kingdom / States take it: _the Czech Republic, the UK, the USA_.
- Titles, Mount and Lake take no the: _Doctor Johnson, Mount Everest, Lake Victoria_.
- Oceans, seas, rivers and deserts take **the**: _the Atlantic, the Nile, the Sahara_.
- _the north_ of a country, but _North America_ and _northern Brazil_.
:::
