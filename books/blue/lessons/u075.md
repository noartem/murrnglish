::: scene 🍽️ Around the table
The family has finished lunch. Someone puts the fruit bowl on the table.
> Mum: Who wants ==apples==? There are ==some pears== left too.
> Grandad: Pass me ==the fruit bowl==, please. And could you cut ==the apples==?
> Mum: I love ==bananas==, but I never buy ==oranges== — they're too expensive.
:::

## In general: no article

Talking about things **as a whole class**, English drops the article.

::: compare
# 🌍 in general
- I'm afraid of ==dogs==.
- ==Doctors== are usually paid more than ==teachers==.
- Do you know anybody who collects ==stamps==?
- ==Money== makes the world go round.
- My favourite sport is ==football==.
# 🎯 a specific group
- We took ==the children== to the zoo.
- Every vehicle in this yard belongs to us.
- Can you pass ==the sugar==, please?
- ==The visitors== I met were from Japan.
:::

**most** does the same job: _most shops_, _most big cities_ — no _the most shops._

## When the line is thin

Sometimes "in general" and "this particular one" blur. Ask yourself: is it **all of them**, or **the ones we can point at**?

::: cards
# ☕ coffee
Do you like ==coffee==? (coffee in general)

==The coffee== we had after dinner was awful. (that cup)
# 👥 people
I get on well with ==people==. (people in general)

I get on well with ==the people== I work with. (my colleagues)
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? ___ are good for you.
= Apples
? ___ on that tree are very big.
= The apples
? Look at ___ in this photo. They're all wearing hats.
= the people
? I'm not good at remembering ___.
= names
? We enjoyed our holiday. ___ was good.
= The weather
:::

::: summary
- In general, no article: _Apples are healthy, Doctors are paid well_.
- **the** when both speakers know which ones: _The apples on that tree, The weather today_.
- **most** behaves the same way: _most shops_, not _the most shops_.
- When in doubt, ask: all of them, or the ones we can point at?
:::
