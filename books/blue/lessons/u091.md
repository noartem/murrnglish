## A part, or the whole set

**each** = one by one, taken separately. **every** = the group, seen as a whole. The meaning is close; the emphasis differs.

::: compare
# 🔹 each — one at a time
- ==Each stamp== in the album is different.
- The coach spoke to ==each player== before the match.
- ==Each of the stamps== was different.
# 🔸 every — the whole group
- ==Every stamp== in the album is different.
- Kate has read ==every novel== on the course.
- I want to visit ==every island== in that group.
:::

**each** works on its own and takes **of**; **every** needs a noun, or the two-word **every one**.

::: form
# 🔹 each
Each is different. · Each of them was tired. · Each of these cards is new.

# 🔸 every
She has ==every book== on her shelf.
I know ==every one== of them. (every one of them)
:::

## Two things each can't do, two things every can't do

::: trap
✗ In football, ==every team== has eleven players.
✓ In football, ==each team== has eleven players.
✗ 'How often do you take the bus?' '==Each day==.'
✓ 'How often do you take the bus?' '==Every day==.'
:::

**each** suits a **small** number; **every** suits a **large** one.

## each in the middle, and each for a price

::: examples
- The children were ==each== given a present. (= each child)
- These melons are £2 ==each==.
- Sonia and I ==each== paid £15. / We paid £15 ==each==.
:::

## everyone or every one?

**everyone** (one word) is only for **people**. **every one** (two words) works for things and people.

::: compare
# 👥 everyone
==Everyone== enjoyed the party. (= everybody)
# 📦 every one
Sarah goes to ==every one== of her sister's weddings.
She answered ==every one== of our questions.
:::

## Quiz

::: quiz
? There were four forks on the table. ___ fork was clean.
= Each
? ___ seat in the row was sold.
= Every
? ___ of these cards is a joker.
= Each
? She goes to ___ of the conferences.
= every one
? The taxi comes ___ fifteen minutes.
= every
:::

::: summary
- **each** = one by one; **every** = the group as a whole.
- **each** stands alone and takes of: _each of them_. **every** needs a noun: _every book_, or _every one_.
- **each** for two things and for small numbers; **every** for how often.
- **everyone** for people only; **every one** for things too.
:::
