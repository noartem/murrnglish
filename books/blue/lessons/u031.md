::: scene 🏥 Nurse Amara's week
Amara is a nurse. Her friend Pip asks about her job.
> Pip: Do you ==have to== work nights?
> Amara: One week a month. Last week I ==had to== do five in a row.
> Pip: Ouch. And weekends?
> Amara: I ==don't have to== work weekends, thank goodness. But I really ==must== get more sleep!
:::

## Have to: it's necessary
**have to + verb** = it is necessary; there's no choice. It works in every tense, with _do / does / did_ for questions and negatives.

:::: row
::: form
# ➕
I / we / they | ==have to== | go
he / she / it | ==has to== | go
past | ==had to== | go
future | ==will have to== | go
# ❓
==do / does / did== | you / she… | ==have to== go?
:::
::: rule 🚌 no choice
I ==have to== take two buses to school.

We ==had to== queue for an hour.

You=='ll have to== book a table — it's busy.

==Did== you ==have to== pay to get in?
:::
::::

::: trap
✗ ~~Have you to~~ wear a uniform?
✓ Do you have to wear a uniform?
✗ I ~~must~~ work late yesterday.
✓ I had to work late yesterday.
**must** has no past form of its own.
:::

## Must or have to?
Often both are possible. But there's a difference in who decides.

::: compare
# 💭 must — I feel it's necessary
- I ==must== call Grandma. I haven't spoken to her for ages.
- You ==must== try this cake — it's amazing!
- the speaker's own opinion
# 📜 have to — the situation says so
- I ==have to== be at work at seven.
- In this country you ==have to== be 18 to vote.
- a fact, a rule, an arrangement
:::

::: note 🧾
**must** only exists in the present and future: _I must go now / tomorrow._ For the past and other forms, use **have to**: _had to, have had to, will have to_.
:::

## Mustn't or don't have to?
These look alike but mean something very different.

::: cards
# 🚫 mustn't = don't do it!
You ==mustn't== tell anyone. It's a secret.

You ==mustn't== touch the paintings.
# 🆓 don't have to = it's not necessary
You ==don't have to== come, but you're welcome.

Kim ==doesn't have to== wear a tie, but she likes to.
:::

::: words
mustn't = it's important NOT to · don't have to = you can if you want · didn't have to = it wasn't necessary
:::

::: quiz
? The flight was cancelled, so we ___ (stay) in a hotel.
= had to stay
? Tomorrow's a holiday. I ___ (not / get up) early.
= don't have to get up
? Shh! You ___ wake the baby.
= mustn't
? How many hours a day ___ Ben ___ (work)?
= does … have to work
:::

::: summary
- **have to** = necessary; any tense: _had to, will have to, do you have to…?_
- **must** = the speaker feels it's necessary; only present / future.
- **mustn't** = don't do it; **don't have to** = not necessary.
:::
