## Active and passive
In an **active** sentence, the subject does the action. In a **passive** sentence, the subject has something done to it. We use the passive when the action is more important than who did it, or when we don't know who did it.

::: compare
# 🙋 active
- A storm ==damaged== the roof.
- Somebody ==cleans== the windows every week.
- They ==make== these phones in Korea.
# 📦 passive
- The roof ==was damaged== (by a storm).
- The windows ==are cleaned== every week.
- These phones ==are made== in Korea.
:::

## Is done, was done
The passive is **be + past participle**: _cleaned, made, built, seen…_ **be** shows the tense.

:::: row
::: form
# ⏺️ present simple
I | ==am== | invited
he / she / it | ==is== | invited
we / you / they | ==are== | invited
# ⏪ past simple
I / he / she / it | ==was== | invited
we / you / they | ==were== | invited
:::
::: form
# ❓ questions
==Is== | the bread | ==baked== here?
==Where was== | the film | ==made==?
==How are== | rainbows | ==formed==?
:::
::::

::: examples
- Rice ==is grown== in many hot countries.
- My bike ==was stolen== last night.
- ==When was== the first bicycle ==built==?
- We ==weren't told== about the change.
:::

::: trap
✗ The bridge ~~built~~ in 1900.
✓ The bridge was built in 1900.
✗ My grandfather ~~was died~~ in 2010.
✓ My grandfather died in 2010.
Don't forget **be**. And verbs without an object (_die, happen, arrive, fall_) have no passive.
:::

## By … : who did it
Add **by** + the doer only when it's important or interesting.

::: cards
# 🎨 interesting
_The Starry Night_ ==was painted by== Van Gogh.
# 🤷 obvious, unknown
The thief ==was arrested== (by the police). (no need to say)
# 🔄 they / people / somebody
They cancelled the match. → The match ==was cancelled==.
:::

::: quiz
? English ___ (speak) in many countries.
= is spoken
? These houses ___ (build) about 100 years ago.
= were built
? The accident ___ (happen) at midnight.
= happened
? Somebody found my wallet. → My wallet ___.
= was found
:::

::: summary
- Passive = **be + past participle**: _is made, are made, was made, were made_.
- Use it when the action matters more than who does it.
- **by + doer** only when it's important.
- No passive for _die, happen, arrive, fall_…
:::
