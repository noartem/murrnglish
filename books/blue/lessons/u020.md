::: scene 🛠️ The garden shed
Nadia is standing in front of an old wooden shed with a hammer.
> Neighbour: What ==are== you ==going to do== with that?
> Nadia: I=='m going to turn== it into a little studio.
> Neighbour: Careful — that roof ==is going to fall== on you!
:::

## I'm going to: already decided
**going to + verb** says what someone has decided or intends to do. The decision was made before you speak.

:::: row
::: form
# ➕ / ➖
I'm / you're / she's… | ==going to== / ==not going to== | paint
# ❓
==am / are / is== | I / you / she… | ==going to== paint?
:::
::: timeline
point 25 | Nadia decides
point now | she tells her neighbour
arrow now future | the studio
:::
::::

::: examples
- I=='m going to learn== to swim this summer.
- We=='re not going to buy== a car. We'll cycle.
- What ==are== you ==going to call== the puppy?
- Leo has saved up. He=='s going to travel== around Japan.
:::

::: note 🧭
_I'm doing_ (unit 19) is an arrangement with other people or a booking. _I'm going to do_ is just your decision. Often both work: _I'm seeing / I'm going to see Ana tomorrow._
:::

## It's going to: you can see it coming
Use **going to** to predict something when the signs are already there.

::: cards
# 🌫️ fog
I can't see the road. We=='re going to miss== the turning.
# 🍳 smoke
The pan is smoking! It=='s going to burn==.
# 🏃 the clock
It's 8.55 and the bus is gone. She=='s going to be== late.
# 🔋 2%
My phone=='s going to die== any minute.
:::

::: trap
✗ Look at that sky! It ~~rains~~.
✓ Look at that sky! It's going to rain.
Something you can see starting now is a **going to** future, not the present simple.
:::

## Was going to: the plan that changed
**was / were going to** is a past intention or prediction — usually it didn't happen.

::: compare
# 💭 the plan
- I ==was going to call== you,
- We ==were going to eat== outside,
- I thought the film ==was going to be== boring,
# 🔄 what happened
- but my phone died.
- but it started to rain.
- but I loved it.
:::

::: quiz
? Your friend has bought a guitar. What ___ (you / play) on it?
= are you going to play
? Watch out! That glass ___ (fall).
= is going to fall / 's going to fall
? We ___ (go) to the beach, but it was too windy.
= were going to go
? This soup is terrible. I ___ (not / finish) it.
= 'm not going to finish / am not going to finish
:::

::: summary
- **am/is/are going to + verb** = something already decided.
- **It's going to…** = a prediction from what you see now.
- **was/were going to** = a past plan, often not carried out.
:::
